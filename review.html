Approve / reject

Read-only here. Approve and reject are server-side operations: they need the CRON_SECRET bearer token, which never goes in a browser page or a URL. To decide, use the secret from the vault operator's shell:

curl -X POST "$SITE/api/review-decide" \
-H "Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
-d '{"id":"<candidate id>","decision":"approve","note":"<optional>"}'

Approvals record approved_via:'scout' on the decision; the human's wave adds the real activity in original words, nothing auto-publishes.
