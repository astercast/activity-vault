🎉 The Party Pack, the flagship

A whole party,
packed from real cards.

Pick an occasion, an age band, and a group size, the Party Pack composes a five-part party arc (welcome, big group opener, making & games, movement break, calm close) from real Activity Vault activities that honestly match all three, then opens those activities' own materials lists and packs the party bag: one deduplicated list, every line traced to the activity that asked for it. And it keeps the Vault's oldest promise: if an occasion is honestly thin at your age band, Valentine's for three-year-olds holds four real activities, not five, the Pack says so in numbers, fills only what honestly fits, and shows the nearest real options it verified against the catalog. It never pads a plan with invented activities.
