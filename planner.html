    🗓️ Day Planner, the flagship

    One day, composed.

    Five slots make a program day, arrival, morning meeting, centers, outdoor, closing. Pick your room's age and the Day Planner fills each slot from the real catalog: every suggestion is a real activity whose age band covers your kids and whose minutes fit the slot. Swap any slot, or start from the Just Three starter day, a whole day pre-composed from records written for three-year-olds alone. Then print the day sheet and pin it where the day can see it.

    Filters use each activity's real age band, a record only appears if it honestly covers this age.
