📅 Smart Weekly Planner 3.0

Your weeks, auto-built and balanced.

Pick a program, an age group, and up to four weeks, the Vault deals no-repeat weeks from the Vault's real activities, balances the energy across every day, and warns you when a day is lopsided. Every day gets a ⚡ brain-break idea, every outdoor slot a 🌦️ weather backup, every plan a 🛒 supply list and a 🆘 substitute kit. Then drag it into shape.

🗓️ New, the Day Planner: composing a single day instead? Open the Day Planner, five slots (arrival → closing) filled by age from the real catalog, a pre-composed Just Three starter day, and a printable day sheet.
