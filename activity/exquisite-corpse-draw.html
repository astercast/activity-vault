Exquisite Corpse Drawing

Three artists, one folded paper, one gloriously weird creature.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Fold each paper into thirds. Each kid starts with one folded paper.

Materials

  • paper
  • markers or crayons

Steps

  1. On the top third, draw a head — any head: human, monster, robot.
  2. Fold the head section back so only two little neck lines show. Pass the paper left.
  3. On the middle third, draw a torso connecting to the neck lines. Fold it back, pass left.
  4. On the bottom third, draw legs and feet. Unfold the whole thing.
  5. Reveal the creatures! The mismatched combos are always hilarious. Hang the best.

Variations

Easier: Do just two sections (head + body) for younger kids.

Harder: Add a rule: each section must be a different art style (realistic, cartoon, robot).
