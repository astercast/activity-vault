Flag Tag

Classic capture-the-flag with freeze-and-rescue rules — strategy, sprinting, and glorious last-second steals.

Ages
7–12
Group
8–30 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Mark two territories with cones; place flags visibly at each back line.

Materials

  • two bandanas or flags
  • cones to mark territories

Steps

  1. Split into two teams, each with a 'territory' half of the field and a flag (bandana) tucked visibly at the back — not hidden, just guarded.
  2. Players cross into enemy territory to steal the flag. If tagged there, they freeze and must be freed by a teammate's touch.
  3. Carry the flag back to your side to score. Dropped flags stay where they fall.
  4. Play 10-minute rounds, then switch: let teams redesign their defense between rounds.
  5. No guarding within arm's reach of your own flag — that rule keeps the game moving.

Variations

Easier: Play with a bigger field and longer rounds.

Harder: Add a neutral 'spy' who can free frozen players for either team.

⚠️ Safety

No tackling, tags only. Watch for collisions.
