20 Questions

Twenty yes-or-no questions to crack the mystery.

Ages
7–12
Group
3–25 kids
Time
10–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

One player thinks of something (person, place, or thing). Everyone else asks yes-or-no questions.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. The thinker announces the category: animal, vegetable, or mineral (person/place/thing works too).
  2. Players take turns asking yes-or-no questions: 'Is it alive? Can you eat it?'
  3. Count the questions out loud — 20 is the limit.
  4. If the group guesses it, the winner becomes the next thinker.
  5. If 20 questions run out, the thinker reveals it and picks again.

Variations

Easier: Give 25 questions and allow one free hint.

Harder: Ban the most obvious opening questions ('Is it alive?') after round one.
