Group Mural Project

Cover a wall in butcher paper and let every group claim a section — the finished mural is your group's portrait.

Ages
5–12
Group
6–30 kids
Time
45–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Tape butcher paper to the wall; divide into sections.

Materials

  • butcher paper
  • markers
  • paint
  • magazines for collage
  • glue sticks
  • tape

Steps

  1. Tape a long sheet of butcher paper to the wall or floor. Write the theme across the top: 'Our Community,' 'What We Love,' 'Welcome.'
  2. Divide the paper into sections — one per small group — or let it be a free-for-all collage.
  3. Each group gets 20 minutes to fill their section: drawings, words, handprints, collage pieces.
  4. Walk the mural together. Each group explains their section and one choice they made.
  5. Hang it somewhere permanent. Date it. Start a new one each season and watch the wall tell your group's story.

Variations

Easier: Give each group a pre-drawn section to color.

Harder: Require the mural to tell a story left to right.

⚠️ Safety

Ventilate if using paint; smocks on.
