🌧️ Rainy Day

Indoor Scavenger Hunt

The rainy-day classic: find it, don't move it.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Write a checklist of things to find without moving anything: something older than you, something with wheels, something blue, a book with an animal on the cover, something that ticks, the softest thing in the room.

Materials

  • printed clue lists
  • pencils

Steps

  1. Hand out checklists. Set the boundaries (which rooms) and the time limit.
  2. Kids hunt in pairs, writing WHERE each item is — nothing gets moved.
  3. Regroup and verify the trickiest finds together.
  4. Bonus points for the most creative interpretation of a clue.
  5. Reset the room together — leave it better than you found it.

Variations

Easier: Use picture clues for younger hunters.

Harder: Make it a riddle hunt — each clue is a riddle leading to the next location.

⚠️ Safety

Walking feet inside — no running. Stay in the allowed rooms.
