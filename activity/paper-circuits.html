Paper Circuit Greeting Cards

Build a real working circuit into a greeting card — copper tape, a coin battery, and an LED that actually lights up.

Ages
8–12
Group
1–20 kids
Time
45–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pre-cut cardstock cards; set out copper tape, batteries, and LEDs.

Materials

  • cardstock
  • copper tape
  • 3V coin batteries
  • LEDs
  • tape
  • markers
  • scissors

Steps

  1. Draw a simple card design on cardstock — a house with a glowing window, a rocket with a lit flame.
  2. On the inside, map the circuit: draw the path from battery to LED to battery, marking + and −.
  3. Lay copper tape along your drawn path, folding it around corners (never overlapping the two lines).
  4. Tape the LED's legs to the copper tape — long leg to the + line. Tape the coin battery at the other end with the + side matching.
  5. Press to complete the circuit. If it doesn't light, check: battery direction? Tape touching both legs? Lines accidentally crossing?
  6. Decorate the front so the LED shines through a cut-out window.

Variations

Easier: Use pre-drawn circuit templates to follow.

Harder: Add a second LED in parallel and a paper switch.

⚠️ Safety

Coin batteries are a swallowing hazard — ages 8+ and supervised.
