Build-a-Scarecrow

Stuff old clothes with straw and raise a guardian for the school garden.

Ages
6–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
40–60 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Gather stuffing material and clothes. Pick the scarecrow's post — garden bed, playground corner, or field edge — and pound the stake in first.

Materials

  • old long-sleeve shirt and pants
  • a pillowcase
  • straw, hay, or shredded paper
  • twine or rope
  • a wooden stake or broom handle
  • markers
  • an old hat

Steps

  1. Stuff the shirt with straw and tie the cuffs and bottom shut with twine.
  2. Stuff the pants the same way and tie the ankles.
  3. Draw a face on the pillowcase, stuff it lightly, and tie it closed for the head.
  4. Dress the scarecrow: shirt over the stake, pants tied on, head pinned at the top, hat on.
  5. Give your scarecrow a name and a job description. Vote on the name if there are rival ideas.

Variations

Easier: Build a mini tabletop scarecrow from a paper bag and shredded paper instead.

Harder: Write a 'day in the life' story from the scarecrow's point of view.

⚠️ Safety

Straw can be scratchy — no throwing it, and watch for kids with hay allergies.
