Storytelling Circle

One object, one circle, true stories — the fastest way to know each other.

Ages
6–12
Group
5–25 kids
Time
15–40 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Chairs in a circle. One object to pass as the talking piece — a ball, a stuffed animal, anything.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Whoever holds the talking piece tells a short true story from their life — funny, scary, or proud.
  2. Pass the piece around the circle. Everyone gets a turn; passing is allowed, no pressure.
  3. After a full round, open it up: ask follow-up questions about the best stories.
  4. Round two (optional): tell a story about someone else in the circle — something you admire about them.
  5. End by naming one thing you learned about someone today.

Variations

Easier: Give story prompts: 'a time you laughed really hard,' 'something you are good at.'

Harder: Build one group story — each person adds one sentence, keeping it coherent.
