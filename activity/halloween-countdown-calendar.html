Halloween Countdown Calendar

Thirteen doors, thirteen mini-activities: the class counts down to the 31st.

Ages
5–14
Group
4–35 kids
Time
20–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Draw a 13-door grid on the poster board (or tape up 13 folded paper 'doors'). Number them 13 down to 1.

Materials

  • large paper or poster board
  • markers
  • sticky notes
  • tape

Steps

  1. Brainstorm as a class: 13 tiny Halloween activities that fit in 10 minutes - a riddle, a drawing, a chant, a stretch.
  2. Write one activity per sticky note and hide it behind each door.
  3. Every day from Oct 19 to Oct 31, open the next door together.
  4. Do the mini-activity on the spot - the calendar IS the plan.
  5. Door 1 (Oct 31): the class votes on the grand finale activity.

Variations

Easier: Teacher pre-fills the doors; the class just opens and does.

Harder: Each table designs its own 13-day calendar for a different theme.

⚠️ Safety

Sticky notes only - no cutting doors for little kids; lift the flap.

🤝 Inclusion notes

Every door's activity has a seated version; the class picks activities everyone can do.
