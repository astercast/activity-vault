Warm-Up Countdown

Call-and-response counting with jumping jacks — math that warms the room.

Ages
5–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
10–15 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Push desks back so everyone has arm room. Teacher leads the first round.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Teacher calls a number and a move: 'Ten jumping jacks — go!' The group counts aloud together while doing it.
  2. Rotate the caller: each kid picks the next number and move (high knees, arm circles, toe touches).
  3. Add math: 'Seven minus two jumping jacks — how many?' Solve, then do them.
  4. Final round: count down from 20 to 1 together, getting quieter each number, ending seated.

Variations

Easier: Just counting with claps, no big moves; teacher picks every number.

Harder: Add multiplication: 'Three sets of four arm circles — how many total?'

⚠️ Safety

Moves stay low-impact near furniture; anyone can march in place instead.
