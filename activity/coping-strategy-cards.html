🧘 Quiet & Focus

Coping Strategy Cards

Kids build a personal deck of calm-down cards to keep in their desk.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Brainstorm calm-down strategies on the board first (deep breaths, count to ten, squeeze a stress ball, draw, ask for a break). Give each kid 4–5 blank cards.

Materials

  • index cards (4–5 per kid)
  • markers or crayons
  • a hole punch
  • binder rings or yarn to bind the deck

Steps

  1. Each kid picks 4–5 strategies from the board and makes one card per strategy: draw a picture on one side, write the steps on the other.
  2. Punch a hole in the corner of each card and bind them into a little flip deck.
  3. Practice: the teacher names a tricky feeling, and kids flip to the card they'd use.
  4. Keep the decks in desks or a calm corner. Kids can add new cards anytime.
  5. Revisit in a month: which cards actually got used? Retire the duds, add new ideas.

Variations

Easier: Make just 2 cards with teacher-chosen strategies.

Harder: Kids design a 'help a friend' card: what to say when a classmate is upset.
