No-Carve Pumpkin Painting

All the fall fun of pumpkins with zero knives: paint, stickers, and googly eyes turn mini pumpkins into characters.

Ages
4–12
Group
1–30 kids
Time
40–40 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cover tables with newspaper; set out one pumpkin per child plus shared paint stations.

Materials

  • mini pumpkins
  • acrylic paint or paint pens
  • paintbrushes
  • googly eyes
  • stickers
  • glue
  • smocks or old shirts

Steps

  1. Cover tables and hand out smocks — acrylic paint does not negotiate with clothing.
  2. Let each child pick a mini pumpkin and plan their design: a face, an animal, an abstract pattern.
  3. Paint a base coat first and let it dry for 5 minutes while kids sketch ideas on scrap paper.
  4. Add details with paint pens, then glue on googly eyes, stickers, or other decorations.
  5. Display the finished patch together for a group photo before anyone takes theirs home.

Variations

Easier: Use stickers and markers only — no paint.

Harder: Paint a scene or character with a backstory to present.

⚠️ Safety

Smocks on — acrylic paint stains. Wash hands before touching faces.
