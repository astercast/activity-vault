Ghost Balloon Stomp

Ghost faces on ankles: stomp others' ghosts while guarding your own.

Before you begin

Ages
5–12
Group
6–30 kids
Time
10–20 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Draw ghost faces on inflated balloons. Tie one to each kid's ankle with a foot of string - two per kid for a second life.

Materials

  • balloons (2 per kid)
  • string
  • markers

Steps

  1. Spread out - stomping needs space.
  2. On GO: try to pop others' ankle ghosts by stomping them.
  3. Guard your own: hop, dodge, hide behind friends.
  4. Popped both? Grab a 'ghost buddy' role - you call out incoming stompers.
  5. Last ghost floating wins the round. New balloons, new round.

Variations

Easier: One balloon each, and stomping is walking-speed only.

Harder: Team version: protect your team's ghosts, hunt the other team's.

Safety

Pop means pieces: keep balloons away from mouths, and pick up EVERY popped piece right away - balloon pieces are a choking hazard.
