Marco Polo

Eyes closed in the pool: one voice calls 'Marco' and every splash answers 'Polo'.

Before you begin

Ages
7–14
Group
4–20 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Play in a pool where everyone can swim the depth, under a lifeguard's or supervising adult's eye. One player is Marco. Everyone else spreads through the water. Agree the boundaries first: the game stays inside the marked area, and climbing out is out of bounds unless the group votes to allow deck play.

Materials

  • a swimming pool, with a lifeguard or supervising adult on watch

Steps

  1. Marco closes their eyes, counts to ten out loud, and calls 'Marco!'
  2. Every other player answers 'Polo!' at once, wherever they are. No silent swimmers; the answer is the game.
  3. Marco swims toward the voices, calling as often as they like, hunting by ear for a one-hand tag.
  4. Players dodge, duck, and glide quietly between calls, but the moment 'Marco' rings out, their 'Polo' gives them away.
  5. A tagged player becomes the new Marco, and the old Marco joins the chorus.

Variations

Easier: Play in the shallow end where everyone can stand, and let Marco take one open-eyed look between every third call.

Harder: Fish out of water: if Marco calls it and a player is sneaking along the deck, that player is caught on the spot. Use it only where the deck is dry and safe to walk.

Safety

Supervision is the first rule: a lifeguard or adult watches the whole game. No diving, no holding under, no play in water over a swimmer's depth. A player who needs a break climbs out and says so, and the game pauses for anyone in trouble, instantly.
