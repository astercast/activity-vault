Animal Walk Relay

Waddle like a penguin, stomp like a polar bear — a relay across the gym floor.

Ages
4–12
Group
6–40 kids
Time
10–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Split the group into equal teams behind the start line. Name three winter animal walks and demo each one.

Materials

  • cones or tape to mark the relay line

Steps

  1. Leg 1: penguin waddle (heels together, arms at sides, wobble to the cone and back).
  2. Leg 2: polar-bear prowl (bear crawl — hands and feet, knees off the floor).
  3. Leg 3: arctic-hare hop (two-footed hops all the way).
  4. Leg 4: snowy-owl swoop (arms out, slow flapping glide). First team sitting in a line when their last runner returns wins.

Variations

Easier: One animal walk per round instead of four legs; everyone does the same one.

Harder: Add a 'blizzard obstacle': teammates crawl under a held-up blanket halfway.

⚠️ Safety

Clear the floor of anything slippery first; no running full speed on tile.
