Puffy Snow Slime

Mix up a batch of moldable, stretchy 'snow' and sculpt a miniature winter world with small toys.

Ages
6–12
Group
1–20 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pre-measure glue and baking soda into bowls; keep contact solution at a central station.

Materials

  • white school glue
  • shaving cream
  • contact lens solution (with boric acid)
  • baking soda
  • glitter
  • bowls
  • spoons
  • small plastic animals or figures

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix 1/2 cup white glue with 1/2 teaspoon baking soda and stir well.
  2. Add 2–3 big squirts of shaving cream and fold it in — this is what makes it puffy like snow.
  3. Stir in glitter, then add contact lens solution one tablespoon at a time, kneading between additions, until it pulls away from the bowl.
  4. Knead for 2–3 minutes. If it's sticky, add a few more drops of solution — never pour.
  5. Sculpt a mini winter scene on a tray and add small plastic animals or figures.
  6. Talk through the science: the glue's polymers link up (cross-link) when the solution is added, turning liquid into stretchy solid.

Variations

Easier: Leader mixes the base; kids knead and decorate.

Harder: Run the experiment round with three different add-ins and chart results.

⚠️ Safety

Check for skin sensitivities; wash hands after. Keep ingredients away from mouths.
