Spooky Story Circle

A not-too-spooky group story, two sentences at a time.

Ages
6–12
Group
4–25 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Arrange chairs or cushions in a tight circle. Dim the lights a little if you like; keep one flashlight handy for the narrator.

Materials

  • one flashlight (optional)

Steps

  1. Set the ground rules: spooky is fun, scary is not the goal, and anyone can say 'pass' at any time.
  2. The leader starts a story with two sentences and passes the flashlight.
  3. Each kid adds two sentences. The story can turn silly at any point.
  4. Go around twice, then let the group vote on the best ending — or just let the last kid finish it.
  5. Cool-down: name one thing that made you laugh during the story before moving on.

Variations

Easier: The leader tells the whole story; kids only add sound effects.

Harder: Add a prop bag — each narrator draws a random object that must appear in their part.

⚠️ Safety

Keep it spooky-fun, not scary — anyone can pass, no jump scares on purpose, and keep the room lit enough to see faces.
