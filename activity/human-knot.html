Human Knot

Grab random hands across the circle, then untangle without letting go.

Ages
7–12
Group
8–20 kids
Time
10–25 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Circle up, shoulder to shoulder. Works best with 8–16 players.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Everyone reaches their right hand into the middle and grabs someone else's right hand — not the person next to them.
  2. Do the same with left hands, grabbing a different person.
  3. The result is a human knot. The goal: untangle into a clean circle without letting go.
  4. Talk while you work — 'step over,' 'duck under,' 'turn around.'
  5. If the knot is truly stuck after several minutes, allow one grip to re-grab and continue.

Variations

Easier: Play with eyes open (standard) and a smaller group of 8.

Harder: One player is the 'director' who can only talk, not be part of the knot.

⚠️ Safety

Gentle hands only — no yanking arms. If a grip hurts, let go and re-grab.
