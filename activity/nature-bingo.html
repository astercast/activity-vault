Nature Bingo

Bingo cards meet the great outdoors — mark what you spot.

Ages
6–11
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–35 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Print bingo cards with pictures or words: bird, pinecone, yellow flower, spider web, smooth stone, mushroom, feather, ant, cloud shaped like something, litter (pick it up!).

Materials

  • printed nature bingo cards
  • pencils or crayons

Steps

  1. Hand out cards and pencils. Explain: mark a square when you FIND it — no collecting needed.
  2. Set boundaries and a time limit (15–20 minutes).
  3. First to complete a row shouts 'BINGO!' — verify the finds together.
  4. Keep going for blackout (whole card) if the group is engaged.
  5. End by picking up any litter spotted during the game.

Variations

Easier: Use picture-only cards for pre-readers.

Harder: Require photo evidence of each square for a camera round.

⚠️ Safety

Stay on paths and in the agreed area. Look with eyes first — some plants sting.
