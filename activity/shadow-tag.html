Shadow Tag

Tag isn't bodies — it's shadows. Step on someone's shadow to make them 'it.' Zero contact, all strategy.

Ages
5–12
Group
4–25 kids
Time
15–15 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

None — just need sun and open pavement.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Play on a sunny day on pavement or short grass where shadows are sharp.
  2. One person is 'it' — but instead of tagging bodies, they tag SHADOWS by stepping on them.
  3. Tagged players become 'it.' No pushing, no contact at all — that's the beauty of it.
  4. At noon the game gets brutally hard (short shadows); late afternoon it's easy mode. Talk about why.
  5. Variation: freeze tag version — tagged shadows freeze until a free player steps on their shadow to unfreeze them.

Variations

Easier: Bigger play area.

Harder: Play at noon when shadows are shortest.

⚠️ Safety

Sun safety — hats and water; no pushing, ever.
