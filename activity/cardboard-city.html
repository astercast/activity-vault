Cardboard Box City

Teams of engineers build a cardboard metropolis — houses, shops, landmarks — then connect their blocks into one city.

Ages
6–12
Group
4–25 kids
Time
60–60 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Collect boxes for two weeks; assign each team a table block.

Materials

  • cardboard boxes (all sizes)
  • paper towel tubes
  • tape (lots)
  • scissors
  • markers
  • paint
  • paper

Steps

  1. Split into teams of 3–4. Each team claims a table as their city block.
  2. Assign building types: every team must build at least one house, one shop, and one landmark — everything else is free choice.
  3. Build with boxes, tubes, and tape. Cut windows and doors, draw brick patterns, add paper roofs.
  4. Connect the blocks: teams negotiate where roads, bridges, and parks go between their tables.
  5. City tour: each team presents their block. Vote on awards — tallest building, funniest shop name, best park.

Variations

Easier: Give each team a pre-cut house kit to decorate.

Harder: Add a working drawbridge or elevator using string and pulleys.

⚠️ Safety

Box cutters are adult-only, always. Watch for staples in recycled boxes.
