DIY Lava Lamps

Oil, water, and fizzing tablets become a working lava lamp — and a perfect demo of density and gas.

Ages
6–12
Group
1–20 kids
Time
25–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pre-fill bottles one-third with water; set out oil and coloring.

Materials

  • clear plastic bottles
  • vegetable oil
  • water
  • food coloring
  • Alka-Seltzer tablets

Steps

  1. Fill a clear bottle one-third full with water, then add vegetable oil until nearly full. Let it settle — the layers separate.
  2. Add 8–10 drops of food coloring. Watch them sink through the oil and burst into the water layer.
  3. Drop in half an Alka-Seltzer tablet and watch: blobs of colored water rise, pop, and fall back down.
  4. Ask why: the tablet makes carbon dioxide bubbles that carry water upward; when bubbles pop, gravity pulls the water back.
  5. When the fizzing stops, cap the bottle — it becomes a permanent (if sleepy) decoration. Add another half-tablet to wake it up.

Variations

Easier: Leader adds the tablet; kids observe and describe.

Harder: Test variables: warm vs. cold water, tablet halves vs. quarters.

⚠️ Safety

Alka-Seltzer is not candy — keep tablets away from mouths; wash hands after.
