DIY Shaker Instruments

Fill, seal, and decorate shakers with different fillings — then line them up soft-to-loud and jam.

Ages
4–10
Group
1–20 kids
Time
25–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set out containers, fillings, and decorating supplies.

Materials

  • small sealable containers
  • rice
  • dried beans
  • beads
  • sand
  • tape
  • stickers
  • markers

Steps

  1. Fill each sealed container (plastic eggs, small bottles, paper cups taped shut) with a different filler: rice, beans, beads, sand.
  2. Seal everything tight — tape lids, glue caps. Shakers that open mid-song are a special kind of chaos.
  3. Decorate the outside with stickers, tape patterns, and markers.
  4. Sound test: line up all the shakers from softest to loudest. The variety is the instrument collection.
  5. Jam session: play a song and let everyone shake along. Assign 'loud parts' and 'quiet parts' like a real band.

Variations

Easier: Leader pre-fills; kids decorate.

Harder: Tune shakers by fill amount; arrange soft-to-loud scientifically.

⚠️ Safety

Seal containers twice — rice on the floor is forever. Choking hazard check for under-4s.
