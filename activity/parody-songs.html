Rewrite-the-Song Parody

Rewrite a hit song's lyrics about your group — same melody, all-new words — then perform it in concert.

Ages
8–12
Group
3–20 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Print original lyrics; split into writing groups.

Materials

  • paper
  • pencils
  • lyrics of the original song (printed)

Steps

  1. Pick a song everyone knows — a pop hit, a classic, a school anthem.
  2. Small groups rewrite the lyrics about your group: inside jokes, the week's events, a teacher's catchphrases (with permission).
  3. Keep the melody and rhythm — only the words change. That's what makes parodies work.
  4. Groups get 20 minutes to write and rehearse. Rhyming is encouraged but optional.
  5. Concert time: each group performs. Vote on funniest lyrics, best performance, most likely to get stuck in your head.

Variations

Easier: Rewrite just the chorus.

Harder: Perform with choreography and costumes.

⚠️ Safety

Keep lyrics kind — parody the week, not people.
