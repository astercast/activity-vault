Papel Picado Banners

Fold and snip tissue paper into the colorful cut-paper banners of Día de los Muertos.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
30–50 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cut tissue paper into rectangles (about 8x10 inches). Stack 3–4 sheets to cut several at once. Set out string for the finished banner.

Materials

  • bright tissue paper
  • scissors
  • string or yarn
  • tape or glue

Steps

  1. Fold the tissue rectangle in half, then in half again — small folds make detailed cuts.
  2. Cut shapes out of the folded edges: triangles, half-circles, diamonds. Don't cut the fold completely apart.
  3. Unfold carefully to reveal the design — every banner looks different.
  4. Tape the top edge of each finished panel to the string, spacing them out.
  5. Hang the banner up. Share what you know: papel picado are the festive cut-paper banners made in Mexico for Día de los Muertos — we're practicing the craft with respect for the tradition it comes from.

Variations

Easier: Cut simpler shapes (just triangles along one edge) on fewer folded layers.

Harder: Design a panel with a specific symbol — a marigold, a skull, a heart — and learn what it represents.

⚠️ Safety

Scissors cut paper only. Tissue tears easily, so unfold slowly.
