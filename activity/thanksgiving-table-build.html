Thanksgiving Table Build

Toothpicks and glue: build a table with at most 4 legs that holds the feast.

Ages
8–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
40–60 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cover the table with wax paper — glue peels right off. Hand out toothpicks and glue per team.

Materials

  • toothpicks
  • white glue
  • wax paper
  • books for weight-testing

Steps

  1. The constraint: a table with AT MOST 4 legs, standing on its own.
  2. Sketch the leg design first — triangles beat squares for strength.
  3. Build on the wax paper; let the glue set (build one day, test the next if you can).
  4. Load books one at a time onto the tabletop; record the max before it fails.
  5. Compare: which leg designs held the most? What would you change?

Variations

Easier: Build with thicker skewers and test with paperback books only.

Harder: Limit it to 20 toothpicks total — the constraint forces smarter design.

⚠️ Safety

Toothpicks are pointy — build on the table, never sword-fight. Check glue bottles for clogs before frustrated squeezing starts.

Inspired by an idea from the teaching community — source post (adapted in our own words).

🤝 Inclusion notes

Thicker skewers for easier grip; team roles (planner, builder, tester); test narrated aloud so everyone follows the results.
