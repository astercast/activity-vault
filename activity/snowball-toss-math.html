Snowball Toss Math

Toss indoor snowballs into point buckets — arithmetic with your whole body.

Ages
6–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Line up the point buckets along one wall, a few feet from the throw line. Give every kid two snowballs. Post a score chart.

Materials

  • soft indoor snowballs (crumpled paper or yarn balls, 2 per kid)
  • buckets or boxes labeled 1, 5, 10, 25

Steps

  1. Each kid throws both snowballs into the buckets and adds their points.
  2. After everyone has thrown, kids compute their running totals.
  3. Round two: teams throw and pool their points — first team to 100 wins.
  4. Close with the 'blizzard round': everyone throws at once, then the class sorts and counts every bucket together.

Variations

Easier: Use buckets labeled 1 and 2 only; count together on fingers.

Harder: Add a multiplier bucket: a snowball in the star bucket doubles your round.

⚠️ Safety

Throw underhand only, and only toward the buckets — no aiming at people.
