Shadow Puppet Theatre

Cardboard, a flashlight, and a sheet — a theatre in the dark.

Ages
5–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
30–60 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Hang a white sheet as the screen with the light behind it. Cut simple puppet shapes from cardboard and tape them to sticks.

Materials

  • a flashlight or desk lamp
  • a white sheet or large paper screen
  • cardboard, sticks, and tape for puppets

Steps

  1. Darken the room and set the light behind the screen.
  2. Show how shadows work: move a puppet closer for big, farther for small.
  3. Let kids make their own puppets — animals are the easiest first try.
  4. Each kid or pair performs a 1-minute shadow scene.
  5. Finale: the whole group does one big shadow story together.

Variations

Easier: Use hands only — classic shadow animals, no puppets to build.

Harder: Add a narrator and sound effects crew for a full production.

⚠️ Safety

Keep the light away from the sheet if it gets hot — LED flashlights are safest, and never stare into the beam.
