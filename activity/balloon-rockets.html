Balloon Rocket Races

Balloons taped to straws become jet-powered rockets racing along strings — Newton's third law you can see.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–20 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

String lines across the room and tape them taut.

Materials

  • balloons
  • drinking straws
  • string
  • tape
  • tape measures

Steps

  1. Thread a straw onto a long piece of string and stretch the string tight across the room — tape both ends.
  2. Blow up a balloon (don't tie it) and tape it to the straw, nozzle pointing backward.
  3. Countdown, release, and watch the rocket zoom along the string. Measure the distance.
  4. Experiment: does a bigger balloon go farther? What if you angle the string uphill?
  5. Race round: two strings, two rockets, best of three. Losers explain what they'd change.

Variations

Easier: Race on short 6-foot strings.

Harder: Angle the string uphill and engineer for maximum distance.

⚠️ Safety

Balloons are a choking hazard for under-4s; dispose of popped pieces immediately.
