Capture the Flag

Sneak in, grab the flag, get home — the playground epic.

Ages
8–12
Group
10–30 kids
Time
20–45 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Split a field in half with a center line. Each team hides its flag somewhere on its side (visible, not buried). Mark a jail zone on each side.

Materials

  • two flags (bandanas, cones, or pool noodles)
  • cones or rope to mark the center line and jail zones

Steps

  1. Split into two teams and send each to its side of the field.
  2. Goal: sneak across, grab the other team's flag, and carry it back to your side.
  3. If tagged on enemy territory, you go to their jail — teammates can free you with a tag.
  4. First team to carry the enemy flag across the center line wins.
  5. Play best-of-three with new flag spots each round.

Variations

Easier: Play with a visible flag on a pole to keep rounds short.

Harder: Add a second flag per team, or make jailed players do 10 jumping jacks to earn release.

⚠️ Safety

Agree on boundaries before starting. Tag with two fingers on the shoulder — no tackling, pushing, or tripping.
