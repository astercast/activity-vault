Spiderweb Plate Weaving

Weave yarn across a paper plate into a web, then add a spider.

Ages
5–11
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Punch 10–12 holes evenly around each plate's rim. Cut the center out of the plates so only the rim ring remains (adults do the center cut).

Materials

  • paper plates
  • hole punch
  • yarn or string
  • scissors
  • plastic spider rings or pom-poms (optional)

Steps

  1. Tie yarn through one hole with a knot to anchor it.
  2. Weave across the plate: through one hole, across the middle, out another hole — crisscross until a web forms.
  3. Tie off the end with a knot and trim the tail.
  4. Add a spider: a plastic ring spider, or make one from a pom-pom with pipe-cleaner legs.
  5. Hang the webs from the ceiling with string.

Variations

Easier: Use pre-punched rims and shorter yarn pieces; the teacher helps with knots.

Harder: Weave a pattern (over-two, under-one) and add a second color for a spiral effect.

⚠️ Safety

Adults cut the plate centers — rims tear easily, so go slow.
