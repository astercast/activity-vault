👋 Icebreakers

Name Memory Chain

Repeat every name-and-favorite before adding your own — the chain grows until the whole circle is laughing.

Ages
5–12
Group
5–30 kids
Time
15–15 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Sit in a circle; no materials needed.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Sit in a circle. The first person says their name plus one thing they like: 'I'm Maya and I like skateboarding.'
  2. The next person repeats everything before them, then adds their own: 'That's Maya, she likes skateboarding. I'm Devon and I like tacos.'
  3. Keep going around. By person ten, the whole group is holding their breath trying to remember.
  4. If someone gets stuck, the group helps — it's collaborative, not a test.
  5. For big groups, split into two circles so the chains stay manageable.

Variations

Easier: Just names, no favorite things.

Harder: Add a gesture for each person that the group must repeat.

⚠️ Safety

Keep it supportive, never a gotcha.
