Kwanzaa Unity Garland

A red, black, and green garland made while learning about Kwanzaa's seven principles — a values craft, not a ceremony.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cut paper into strips. Frame the activity as learning about Kwanzaa — a week-long celebration of family, community, and culture — and its seven principles (the Nguzo Saba). This is a classroom craft about its values, not a ceremony.

Materials

  • red, black, and green construction paper
  • scissors
  • glue sticks or tape
  • markers

Steps

  1. Cut strips of red, black, and green paper.
  2. On each strip, write one of the seven principles (unity, self-determination, collective work, cooperative economics, purpose, creativity, faith).
  3. Link the strips into a garland, alternating the three colors.
  4. Read the garland together and discuss: what does each principle look like in our classroom?

Variations

Easier: Use fewer strips (three colors, one word each: unity, creativity, faith).

Harder: Research what each principle means in practice and add an example to each strip.

⚠️ Safety

Cultural note: this craft is for learning about Kwanzaa, not performing it. Present it as a values celebration some families observe; keep the tone respectful.
