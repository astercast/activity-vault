Spring Bulb Forcing

Trick a bulb into blooming weeks early, in a jar.

Ages 6–12 · 20–40 min · 4 materials

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Fill each jar a third of the way with pebbles. Nestle one bulb pointy-side-up on the pebbles and add water just to the bulb's base.

Materials

  • flower bulbs (daffodil or paperwhite)
  • clear jars or glasses
  • pebbles or marbles
  • water

Steps

  1. Build the jars: pebbles, bulb on top, water touching only the bulb's bottom.
  2. Place jars in a cool, dark spot for the first week. A closet or under a box works.
  3. Move to a bright windowsill and watch roots, then shoots, then buds appear.
  4. Measure and record growth every few days on a shared chart.
  5. Celebrate the first bloom, then plant the bulbs outside after the last frost.

Variations

Easier: One class jar everyone watches together.

Harder: Run an experiment: dark closet vs. bright window from day one, compare.

⚠️ Safety

Wash hands after handling bulbs. Keep bulbs away from mouths. Some are irritating if chewed.
