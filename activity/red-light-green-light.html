Red Light, Green Light

Sprint on green, freeze on red. Get caught moving and start over.

Before you begin

Ages
5–11
Group
6–40 kids
Time
10–25 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
No prep needed

Setup

One caller stands at the far end with their back to the group. Everyone else lines up at the start line.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Caller shouts 'green light!' and turns around. Everyone runs toward them.
  2. Caller shouts 'red light!' and spins back. Everyone must freeze instantly.
  3. Anyone the caller catches moving goes back to the start line.
  4. First player to tag the caller becomes the next caller.
  5. Add 'yellow light' (slow motion) for a fun twist in later rounds.

Variations

Easier: Shorten the field so rounds end faster.

Harder: Caught players become extra spotters helping the caller.

Safety

Play on a flat field or gym, no running on pavement near traffic.
