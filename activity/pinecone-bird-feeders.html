Pinecone Bird Feeders

Pinecones + peanut butter + birdseed = a winter buffet for local birds.

Ages
6–11
Group
2–25 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Outdoors & Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

A winter favorite. Collect pinecones ahead of time or buy in bulk. Tie string to each cone first — it is fiddly work kids find frustrating.

Materials

  • large pinecones
  • peanut butter or sunflower-seed butter
  • birdseed
  • string
  • butter knives or popsicle sticks for spreading

Steps

  1. Tie a loop of string around the top of each pinecone for hanging.
  2. Spread peanut butter (or sunbutter) all over the cone, pushing it into the cracks.
  3. Roll the sticky cone in birdseed until it is fully coated.
  4. Hang the feeders on trees near a window where kids can watch.
  5. Keep a bird-spotting log: which birds visit, and when?

Variations

Easier: Use toilet-paper rolls instead of pinecones — easier to coat.

Harder: Turn it into a study: which seed mix attracts the most species?

⚠️ Safety

Check for nut allergies first — use sunflower-seed butter if anyone is allergic. Wash hands after.
