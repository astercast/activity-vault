Kindness Scavenger Hunt

A scavenger hunt where the items are kind acts.

Ages
7–12
Group
6–30 kids
Time
25–45 min
Where
Outdoors & Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Print a checklist of kind acts: hold a door, pick up litter, compliment a stranger's shoes, help carry something, draw chalk art for the sidewalk. Works around holidays or any day.

Materials

  • printed kindness checklists

Steps

  1. Split into teams of 3–4 and hand out the checklists.
  2. Set the boundaries and the time limit (20–30 minutes).
  3. Teams roam and complete as many kind acts as they can. An adult verifies each one.
  4. Regroup and share the best stories — what act got the biggest reaction?
  5. No prizes for most acts; celebrate the stories instead.

Variations

Easier: Do it inside the building with staff as the targets of kindness.

Harder: Add a secret bonus act each team must invent themselves.

⚠️ Safety

Teams stay within the agreed boundaries and in sight of an adult.
