Cloud Storytelling

Lie back, find shapes in the clouds, then build a group story where every sentence starts from a new cloud.

Ages
4–10
Group
2–20 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Lay out blankets; no other prep.

Materials

  • blankets or mats

Steps

  1. Lie down on blankets or grass and watch the clouds for 2 quiet minutes. No talking yet — just looking.
  2. Each person picks one cloud and announces what they see: 'That one looks like a dragon eating a taco.'
  3. Now the story round: the leader starts a tale using someone's cloud ('The dragon swooped down...'), and each person adds a sentence inspired by a new cloud shape.
  4. Keep it going until the story ends or the clouds run out.
  5. The best stories get retold at pickup time — kids love performing them for parents.

Variations

Easier: Just find shapes — skip the story round.

Harder: Write the story down afterward as a group book.

⚠️ Safety

Sun safety; don't stare at the sun.
