Paper Airplane Challenge

Fold, throw, measure, iterate — the cheapest engineering lesson there is.

Ages
7–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
20–45 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Clear a throwing lane down the longest hallway, gym, or field you have. Mark a launch line with tape. Give every kid 3 sheets of paper.

Materials

  • printer paper (a stack)
  • tape measure or long measuring tape

Steps

  1. Show the group one basic dart fold, step by step — pause so everyone can follow along.
  2. Let kids fold their first plane however they like (copies of the demo are fine).
  3. One at a time, each kid throws from the launch line. Measure each flight and write the distance on a board.
  4. Round two: kids redesign. They can refold, add a second sheet, or try a wide-wing glider style.
  5. Measure again. Celebrate biggest improvement, not just longest flight — that rewards the iterating.
  6. Optional finals: best distance, best style, most creative design, weirdest crash.

Variations

Easier: Pre-crease the paper down the middle so the first fold is easy to line up.

Harder: Add target rings (hula hoops on the ground) and score by accuracy instead of distance.

⚠️ Safety

Throw only down the lane and only on your turn — no aiming at other kids.
