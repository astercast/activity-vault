Peer Skill Swap

Everyone teaches a five-minute skill. You leave knowing twelve new things.

Ages 12–14 · 30–60 min · no materials

Ages
12–14
Group
6–30 kids
Time
30–60 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

No setup. Everyone needs one teachable 5-minute skill. Give them a minute to think of theirs.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Go around: each person names a skill they can teach in 5 minutes. Juggling, knots, a card trick, a phrase in another language.
  2. Pair up. Teacher teaches, learner learns, 5 minutes, then switch roles.
  3. Rotate: find a new partner and teach YOUR skill again. Teaching twice makes it stick.
  4. After three rotations, everyone demos one skill they LEARNED (not their own) for the group.
  5. Vote: most surprising skill, best teacher, skill most likely to get used again.

Variations

Easier: Do two rotations instead of three, or let pairs teach the whole group instead of rotating.

Harder: Chain the skills (each learner must use a learned skill in their next lesson), or film 60-second tutorials.

⚠️ Safety

Skills must be classroom-safe, no fire, no sharp tools, nothing that would need a permission slip.
