Cup Song Circle

One cup, one rhythm, a whole circle in sync.

Ages
8–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Sit in a circle with one plastic cup in front of each kid. Learn the rhythm together slowly.

Materials

  • plastic cups (one per kid)

Steps

  1. Teach the basic pattern step by step: clap, tap the cup, pass it right.
  2. Practice slowly as a group until the pattern is smooth.
  3. Speed up gradually — the circle keeps the beat together.
  4. Challenge: pass the cup AND keep a steady chant or song going.
  5. Performance round: the whole circle runs the pattern three times clean.

Variations

Easier: Just clap and tap — skip the passing.

Harder: Two cups per person, or pass in alternating directions.

⚠️ Safety

Cups stay on the table or floor — no throwing, no stacking on heads.
