Snow Globe Slow-Fall Lab

Build a real snow globe, then hunt for the slowest-falling snow.

Ages
5–12
Group
1–24 kids
Time
30–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set out jars and snow ingredients in separate cups; hot-glue trees to lids ahead of time if kids are under 7.

Materials

  • small clear plastic jars with lids
  • water
  • glitter, foam beads, and paper-hole-punch dots (the snow)
  • a spoonful of clear glue (optional, slows the fall)
  • small waterproof figurines or bottle-brush trees
  • waterproof tape

Steps

  1. Glue a tiny tree or figure to the inside of the jar lid and let it set.
  2. Fill the jar nearly full with water and add a spoonful of clear glue.
  3. Try one kind of snow at a time. Shake, then time how long the snow takes to settle.
  4. Record each snow type's fall time on a chart.
  5. Pick the slowest snow for the final globe, then seal the lid with tape.

Variations

Easier: Skip the timing; just build the globe with the snow you like best.

Harder: Graph all the fall times and write one sentence about why the slowest one won.

⚠️ Safety

Plastic jars only for kids; adult tightens and tapes the lid shut.
