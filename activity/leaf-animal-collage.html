Leaf Animal Collage

Dry leaves become foxes, owls, and hedgehogs — nature's sticker book.

Ages
4–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Spread the leaves out on a table so kids can browse shapes. Hand out cardstock and glue.

Materials

  • a basket of dry fall leaves in different shapes and colors
  • glue sticks
  • cardstock or heavy paper
  • googly eyes (optional)
  • markers

Steps

  1. Sort through the leaves: which ones look like ears? A round body? A pointy tail?
  2. Lay out an animal on the paper WITHOUT gluing first — arrange, step back, adjust.
  3. Glue the pieces down one at a time, biggest pieces first.
  4. Add details with markers or googly eyes: faces, legs, whiskers.
  5. Title the piece and hang a class 'leaf zoo' gallery.

Variations

Easier: Glue pre-cut leaf shapes onto a printed animal outline.

Harder: Make a whole habitat scene — the animal plus leaf trees, sun, and ground.
