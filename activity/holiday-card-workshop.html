Holiday Card Workshop

Handmade cards beat store-bought ones — every time.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–40 kids
Time
20–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Pre-fold cardstock into cards. Set out decorating supplies assembly-line style.

Materials

  • folded blank cards or folded cardstock
  • markers, crayons, colored pencils
  • stickers, stamps (optional)
  • envelopes

Steps

  1. Each kid picks who their card is for — family, a friend, a teacher.
  2. Design the front: a drawing, a pattern, or a big bold letter.
  3. Write the message inside. Prompt: one real memory + one wish for them.
  4. Decorate the envelope too — it is part of the gift.
  5. Collect cards in a 'post box' and hand them out at pickup time.

Variations

Easier: Provide printed card templates with a design to color.

Harder: Make pop-up cards — cut two slits in the fold and push the shape inward.
