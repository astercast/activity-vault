Magic Milk Art

A drop of soapy cotton swab sends food coloring exploding across milk in marbled swirls.

Ages
4–10
Group
1–20 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pour milk into shallow dishes; set out coloring and soapy swabs.

Materials

  • whole milk
  • food coloring
  • dish soap
  • cotton swabs
  • shallow dishes or plates

Steps

  1. Pour whole milk into a shallow dish until the bottom is covered.
  2. Drop 4–5 drops of different food coloring around the milk — don't stir.
  3. Dip a cotton swab in dish soap, then touch it to the milk's surface.
  4. Watch the colors explode outward in swirling patterns. Touch a new spot to trigger it again.
  5. Explain simply: soap breaks the milk's surface tension and chases the fat molecules, dragging color along for the ride.

Variations

Easier: Leader demos while kids watch and describe.

Harder: Test whole vs. skim milk and compare the color explosions.

⚠️ Safety

Food coloring stains — smocks on.
