Bookmarks That Matter

Design a bookmark worth keeping — then actually use it.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–40 kids
Time
15–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Pre-cut cardstock into strips (about 2 x 6 inches). Set out decorating supplies.

Materials

  • cardstock cut into bookmark strips
  • markers, colored pencils
  • hole punch and yarn or ribbon (optional)

Steps

  1. Each kid takes a bookmark strip and picks a theme: favorite book, a quote, a pattern, a monster.
  2. Decorate both sides — one side art, one side words.
  3. Punch a hole at the top and tie on yarn for a tassel if you have it.
  4. Laminate or cover with clear tape to make them last.
  5. Trade one with a friend and keep one for your own book.

Variations

Easier: Provide printed templates with borders to color in.

Harder: Make corner bookmarks (origami fold that hugs the page corner).
