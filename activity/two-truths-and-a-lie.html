Two Truths and a Lie

Two true facts, one fib — can the group spot the lie?

Ages
7–12
Group
5–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Circle up. Everyone thinks of two true things about themselves and one made-up thing.

Materials

  • paper and pencils (optional)

Steps

  1. One player shares their three statements in any order.
  2. The group discusses and votes on which one is the lie.
  3. Reveal! The best liars get the biggest reactions.
  4. Go around the circle — keep statements short so the game moves.
  5. Award the 'best poker face' at the end.

Variations

Easier: Do it in small groups of 5–6 instead of one big circle.

Harder: Theme it: two truths and a lie about summer, about food, about a talent.
