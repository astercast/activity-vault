Common Ground Hunt

Small groups race to find five surprising things all members share — obvious answers don't count.

Ages
7–12
Group
8–30 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Split into groups of 4–5 with paper and pencils.

Materials

  • paper
  • pencils
  • timer

Steps

  1. Split into groups of 4–5. Each group has 5 minutes to find 5 things ALL members have in common — not obvious ones like 'we're all kids.'
  2. Groups write their list, avoiding the easy answers. 'We've all lost a tooth at school' beats 'we all have hair.'
  3. Each group reads their list. The group with the most surprising common ground wins.
  4. Round two: find one thing that's TRUE for everyone in the whole room.
  5. The hunt forces real conversation — kids discover connections they'd never have guessed.

Variations

Easier: Find 3 things instead of 5.

Harder: The common ground can't involve school, age, or appearance.

⚠️ Safety

None.
