Class Mural Project

One square foot per kid, one giant story on the wall.

Ages
5–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
40–90 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Roll butcher paper across a long table or the floor. Sketch a light theme outline — a garden, a city, a galaxy.

Materials

  • a long roll of butcher paper
  • tempera paint or big markers
  • pencils for sketching

Steps

  1. Assign sections: every kid owns one square foot of the mural.
  2. Sketch first in pencil — no paint until the sketch is approved by a neighbor.
  3. Paint the mural together over the session.
  4. Add everyone's name or handprint along the border.
  5. Hang it where the whole school can see it.

Variations

Easier: Coloring-style: the teacher draws the outlines, kids fill them in.

Harder: No talking during painting — the mural must come together silently.

⚠️ Safety

Smocks or old shirts for painters, and wash hands before touching anything else.
