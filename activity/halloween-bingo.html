Halloween Bingo

Bingo with bats, pumpkins, and black cats — shout 'BOO!' instead of 'bingo'.

Ages
5–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
20–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Hand out cards and tokens. The caller needs the full list of Halloween words/pictures (ghost, bat, pumpkin, witch hat, spider, moon, candy, black cat, haunted house).

Materials

  • printed Halloween bingo cards (or blank cards kids fill with Halloween drawings)
  • tokens (dried beans, paper squares)
  • a caller set of picture cards or just call words aloud

Steps

  1. The caller draws or announces one Halloween item at a time.
  2. Kids cover the matching square on their card.
  3. First to complete a row shouts 'BOO!' — check the card, then keep playing for blackout.
  4. Winners get to be the caller for the next round.
  5. Final round: everyone plays two cards at once for the championship.

Variations

Easier: Play with picture cards and matching (no reading needed) for younger kids.

Harder: Kids make their own cards by drawing 9 Halloween items in a grid — then swap cards and play.
