Compliment Card Exchange

Everyone makes a card mailbox, then the group fills them with anonymous compliments — the sweetest mail call of the year.

Ages
5–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set up mailbox bags and stacks of compliment slips with pens.

Materials

  • paper bags or folded cardstock
  • markers
  • stickers
  • slips of paper
  • tape

Steps

  1. Each child decorates a paper bag as their personal 'mailbox' and tapes it to their desk or a wall spot.
  2. Hand out slips of paper. Everyone writes one genuine compliment for every other kid — specific beats generic ('you're great at explaining games' beats 'you're nice').
  3. Set a rule: compliments must be true, kind, and about something the person does, not just looks.
  4. Deliver all notes into mailboxes anonymously.
  5. Open mail call: kids read their favorites aloud if they want. Keep a few blank slips for kids who finish early to write extras.

Variations

Easier: Give each child 3 assigned recipients instead of the whole group.

Harder: Write compliments as short poems or acrostics.

⚠️ Safety

Set the tone: compliments must be kind and true — no joke insults.
