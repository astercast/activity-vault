Mini Pumpkin Bowling

Roll small pumpkins at bottle 'pins' in this harvest twist on bowling — easy to set up, endlessly replayable.

Ages
4–10
Group
2–20 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set up bottle pins and mark a foul line with tape.

Materials

  • mini pumpkins (the 'balls')
  • plastic bottles or cups (the 'pins')
  • tape
  • painter's tape for the lane

Steps

  1. Fill plastic bottles with a little water or sand so they stand but still topple, and arrange 6–10 as pins.
  2. Mark a foul line with painter's tape about 8–10 feet from the pins (closer for little kids).
  3. Players roll (never throw) their mini pumpkin down the lane — pumpkins wobble, which is the whole fun.
  4. Score like bowling or just count total pins; play in teams to keep everyone rotating quickly.
  5. Decorate the pins as ghosts with white paper for extra Halloween spirit.

Variations

Easier: Move the foul line closer; use a soft ball instead of a pumpkin.

Harder: Narrow the lane and add a second row of pins for a split.

⚠️ Safety

Roll, never throw, the pumpkins. Clear the lane of tripping hazards.
