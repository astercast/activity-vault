Arctic Animal Charades

Be the polar bear. Be the arctic fox. No talking — the tundra is silent.

Ages
5–11
Group
4–30 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Write arctic animals on cards (polar bear, arctic fox, snowy owl, walrus, penguin, caribou, seal, narwhal). Into the hat.

Materials

  • animal name cards
  • a hat or bowl

Steps

  1. One kid draws a card and acts the animal — no sounds, no words, movement only.
  2. The group shouts guesses. Correct guesser acts next.
  3. Round two: act the animal DOING something — the polar bear swimming, the owl hunting.
  4. Keep a list of every animal guessed on the board — that's your arctic field guide by the end.
  5. Final round: the whole group acts one animal together. Twenty penguins waddling is unforgettable.

Variations

Easier: Let kids make sounds too, or act in pairs.

Harder: Research round: learn one true fact per animal and announce it with the acting.
