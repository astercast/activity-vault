Seed Starter Cups

Plant seeds in clear cups and watch roots and sprouts develop day by day — a living science journal.

Ages
5–12
Group
1–25 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Fill cups with soil ahead of time; set out seeds, labels, and sprayers.

Materials

  • clear plastic cups
  • potting soil
  • fast seeds (beans, radish, sunflower)
  • water
  • labels
  • markers
  • rulers

Steps

  1. Poke 2–3 drainage holes in the bottom of each clear cup.
  2. Fill cups with damp potting soil, leaving an inch at the top. Press 2–3 seeds about a half-inch deep near the cup's edge so roots stay visible.
  3. Label each cup with the child's name, seed type, and planting date.
  4. Water lightly and place in a sunny spot. Water only when the top of the soil feels dry.
  5. Each day, kids measure sprout height and sketch what they see — roots first, then the shoot.
  6. After 2–3 weeks, transplant outdoors or send cups home with care instructions.

Variations

Easier: Plant pre-sprouted beans so results show on day one.

Harder: Run a variables test: light vs. dark, more vs. less water — track and graph.

⚠️ Safety

Potting soil can irritate eyes — no rubbing faces with dirty hands.
