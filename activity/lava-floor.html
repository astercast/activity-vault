The Floor Is Lava

Cushions become islands in a sea of lava — hop between them as islands disappear each round.

Ages
4–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Scatter cushions as islands with clear paths between.

Materials

  • cushions
  • mats or foam tiles
  • open floor space

Steps

  1. Scatter cushions, mats, and foam tiles across the floor as 'safe islands.' Clear a path between them.
  2. Shout 'THE FLOOR IS LAVA!' — everyone has 5 seconds to get onto an island. Anyone touching floor is 'melted' (out for the round, or does 5 jumping jacks to rejoin).
  3. Remove one island each round so it gets harder. Rearrange between rounds.
  4. Variation: call out how to travel — 'hop like a frog to the next island!' 'crab-walk!'
  5. Final round: one island left, whole group must fit on it together for 10 seconds.

Variations

Easier: Keep all islands in place; no removals.

Harder: Remove two islands per round and require specific travel moves.

⚠️ Safety

Cushions must not slide — use mats with grip; no jumping from heights.
