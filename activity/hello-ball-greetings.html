Hello Ball Greetings

Sit in a circle, roll a soft ball to a friend, and say their name out loud — the first icebreaker that works at three.

Ages
3–5
Group
4–20 kids
Time
10–15 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Sit in a circle close enough that a rolled ball reaches the kid across without a chase. Sit in the circle yourself — at three, the adult is a player, not a referee.

Materials

  • a soft, easy-grip ball (foam or playground ball, not a hard ball)

Steps

  1. Start every round yourself: roll the ball slowly to one kid and say it the way you want to hear it back — 'Hello, Sam.' Sam catches or stops the ball.
  2. Sam rolls it to someone new and greets them by name. A name that won't come gets a whisper from a neighbor or from you — nobody sits stuck holding the ball.
  3. Keep it moving: the greeter becomes the roller, and the game walks itself around the circle two or three times.
  4. Change the greeting, not the game: round two adds a wave, round three is a hello in your silliest quiet voice.
  5. Finish when every kid has been greeted at least twice — say so out loud as the last roll lands, so the shy kids hear their count too.

Variations

Easier: A smaller circle and a bigger ball; if rolling is still too much, the adult passes every ball hand to hand.

Harder: Add the return greeting — the catcher says 'Hello back' and the roller's name before rolling on. That is the first real name-memory game.

⚠️ Safety

The ball rolls on the floor, never throws — a foam ball at three is a rolling toy, full stop.

🤝 Inclusion notes

A greeting is any clear hello — a wave, a sign, a word tapped out on the floor. A kid who doesn't talk yet greets with the ball and a wave, and gets greeted back in full, by name, like everyone else.
