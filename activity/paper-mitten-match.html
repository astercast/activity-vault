Paper Mitten Match

Decorate mitten pairs, mix them up, and play memory across the room.

Ages
4–10
Group
2–24 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cut out pairs of paper mittens ahead of time (trace one hand shape and flip it). Give each kid two matching mittens.

Materials

  • paper mitten cutouts (2 per kid)
  • crayons and markers
  • a paper bag

Steps

  1. Each kid decorates their two mittens identically — same pattern, same colors.
  2. Collect one mitten from every kid into the paper bag and hide the other halves.
  3. Scatter the hidden mittens face-down on the rug; kids draw a mitten from the bag.
  4. One at a time, each kid flips a rug mitten trying to find their pair's match. Matched pairs hang on the 'clothesline' to dry.

Variations

Easier: Teacher matches for them: hold up two mittens, 'same or different?'

Harder: Three-mitten sets: decorate a trio and match all three.

⚠️ Safety

No running while searching — walk to the rug.
