Dissolving Warm-Drink Lab

Hot cocoa science: does powder dissolve faster in hot water or cold?

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set up two stations: warm water and cold water. Give each pair two clear cups, one spoon of cocoa mix, and a sugar cube.

Materials

  • cocoa mix
  • sugar cubes
  • clear cups
  • warm and cold water
  • spoons
  • a stopwatch or timer

Steps

  1. Predict: which cup dissolves the cocoa faster — warm or cold? Write it down.
  2. Add the cocoa to both cups at the same time. Time how long each takes to fully dissolve with the same stirring.
  3. Repeat with the sugar cubes — but this time, one cup stirred, one not.
  4. Chart the class results. Discuss: why did the warm water win?

Variations

Easier: Teacher runs the demo up front; kids just predict and watch.

Harder: Try milk vs. water, or test whether crushed vs. whole sugar cube matters.

⚠️ Safety

Water should be warm, not hot — an adult handles the kettle. Check for dairy allergies if tasting.
