Food Drive Planning

Kids plan the whole canned-food drive: goal, posters, collection crew.

Ages
7–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
30–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Call the local food bank or pantry first (teacher job) to confirm what they accept and when to deliver.

Materials

  • big paper
  • markers
  • empty boxes for collection bins
  • tape for posters

Steps

  1. Set the goal together: 100 cans? One can per kid per week?
  2. Make posters: what to bring, where the bins are, the deadline.
  3. Build the collection bins from boxes — decorate them loud.
  4. Assign the crew: bin monitors, counters, delivery team.
  5. Count day: tally everything, celebrate the total, deliver together.

Variations

Easier: Run a one-day mini drive: goal of one bag per kid, counted the same afternoon.

Harder: Kids write the thank-you notes to every family that donated.

⚠️ Safety

Cans only — no open or homemade food in the bins. Heavy boxes get carried by adults.

🤝 Inclusion notes

Poster, counter, and greeter roles for every mobility level.
