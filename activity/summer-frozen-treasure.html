Frozen Treasure Rescue

Small toys frozen in ice blocks become an excavation mission. Kids experiment with ways to melt and free them.

Before you begin

Ages
4–10
Group
1–20 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Freeze treasure blocks the night before (layer water, toy, water).

Materials

  • small plastic toys
  • plastic containers
  • water
  • salt
  • squeeze bottles
  • warm water
  • trays
  • towels

Steps

  1. The night before: place small toys in containers, fill with water, and freeze solid.
  2. Set each frozen block on a tray and hand out 'rescue tools': squeeze bottles of warm water, salt shakers, and spoons.
  3. Challenge: free your treasure using any tools. No smashing. Archaeologists are gentle.
  4. Let kids test hypotheses: does salt melt faster than warm water? What about both?
  5. Debrief: which method won, and why does salt melt ice? (It lowers water's freezing point.)

Variations

Easier: Use warm water only and smaller ice cubes.

Harder: Time each melting method and declare a winning technique with evidence.

Safety

No smashing ice, tools only. Mop meltwater so floors don't get slippery.

Source: Wikipedia: Buried treasure
