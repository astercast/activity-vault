Sink or Float Lab

Vote, drop, splash — the classic first experiment.

Ages
4–8
Group
2–25 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Fill a wide tub halfway with water on a towel-covered table. Gather a tray of test objects.

Materials

  • a wide tub or bin of water
  • assorted test objects: apple, rock, sponge, coin, cork, toy boat
  • towels for the inevitable splashes

Steps

  1. Hold up each object and vote: sink or float? Tally predictions on a chart.
  2. Drop the object in and watch. Cheer for the right predictors.
  3. Pull it out and dry it before the next test.
  4. After ten objects, ask: what did the floaters have in common?
  5. Final test: can you make a sinker float by changing its shape (foil boat challenge)?

Variations

Easier: Test five big, obvious objects only.

Harder: Add a third vote option: 'sink slowly' — and time the slow sinkers.

⚠️ Safety

Water stays in the tub — towels on the floor, no splashing contests, and dry hands before walking.
