Winter Ice Lanterns

Frozen water, berries, and a battery candle — winter glow.

Ages
5–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–45 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

This needs a freezing night (or a big freezer). Fill containers partway with water, add berries and leaves, and set a small weighted cup in the center to form the hollow.

Materials

  • plastic containers or balloons for molds
  • water
  • berries, leaves, or flower petals for decoration
  • battery-operated tea lights

Steps

  1. Fill each mold partway with water and arrange decorations in it.
  2. Set a small cup weighted with pebbles in the middle to create the candle hollow.
  3. Freeze overnight (or all day if it is cold enough outside).
  4. Pop the ice out of the mold and drop in a battery tea light.
  5. Line the lanterns up at dusk and watch them glow.

Variations

Easier: Freeze colored water in muffin tins for simple ice jewels.

Harder: Layer two freezes for decorations suspended at different depths.

⚠️ Safety

Battery candles only — never real flames in ice or near kids. Only handle frozen lanterns with dry hands.
