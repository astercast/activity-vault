Magazine Collage Boards

Tear, arrange, and glue magazine images into personal vision boards — then explain your choices on a gallery walk.

Ages
7–12
Group
1–25 kids
Time
40–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Spread magazine stacks and cardstock; put out glue sticks and markers.

Materials

  • old magazines and catalogs
  • cardstock or poster board
  • scissors
  • glue sticks
  • markers
  • stickers

Steps

  1. Set out stacks of old magazines, catalogs, and newspapers. Give everyone 5 minutes to flip and tear out anything that catches their eye — no plan yet.
  2. Ask a prompt: 'Make a board that shows your perfect weekend,' or 'your dream room,' or 'things that make you laugh.'
  3. Arrange the cutouts on cardstock before gluing — composition first, commitment second.
  4. Glue everything down, overlapping edges. Add doodles, captions, and stickers to tie it together.
  5. Gallery walk: everyone tours the boards. Each artist explains one choice they made.

Variations

Easier: Give a theme ('my perfect day') to focus choices.

Harder: Add a written artist statement explaining three choices.

⚠️ Safety

Scissors safety; nothing sharp in the collage bins.
