Group Juggling

Keep an ever-growing number of balls moving in a fixed circle pattern.

Ages
7–12
Group
6–20 kids
Time
10–25 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Stand in a circle. Establish a throwing order first with one ball before adding more.

Materials

  • 3–6 soft balls or beanbags

Steps

  1. Throw one ball around the circle in a fixed order — A always throws to B, B to C, and so on.
  2. Once the pattern is smooth, the leader calls out names to add a second ball, then a third.
  3. Keep adding balls until the pattern breaks down into laughter.
  4. Reset and try to beat your record for number of balls in play.
  5. Talk about it: the pattern only works when everyone remembers their person.

Variations

Easier: Use scarves or balloons first — they move slowly and are easy to catch.

Harder: Reverse the pattern mid-game on the leader's call.

⚠️ Safety

Use soft balls only — no hard baseballs.
