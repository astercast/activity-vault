Suet Cage Winter Feeder

Pack a wire suet cage with winter bird food — a high-energy feast for cold days.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set out bowls of shortening and dry ingredients. Put one suet cage and a length of string in front of each group.

Materials

  • wire suet cages (one per small group)
  • solid vegetable shortening
  • oats, cornmeal, and birdseed
  • mixing bowls and spoons
  • string

Steps

  1. Mix two parts dry ingredients with one part softened shortening into a crumbly dough.
  2. Pack the dough firmly into the suet cage — press it into every corner.
  3. Thread string through the cage top and tie a strong hanger loop.
  4. Hang the cages where birds can reach them but cats can't, and keep a visitor log.

Variations

Easier: Teacher mixes and packs; kids press the dough in with spoons.

Harder: Make two cages with different mixes (more oats vs. more seed) and compare visitors.

⚠️ Safety

Shortening only — no peanut butter unless allergies are cleared. Wash hands after.
