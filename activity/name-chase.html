Name Chase

Duck-duck-goose where you tap heads saying names — say the group's name and the chase is on.

Ages
5–10
Group
8–25 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Sit in a circle with room to run around it.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Sit in a circle. One person ('it') walks around the outside, tapping heads and saying each person's NAME instead of 'duck.'
  2. When 'it' says the group's name (e.g., 'Sunshine Club!') instead of a name, the tapped person jumps up and chases them around the circle.
  3. If the chaser tags 'it' before they steal the empty spot, 'it' stays 'it.' If not, the chaser becomes the new 'it.'
  4. Everyone learns names fast because you're saying them constantly.
  5. Play until everyone has been 'it' at least once — or until the giggling makes it impossible to continue.

Variations

Easier: Walk instead of run.

Harder: The chaser must say the tapped person's favorite thing, not just their name.

⚠️ Safety

Clear the circle's surroundings — chasers run blind corners.
