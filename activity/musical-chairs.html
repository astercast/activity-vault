Musical Chairs

Circle the chairs, grab a seat when the music stops.

Ages
6–11
Group
8–25 kids
Time
10–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Arrange chairs in a circle, facing outward, one fewer than the number of players.

Materials

  • chairs (one fewer than players)
  • music player

Steps

  1. Play music. Everyone walks (or dances) around the chairs.
  2. When the music stops, everyone grabs a seat. One player is left standing — they are out.
  3. Remove one chair and play again.
  4. Last player seated wins.
  5. Friendship variant: nobody is out — instead, everyone must SHARE the remaining chairs.

Variations

Easier: Play the sharing variant so nobody sits out.

Harder: Chairs face alternating directions to make the scramble trickier.

⚠️ Safety

Walk quickly to chairs — no diving or shoving. Keep the chair circle stable.
