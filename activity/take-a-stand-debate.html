Take-a-Stand Debate

Agree or disagree? Stand on your side, give one reason, and switch if you're convinced.

Ages
9–12
Group
6–40 kids
Time
30–50 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Clear a line down the middle of the room: one side is AGREE, the other is DISAGREE. Prepare 4–6 low-stakes statements (e.g., 'Cereal is better than pancakes', 'Homework should be optional on Fridays').

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Read a statement aloud. Everyone stands on the AGREE or DISAGREE side — no middle.
  2. Each side picks 2 speakers to give their best one-sentence reason.
  3. After hearing both sides, anyone may switch sides. Movers say what convinced them.
  4. New statement, everyone re-stands. Keep a brisk pace — 5 minutes per statement.
  5. Debrief: which switch surprised you? What makes a reason convincing?

Variations

Easier: Use silly, zero-stakes statements and let kids give reasons in pairs instead of to the room.

Harder: Let kids write the statements, then run a formal version with opening statements and rebuttals.

⚠️ Safety

Keep statements low-stakes and impersonal — no topics that single out a kid's life or beliefs.
