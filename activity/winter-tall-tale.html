Winter Tall Tale

A circle story where every kid adds one wild winter sentence.

Ages
4–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
10–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Nothing needed — just a circle. The teacher starts the story with one sentence.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Teacher: 'On the coldest morning of the year, I opened my door and saw...'
  2. Each kid adds exactly one sentence, building on the last one.
  3. Encourage wild but connected additions — the yeti borrowed the bus, the snow spoke back.
  4. After one full circle, the teacher closes the tale in three sentences.
  5. Optional: vote on the best sentence and start a new tale with it.

Variations

Easier: Kids can pass, or just add a sound effect instead of a sentence.

Harder: Two circles compete for the best ending, then swap and judge.
