Would You Rather

Impossible choices, hilarious defenses — the easiest discussion starter.

Ages
6–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
10–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Circle up. The leader has a list of kid-friendly dilemmas ready (or the group invents them).

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Leader asks: 'Would you rather have wings or gills?'
  2. Everyone points left for option A, right for option B — then defend your pick.
  3. Let kids take turns asking the next question.
  4. Rule: no judging anyone's answer — the weirder the reasoning, the better.
  5. End with everyone inventing one question for the group to vote on tomorrow.

Variations

Easier: Give two silly options only — keep it light for young kids.

Harder: Add 'why' — everyone must defend their choice in one sentence.
