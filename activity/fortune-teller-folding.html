Fortune Teller Folding

The classic playground origami — fold it, fill it, tell fortunes.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Pre-cut squares of paper if the group is young. One square per kid, plus extras for mistakes.

Materials

  • square paper (one per kid, plus extras)

Steps

  1. Teach the folds step by step: corners to center, flip, corners to center again.
  2. Tuck fingers under the four flaps to open the fortune teller.
  3. Write numbers on the outer flaps and fortunes inside.
  4. Pair up and tell each other's fortunes: pick a number, count it out, open a flap.
  5. Everyone writes one kind fortune and trades tellers with a friend.

Variations

Easier: Teacher folds along with the group, one crease at a time.

Harder: Theme it: fortunes about the school year, or quiz questions inside.

⚠️ Safety

Paper edges can give paper cuts — fold on the table, not in the air.
