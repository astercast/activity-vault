🧘 Quiet & Focus

Calm Breathing Shapes

Trace shapes in the air while breathing in rhythm — a reset button for loud afternoons.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–40 kids
Time
5–15 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Everyone finds a comfortable seat. Dim the lights if you can. This works great as a transition after high-energy play.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Trace a square in the air with your finger: breathe IN while tracing up one side (4 counts).
  2. Hold for 4 counts while tracing across the top.
  3. Breathe OUT for 4 counts tracing down the other side. Hold 4 across the bottom.
  4. Repeat for 5 rounds — slower each time.
  5. End with one big stretch and a quiet moment. Notice how the room feels different.

Variations

Easier: Just do the in-and-out tracing without counting for young kids.

Harder: Try triangle breathing (3 counts) or star tracing with 5 points.

⚠️ Safety

This is a calm-down routine, not a medical exercise — keep it gentle and stop if anyone feels dizzy.
