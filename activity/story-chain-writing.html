🧘 Quiet & Focus

Story Chain Writing

Start a story, pass it on, and watch it take a left turn.

Ages
8–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Everyone gets paper and a pencil. Agree on a starting sentence together.

Materials

  • paper and pencils
  • a timer

Steps

  1. Write the starting sentence at the top of your page. Set a timer for 2 minutes — write!
  2. When time is up, pass your paper to the left.
  3. Read what you received, then continue the story for 2 more minutes.
  4. Repeat 4–5 rounds until papers come back around.
  5. Read your (now wild) story aloud in small groups.

Variations

Easier: Pass every 3 minutes and allow drawing instead of writing.

Harder: Each round must end mid-sentence — the next writer has to finish the thought.
