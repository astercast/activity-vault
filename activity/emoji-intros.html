Emoji Introductions

Pick three emojis that describe you, draw them big, and explain your choices — instant personality snapshots.

Ages
6–12
Group
4–25 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Print an emoji menu or draw one on the board; hand out cards.

Materials

  • paper cards
  • markers
  • printed emoji sheet

Steps

  1. Hand out a sheet of 20 printed emojis (or draw a big emoji menu on the board).
  2. Each person picks 3 emojis that describe them and draws them big on a card with their name.
  3. Go around: hold up your card and explain your picks. 'I picked the rocket because I want to be an engineer, the pizza because obvious reasons.'
  4. Hang the cards as a 'meet the group' wall for the first weeks.
  5. Revisit in a month: would you pick the same three? What changed?

Variations

Easier: Pick 2 emojis instead of 3.

Harder: Explain your picks as a 30-second story.

⚠️ Safety

None.
