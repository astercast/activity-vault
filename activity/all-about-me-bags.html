All-About-Me Bags

Decorate a bag, fill it with 3–5 items that represent you, and present your story to the group.

Ages
5–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Send bags home 3 days early with instructions.

Materials

  • paper bags
  • markers
  • stickers
  • items from home

Steps

  1. Send paper bags home (or decorate in session): each child decorates the outside with their name and drawings.
  2. Inside the bag: 3–5 small items that represent them — a photo, a toy, a ticket stub, a rock from a trip.
  3. One by one, kids present their bags, pulling items out and telling the story of each.
  4. The group asks one question per presenter. Model curious questions first: 'Where did you find that?' not 'Cool.'
  5. Display the bags on a shelf for the first month so new friendships have conversation starters.

Variations

Easier: Decorate and present in the same session with classroom items.

Harder: Add a 'mystery item' the group must guess the story behind.

⚠️ Safety

No valuable or irreplaceable items from home.
