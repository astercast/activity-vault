Card-Making Station

Run a permanent card-making station with weekly missions — thank-yous, birthdays, welcomes — delivered every Friday.

Ages
5–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Stock the station with cardstock, markers, stickers, and stamps; leave it running.

Materials

  • folded cardstock or blank cards
  • markers
  • stickers
  • stamps and ink pads
  • envelopes

Steps

  1. Set up a card station with folded cardstock, markers, stickers, and stamps — leave it running as an ongoing center.
  2. Teach the formula for a great card: a picture on the front, a specific message inside, and a decorated envelope.
  3. Pick a weekly mission: thank-you cards for school staff, birthday cards for classmates, welcome cards for new students.
  4. Collect finished cards in a 'mailbox' and deliver them every Friday.
  5. Track who you've thanked — the goal is to reach every adult in the building by year's end.

Variations

Easier: Provide fill-in-the-blank card templates.

Harder: Run themed weeks (gratitude, welcome, congratulations) with a delivery log.

⚠️ Safety

Basic scissors safety.
