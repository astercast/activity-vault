New Year Time Capsule

Seal this year's predictions and open them next year.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Find a sturdy box with a lid. Hand out paper and envelopes so kids can seal their own contributions.

Materials

  • a sturdy box with a lid
  • paper, envelopes, pens
  • tape for sealing

Steps

  1. Each kid writes three predictions for the coming year and seals them in an envelope.
  2. Add one drawing of something from this year worth remembering.
  3. Everyone adds one object — a photo, a ticket stub, a favorite doodle.
  4. Seal the box with tape and write the open date on top.
  5. Stash it somewhere safe. Opening day becomes next year's first activity.

Variations

Easier: Draw instead of write — three pictures of the year ahead.

Harder: Write a letter to your future self with advice for next year.

⚠️ Safety

Nothing sharp or perishable goes in the capsule — paper and drawings only.
