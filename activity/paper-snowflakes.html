Paper Snowflakes

Fold, snip, unfold — no two alike, just like the real thing.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–40 kids
Time
15–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

A winter classic. Have scissors ready and demonstrate the fold once before kids cut.

Materials

  • white paper (copy paper or coffee filters for variety)
  • scissors

Steps

  1. Fold a square of paper in half diagonally to make a triangle, then fold into thirds to make a narrow wedge.
  2. Cut small triangles and curves out of the folded edges — the cut edges become the pattern.
  3. Unfold slowly and reveal the snowflake. Tape it to the window or a string for display.
  4. Try a second round with coffee filters — they fold easier and make delicate flakes.
  5. Hang the best ones as a group winter display.

Variations

Easier: Pre-fold the wedges for younger kids so they only do the cutting.

Harder: Challenge kids to cut their initials or a recognizable shape into the design.

⚠️ Safety

Scissors stay at the table — no walking around with open blades.
