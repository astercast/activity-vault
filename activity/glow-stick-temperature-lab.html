Glow Stick Temperature Lab

Same glow sticks, three cups of water - temperature changes the light.

Ages
7–14
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Fill the three cups: ice water, room-temp water, warm tap water. Label them COLD, ROOM, WARM. Crack each glow stick to activate right before the test.

Materials

  • glow sticks (one per group)
  • three clear cups
  • ice water
  • room-temperature water
  • warm (not hot) water

Steps

  1. Drop one activated glow stick in each cup at the same time.
  2. Dim the lights and compare: which cup glows brightest right now?
  3. Wait 5 minutes and compare again - did the order change?
  4. Feel the cups: the warm cup's stick fades fastest. Heat speeds the chemical reaction up - and uses it up faster.
  5. Write the rule in your own words: warmer = brighter but shorter.

Variations

Easier: Teacher runs the demo up front while kids call out observations.

Harder: Add a fourth cup - freezer-cold vs. fridge-cold - and time the fade with a stopwatch.

⚠️ Safety

Don't cut glow sticks open; the liquid inside stays inside. Cracked or leaking sticks go straight in the trash - wash hands after handling.
