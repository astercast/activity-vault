Pictionary

Draw it fast, guess it faster — no words allowed.

Ages
6–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
15–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Split into two teams. One draws at a time while their team guesses.

Materials

  • whiteboard or large paper pad
  • markers
  • prompt slips
  • timer

Steps

  1. The drawer picks a prompt slip and has 60 seconds to draw it — no letters, no numbers, no talking.
  2. Their team shouts guesses. Correct guess scores a point.
  3. Switch drawers each round so everyone draws.
  4. Allow the other team to steal if time runs out — keeps everyone watching.
  5. Championship round: the drawer uses their non-dominant hand.

Variations

Easier: Show the category (animal, food, action) before drawing starts.

Harder: Draw with eyes closed for the final round.
