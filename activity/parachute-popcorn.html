Parachute Popcorn

Shake the big parachute until the foam balls pop like popcorn.

Ages
5–10
Group
8–30 kids
Time
10–25 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Spread the parachute on the ground. Everyone grabs an edge and stands. Toss the foam balls into the middle.

Materials

  • playground parachute
  • foam balls or beanbags (the 'popcorn')

Steps

  1. Everyone lifts and lowers the parachute together to make gentle waves.
  2. On the count of three, shake hard and fast — the balls pop like popcorn.
  3. Challenge: keep ALL the popcorn bouncing for 30 seconds without any falling off.
  4. Popcorn storm: everyone lets go on '3' and watches the parachute float down.
  5. Cool-down: sit under the parachute tent it makes and tell one story.

Variations

Easier: Use beach balls — big, slow, and easy to keep in.

Harder: Assign each color ball a point value and score the popcorn that stays in.

⚠️ Safety

Keep the parachute low when shaking hard — nobody goes under it while popcorn is bouncing.
