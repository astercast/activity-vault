Mad Libs Live

Fill in the blanks blind, then hear the ridiculous story you built.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–40 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Print a few mad-libs templates, or write your own around a theme (a field trip, a holiday, the group itself).

Materials

  • printed mad-libs stories (or write your own templates)
  • pencils

Steps

  1. Ask the group for words WITHOUT showing the story: 'Give me a silly noun! An adjective! A verb ending in -ing!'
  2. Fill in the blanks as the words come in — the sillier, the better.
  3. Read the finished story aloud with dramatic flair.
  4. Let kids pair up and run their own mad libs for each other.
  5. Collect the funniest ones for a group laugh-book.

Variations

Easier: Do one big group mad lib on the whiteboard.

Harder: Kids write their own mad-libs templates for a partner to fill in.
