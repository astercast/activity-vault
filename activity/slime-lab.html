Slime Chemistry Lab

Mix the classic polymer slime, then run real experiments: what does water do? Cornstarch? Record the results.

Ages
6–12
Group
1–20 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pre-measure glue and baking soda; keep solution at a central station.

Materials

  • school glue
  • baking soda
  • contact lens solution (with boric acid)
  • food coloring
  • glitter
  • bowls
  • spoons

Steps

  1. Pour 1/2 cup of clear or white glue into a bowl and stir in 1/2 teaspoon of baking soda.
  2. Add food coloring and glitter if you want — this is the customization phase.
  3. Add 1 tablespoon of contact lens solution and stir. Keep stirring until it pulls away from the bowl.
  4. Knead with your hands for 2 minutes. Sticky? Add solution a few drops at a time.
  5. Experiment round: split your slime and test add-ins — a spoonful of water (stretchier), cornstarch (stiffer). Record what changes.
  6. Cleanup rule: slime stays at the table, hands get washed, clothes get checked.

Variations

Easier: Leader mixes to the kneading stage; kids customize with color and glitter.

Harder: Test three recipes and rank them on stretchiness with measurements.

⚠️ Safety

Check solution labels for boric acid/sodium borate; wash hands after; keep away from mouths.
