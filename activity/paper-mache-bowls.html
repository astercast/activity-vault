Paper Mache Bowls

Newspaper, paste, balloon — a bowl that hardens overnight.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
30–60 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Mix paste ahead of time (roughly 1 part flour to 2 parts water, stir out lumps). Tear newspaper into strips. This is a two-day project: build day one, paint day two.

Materials

  • balloons (one per kid)
  • newspaper torn into strips
  • flour + water paste (or liquid starch)
  • bowls for paste
  • paint for decorating (day 2)

Steps

  1. Blow up a balloon and set it knot-side down in a cup so it sits steady.
  2. Dip newspaper strips in paste, wipe off the excess, and layer them over the top half of the balloon — 3 layers.
  3. Set the bowls aside to dry overnight (they need a full day).
  4. Day two: pop the balloon and lift out the rubber. Trim the rim if needed.
  5. Paint and decorate the hardened bowl.

Variations

Easier: Use only 2 layers and keep bowls small so they dry faster.

Harder: Build the bowl around the whole balloon, pop it, and cut a door to make a little house.

⚠️ Safety

Paste is non-toxic but messy — smocks on, and wash hands before the snack table.
