Community Helper Interviews

Interview a real community helper, publish the profile.

Ages
8–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
30–60 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Invite a community helper (custodian, crossing guard, nurse, librarian) or pick a staff member everyone knows. Prepare questions as a class.

Materials

  • paper and pencils
  • a list of interview questions (made together)

Steps

  1. Brainstorm interview questions together: pick the ten best.
  2. Assign roles: two interviewers, one note-taker, one photographer, the rest are the audience.
  3. Welcome the guest and run the interview — 15 minutes max.
  4. Thank the guest with a round of applause and a handmade card.
  5. Write-up: the group turns the notes into a one-page profile for the wall.

Variations

Easier: Interview a staff member the kids already know well.

Harder: Kids run the whole interview with zero teacher help.

⚠️ Safety

Interviews happen with the helper visiting the group — kids do not go off alone to find people.
