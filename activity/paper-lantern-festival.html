Paper Lantern Festival

Fold, snip, and roll paper into glowing lanterns — battery lights only.

Ages
8–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Demo the fold-and-cut once. Have tape ready; glue is slower for this build.

Materials

  • colored paper
  • scissors
  • tape or glue
  • battery tea lights (real candles never)

Steps

  1. Fold a sheet of colored paper in half lengthwise.
  2. Cut slits from the folded edge toward the open edge, stopping about an inch short.
  3. Unfold, roll the paper into a tube, and tape the short ends together.
  4. Tape a paper handle strip across the top.
  5. Drop in a battery tea light, dim the room lights, and enjoy the glow.

Variations

Easier: Use pre-cut slit templates for younger kids.

Harder: Layer two colors — an outer cut layer over a solid inner tube.

⚠️ Safety

Battery tea lights only — never real candles near paper.
