Paper Plate Sunflower

A paper plate, some yellow paint, and a seed-mosaic center, instant sunflower.

Before you begin

Ages
4–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
No prep needed

Setup

Hand out one paper plate per kid and put the yellow paint or crayons in the middle of each table.

Materials

  • paper plates (one per kid)
  • yellow paint or crayons
  • brown tissue paper or real sunflower seeds (for gluing)
  • green paper strips
  • glue sticks

Steps

  1. Color the whole plate yellow. Paint is brightest, crayons work too.
  2. Cut slits all around the plate edge (about an inch deep) and bend every other flap slightly down: those are the petals.
  3. Glue brown tissue scraps or real seeds in a circle in the middle for the flower head.
  4. Glue a green paper strip to the back as the stem.
  5. Hold them up together, a field of sunflowers, zero watering needed.

Variations

Easier: Use crayons instead of paint for zero drying time.

Harder: Two-tone challenge: paint the petal tips a darker yellow-orange gradient.
