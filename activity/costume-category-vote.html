Costume Category Vote

Everyone wins something when the categories are this creative.

Ages
6–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
20–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Announce fun categories ahead of time: Most Creative, Funniest, Spookiest (Not Scary), Best Duo, Best Homemade. Set out ballots.

Materials

  • paper ballots and pencils
  • a ballot box
  • paper medals or ribbons (optional)

Steps

  1. Give everyone 5 minutes to look at all the costumes up close.
  2. Everyone votes in each category — no voting for yourself.
  3. Two volunteers tally the votes while the class does a costume parade.
  4. Announce winners with a drumroll. Hand out paper medals.
  5. Close by having every kid name one costume (not their own) they admired — everyone gets recognized.

Variations

Easier: Vote on just two categories to keep it short.

Harder: Let kids invent and campaign for their own new categories.

⚠️ Safety

Categories stay kind — celebrate creativity and effort, never single anyone out.
