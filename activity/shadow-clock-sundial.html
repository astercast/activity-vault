Shadow Clock Sundial

A stick, some chalk, and the sun — the oldest clock there is.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–45 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Find a flat sunny spot. Push the stick upright into the ground or a mound of dirt.

Materials

  • a straight stick about 2 feet tall
  • chalk or small rocks
  • a clock or watch for calibration

Steps

  1. On the hour, mark where the stick's shadow falls with chalk or a rock.
  2. Repeat every hour — or every half hour for more detail.
  3. Label each mark with the time. You now have a working sundial.
  4. Next day, test it: can the class tell the time from shadows alone?
  5. Discuss: why do the marks bunch up at midday and stretch in the morning?

Variations

Easier: Mark shadows every hour for just three hours and extrapolate the rest.

Harder: Build a mini sundial on a paper plate to take home.

⚠️ Safety

Sunny-day activity — bring water and hats if it is hot out.
