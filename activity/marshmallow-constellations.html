Marshmallow Constellations

Build the Big Dipper out of marshmallows and toothpicks — then invent your own.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Print or draw a few famous constellations (Big Dipper, Orion, Cassiopeia) as dot patterns on cards. Put marshmallows and toothpicks in bowls on tables.

Materials

  • mini marshmallows
  • toothpicks
  • printed constellation cards or a simple star chart

Steps

  1. Show the group how a constellation is just stars connected by imaginary lines — like dot-to-dot in the sky.
  2. Each kid picks a constellation card and rebuilds it: a marshmallow for every star, toothpicks for the lines.
  3. Once the real ones are built, flip the card over: invent a brand-new constellation and name it.
  4. Kids present their invented constellations and the story behind the shape.
  5. End with a gallery walk — everyone tours the table and votes for most creative.

Variations

Easier: Use pretzel sticks instead of toothpicks for younger kids.

Harder: Build to scale: make one constellation's stars bigger to show brighter vs. dimmer stars.

⚠️ Safety

Toothpicks are sharp — no sword fights, and supervise younger builders closely.
