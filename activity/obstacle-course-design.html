Design-Your-Own Obstacle Course

Teams build the course, then everyone races through all of them.

Before you begin

Ages
7–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
30–60 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Lay out all available equipment. Teams design, then everyone runs every course.

Materials

  • cones, hula hoops, jump ropes, pool noodles. Whatever is on hand
  • timer or stopwatch

Steps

  1. Split into teams of 4–5. Each team gets the same pile of equipment.
  2. Teams have 15 minutes to design a 6-station obstacle course: crawl, jump, balance, throw, run, spin.
  3. Each team demos its course while the others watch and cheer.
  4. Everyone runs every course, timed. Fastest total time wins, style points for the best-designed station.
  5. Vote on awards: toughest, silliest, most creative.

Variations

Easier: Teacher designs one course and everyone runs it. Kids just add one station each.

Harder: Add a rule: the course must include one teamwork station (carry a partner's shoe, three-legged section).

Safety

Check every station for stability before running. No climbing on anything not meant for climbing.
