Cotton-Ball Penguin

A fluffy cotton-ball penguin puppet with real googly attitude.

Ages
4–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pre-cut the black body ovals and white belly ovals. Put cotton balls, eyes, and beaks in shared bowls.

Materials

  • black and white paper ovals (one set per kid)
  • cotton balls
  • glue
  • googly eyes
  • orange paper beaks

Steps

  1. Glue the white belly oval onto the black body oval.
  2. Glue cotton balls all around the edge for a fluffy outline.
  3. Add googly eyes and the orange beak.
  4. Give your penguin a name and introduce it to a neighbor's penguin.

Variations

Easier: Teacher glues the body together; kids stick on cotton balls and eyes.

Harder: Add flippers (small black paper wings) and a paper ice floe to stand on.

⚠️ Safety

Googly eyes are small — keep them out of mouths; supervision for under-5s.
