Costume Parade Freeze

Parade your costume while the music plays — freeze in your best pose when it stops.

Ages
4–10
Group
4–30 kids
Time
20–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Clear a parade loop around the room. Line kids up in their costumes.

Materials

  • a music player
  • room to parade in a circle

Steps

  1. Start the music — everyone parades the loop, showing off their costume.
  2. When the music stops, FREEZE in your best costume pose.
  3. The audience (everyone still frozen counts too) tries to guess each costume. Unfreeze one at a time to take a bow.
  4. Music back on — parade again. Repeat 4–5 rounds.
  5. End with a group freeze: the whole class holds one giant tableau pose.

Variations

Easier: Skip the guessing — just parade and freeze.

Harder: Add categories: freeze like your character would move, or pair up for duo poses.

⚠️ Safety

Costumes need to let kids see and move safely — no vision-blocking masks while parading, and no real or toy weapons, ever.
