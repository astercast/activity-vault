Sidewalk Chalk Murals

Claim sidewalk squares for individual masterpieces or one giant collaborative mural — then photograph it before the rain does.

Ages
4–12
Group
1–30 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Assign sidewalk squares; hand out bulk chalk.

Materials

  • sidewalk chalk (lots)
  • spray bottles with water (optional)
  • camera or phone

Steps

  1. Give each child or team a sidewalk square and a box of chalk. Theme it or go free: 'draw your dream playground,' 'chalk the seasons.'
  2. For big groups, assign a giant collaborative piece: each square is one panel of a huge rainbow, dragon, or story scene.
  3. Outline first in white chalk, then fill with color — it looks dramatically better.
  4. Mist finished art lightly with water from a spray bottle to make colors pop (optional).
  5. Photo-document everything. Chalk art is gloriously temporary — the photos are the keepsake.

Variations

Easier: Free draw with no theme.

Harder: Each square must connect to its neighbors as one continuous mural.

⚠️ Safety

Chalk dust — avoid on windy days near faces; wash hands after.
