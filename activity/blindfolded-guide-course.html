Blindfolded Guide Course

One partner blindfolded, one partner guiding — with words only.

Ages
7–12
Group
6–25 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set up a simple obstacle course with cones or chairs. Pair kids up: one blindfolded walker, one sighted guide.

Materials

  • blindfolds or bandanas
  • cones or chairs for obstacles
  • a small prize or token at the finish

Steps

  1. Walk the course together first with eyes open so walkers know the layout.
  2. Blindfold the walkers. Guides may ONLY use words — no touching.
  3. Guides talk their partner through: 'three small steps forward, now turn left.'
  4. Swap roles and run it again.
  5. Debrief: what instructions worked best? Specific and calm beat loud and vague.

Variations

Easier: Let guides hold their partner's elbow while giving directions.

Harder: Guides must stay at the finish line and direct from a distance.

⚠️ Safety

Clear the course of anything hard or sharp. A spotter walks alongside each pair. Slow walking only — no running blindfolded.
