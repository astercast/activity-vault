Origami Jumping Frogs

Fold a paper frog that actually hops — then hold a frog-jump race.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Pre-cut paper into squares if using copy paper. Practice the fold sequence once yourself so you can demo smoothly.

Materials

  • square paper (origami paper or cut copy paper)
  • markers for decorating

Steps

  1. Fold the square in half both ways and unfold, leaving crease lines.
  2. Collapse the top into a triangle (the classic waterbomb base) — demo this slowly, it is the tricky part.
  3. Fold the triangle's corners up to make the front legs, then fold the bottom up twice to form the springy back.
  4. Press down on the frog's back and release — it jumps.
  5. Decorate with eyes and spots, then race frogs across a table finish line.

Variations

Easier: Use larger paper — big folds are easier for small hands to manage.

Harder: Experiment: does a tighter spring fold make it jump farther? Test and compare.
