Storytelling Dice

Roll picture dice for a character, a setting, and a problem — then invent and tell a story connecting all three.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–20 kids
Time
25–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Make dice or picture card sets.

Materials

  • wooden blocks or dice
  • printed picture stickers or cards
  • tape
  • paper
  • pencils

Steps

  1. Make story dice: tape printed pictures onto wooden blocks, or just use 6 picture cards drawn from a hat.
  2. Roll 3 dice (or draw 3 cards): a character, a setting, and a problem. 'A pirate... in a library... who lost their voice.'
  3. Each child gets 2 minutes to invent a story connecting all three — then tells it, or writes it.
  4. In pairs, combine your dice: now the story needs all 6 elements.
  5. Best stories get illustrated and pinned up. The dice never repeat, so this game never gets old.

Variations

Easier: Roll 2 dice instead of 3.

Harder: Stories must include all 6 dice and a plot twist.

⚠️ Safety

None.
