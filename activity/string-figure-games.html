String Figure Games

A loop of string, ten fingers, endless figures.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pre-tie string loops (about 5 feet of string per loop). Hand one to each kid.

Materials

  • looped string (one per kid, about 5 feet tied in a loop)

Steps

  1. Teach the opening: loop over thumbs and pinkies, pick up the palm strings.
  2. Learn the cup-and-saucer figure step by step, slowly.
  3. Pair up: one kid makes a figure, the partner 'reads' what it looks like.
  4. Learn one transfer trick — passing the figure to a partner without dropping it.
  5. Figure gallery: everyone shows their best figure at once.

Variations

Easier: Learn just the opening position and invent your own figures.

Harder: Chain figures: transfer a figure through three partners in a row.

⚠️ Safety

String stays on hands — never around necks, and cut tangles instead of yanking.
