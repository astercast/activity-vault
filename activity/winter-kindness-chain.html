Kindness Paper Chain Countdown

Each link holds a secret act of kindness. Tear one off daily and watch the chain — and the kindness — shrink toward the holiday.

Ages
4–12
Group
1–30 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cut paper strips in two colors; set out markers and glue.

Materials

  • red and green (or any) paper strips
  • markers
  • glue sticks or tape

Steps

  1. Brainstorm kind acts as a group — small, specific, doable: 'teach someone a game,' 'thank the custodian by name,' 'invite someone new to sit with you.'
  2. Each child writes one kind act on 2–3 paper strips.
  3. Loop strips into links, alternating colors, to form one long chain. Count the links — that's your countdown.
  4. Hang the chain where everyone sees it. Each day, remove one link and the whole group does that act.
  5. Keep a shared log of completed acts; read it aloud on the last day.

Variations

Easier: Leader writes the acts; kids just assemble the chain.

Harder: Kids invent acts, then track completions in a shared log with reflections.

⚠️ Safety

Keep acts school-appropriate and safe.
