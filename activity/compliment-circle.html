🤝 Team & Community👋 Icebreakers

Compliment Circle

Sit in a circle and give every single person specific, genuine compliments — the simplest community builder there is.

Ages
5–12
Group
5–30 kids
Time
15–15 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Arrange chairs in a circle; no materials needed.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Sit in a circle. Explain the rules: compliments must be specific, true, and kind — about actions, not just appearance.
  2. Start with a volunteer in the middle (or a stuffed animal passed around as the 'compliment target').
  3. Go around the circle: each person gives the target one genuine compliment. 'You're great at explaining rules' beats 'you're nice.'
  4. Rotate the target until everyone has had a turn — with big groups, do it over several days.
  5. Close: each person names one compliment they received that surprised them.

Variations

Easier: Use a stuffed animal as the target instead of a person.

Harder: Each compliment must name a specific action from this week.

⚠️ Safety

Set the norm: kind and true only — shut down joke insults immediately.
