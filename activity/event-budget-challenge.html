Event Budget Challenge

Two hundred dollars, one party, zero excuses, plan the whole event on paper and defend every dollar.

Before you begin

Ages
12–14
Group
4–24 kids
Time
35–60 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set the event: an end-of-term party for 30. The budget is $200. Write it big on the board.

Materials

  • paper
  • pencils
  • calculators (optional)

Steps

  1. List everything the party needs: food, drinks, decorations, music, games. No idea is too small to price.
  2. Price it with real numbers. Look up actual store prices if you have a device, estimate honestly if not.
  3. Build the budget sheet. If you're over $200, cut, and every cut needs a reason the party still works.
  4. The twist: cut 20% more. What's the first thing to go? What's untouchable? Defend both.
  5. Each team pitches their final budget in 2 minutes. The room votes: best party per dollar wins.

Variations

Easier: Raise the budget to $500, or plan for 10 guests instead of 30.

Harder: Add a surprise mid-plan (it rains. Move it indoors, reprice), or require a donation/sponsor line.
