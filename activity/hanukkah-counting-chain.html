Hanukkah Counting Chain

A paper chain of eight links plus a leader — a craft for learning how Hanukkah counts its eight nights.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cut paper into strips (about 3 × 20 cm), eight of one color and one of another for the leader link. Frame the activity as learning about a holiday other families celebrate — this is a counting craft, not a ceremony.

Materials

  • blue and white paper strips
  • glue sticks or tape
  • a marker

Steps

  1. Chain the eight links together, one for each night of Hanukkah.
  2. Add the leader link at the front of the chain.
  3. On each link, write something the group has learned about the holiday.
  4. Hang the chain and add one fact per day as a class countdown.

Variations

Easier: Teacher assembles the chain; kids decorate the links with drawings.

Harder: Research one more true fact per night from a reliable source and add it.

⚠️ Safety

Cultural note: this craft is for learning about Hanukkah, not performing it. Treat the holiday with respect — ask, don't assume, about anyone's traditions.
