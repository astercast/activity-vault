Appreciation Postcards

One specific thank-you per kid, delivered like mail.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Hand out one postcard per kid. Everyone draws a name — or the teacher assigns so nobody is missed.

Materials

  • blank postcards or half-sheets of cardstock
  • pens and markers

Steps

  1. Write one specific appreciation: something the person did, not just 'you are nice.'
  2. Decorate the front of the card to match the message.
  3. Drop cards in a mailbox box at the front of the room.
  4. Deliver: read your card privately first, then the group does a thank-you round.
  5. Pin favorites on the wall for the week.

Variations

Easier: Sentence starters on the board: 'I noticed you...'

Harder: Write the card in the voice of a newspaper headline about that person.

⚠️ Safety

Keep notes kind and specific — no backhanded compliments, and nobody has to read theirs aloud.
