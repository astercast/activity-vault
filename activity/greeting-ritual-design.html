Greeting Ritual Design

Invent the class handshake — then use it every morning.

Ages
5–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

No materials. The group will invent its own way of saying hello.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Brainstorm: how do teams greet each other — handshakes, waves, secret signals?
  2. Each small group invents a 5-second greeting ritual with a name.
  3. Groups perform their greeting for the class.
  4. Vote on one to adopt as the official class greeting for the month.
  5. Practice it at the door tomorrow morning.

Variations

Easier: Pick from three teacher-suggested greetings instead of inventing.

Harder: Add a Friday-only fancy version.

⚠️ Safety

Greetings are always optional — a wave or a nod counts, and nobody has to touch.
