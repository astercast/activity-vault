Apple Print Painting

Halved apples become stamps — print rows of apple patterns.

Ages
4–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

An adult halves the apples top-to-bottom (the star-shaped core shows) and crosswise (the round center shows). Pour paint onto plates.

Materials

  • apples (a few)
  • washable paint in fall colors
  • paper plates for paint
  • big paper or cardstock
  • smocks or old shirts
  • paper towels

Steps

  1. Look at the cut apple — spot the star in the middle.
  2. Press the cut side into the paint, then stamp it firmly onto the paper. Lift straight up.
  3. Make patterns: rows of stars, alternating colors, a border frame.
  4. Try stamping with the crosswise half for a different shape.
  5. Sign your print and let it dry flat.

Variations

Easier: Stamp with one color only and make a simple row pattern.

Harder: Carve a simple shape into the apple half with a plastic knife (adult supervises) for a custom stamp.

⚠️ Safety

Adults do all the apple cutting. Smocks on — paint washes out of hands, not always out of shirts.
