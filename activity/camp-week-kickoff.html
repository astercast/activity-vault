Camp Week Kickoff

The opening assembly that turns 30 strangers into one camp: cheer, map, first mission.

Ages 5–12 · 30–50 min · 4 materials

Ages
5–12
Group
10–60 kids
Time
30–50 min
Where
Outdoors & Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Draw the week-map: five big boxes labeled with each day's theme. Set chairs in a wide circle. Have name tags ready.

Materials

  • a big paper week-map or whiteboard
  • markers
  • a camp cheer (made up on the spot)
  • name tags and markers

Steps

  1. Everyone writes a name tag with their name plus one summer thing they love.
  2. Name game lightning round: each kid says their name with a move; the whole circle repeats it.
  3. Invent the camp cheer together, three words, one stomp, one clap. Practice it twice.
  4. Unveil the week-map: walk through each day's headline so the week has a shape.
  5. First mission: mixed teams get one 15-minute challenge (chalk the camp motto on the walkway) and report back.
  6. Close with the cheer. Camp has officially started.

Variations

Easier: Shorter circle, teacher-led cheer, and the mission is a group drawing instead of a challenge.

Harder: Legacy round: each week adds one box to a season-long map, by August the wall tells the whole summer.

⚠️ Safety

Keep the circle in shade if outdoors. Opening assemblies run long and kids wilt. Water within reach.
