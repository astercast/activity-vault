Crystal Gardens

Grow sparkling crystals on pipe-cleaner shapes in supersaturated solution — then sketch the daily growth like real scientists.

Ages
7–12
Group
1–20 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Adult pre-heats water; set out jars, pipe cleaners, and pencils.

Materials

  • borax
  • hot water
  • glass jars
  • pipe cleaners
  • pencils
  • food coloring
  • spoons

Steps

  1. Heat water until steaming (adult job) and stir in borax one spoonful at a time until no more dissolves — that's a supersaturated solution.
  2. Pour into jars and add food coloring if you want tinted crystals.
  3. Twist a pipe cleaner into a fun shape (star, snowflake, spiral) and hang it from a pencil across the jar's mouth, fully submerged.
  4. Set jars somewhere they won't be bumped. Check daily and sketch the growth.
  5. In 3–5 days, pull out crystal-covered shapes. Discuss: the cooling water can't hold all the dissolved borax, so it rebuilds into crystals.

Variations

Easier: Leader mixes the solution; kids shape and hang pipe cleaners.

Harder: Grow two jars (more vs. less borax) and compare crystal size daily.

⚠️ Safety

Borax is not for tasting; adult handles hot water; wash hands after.
