Halloween Shadow Puppet Show

Cut spooky silhouettes, mount them on sticks, and perform a shadow play behind a sheet with flashlights.

Ages
5–12
Group
4–25 kids
Time
45–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Hang the sheet screen, set out cardstock and sticks, test flashlights.

Materials

  • black cardstock
  • scissors
  • tape
  • wooden skewers or straws
  • white sheet
  • flashlights
  • chairs or a clothesline

Steps

  1. Hang the white sheet as a screen and set chairs in front — performers work behind it with flashlights.
  2. Each small group designs 3–4 spooky characters: bats, ghosts, witches, haunted houses. Draw on black cardstock, cut out, and tape to sticks.
  3. Groups get 10 minutes to invent a 2-minute spooky (not scary) story with a beginning, middle, and twist ending.
  4. Dim the lights. One group performs at a time: hold puppets between the flashlight and the sheet, close to the sheet for sharp shadows.
  5. Add a narrator and sound effects from the 'audience' side for full theater effect.

Variations

Easier: Use pre-cut puppet shapes; kids just assemble and perform.

Harder: Write a full script with dialogue and rehearse with lighting cues.

⚠️ Safety

Scissors safety; keep flashlights away from eyes.
