Escape Room Build

Design the locks, hide the clues, then watch another team break out.

Before you begin

Ages
11–14
Group
6–30 kids
Time
45–90 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Split the group into 2-3 build teams. Each team gets a corner of the room and the prop box.

Materials

  • paper
  • markers
  • tape
  • envelopes
  • any classroom props
  • combination locks (if available)

Steps

  1. Each team invents a story: the room is 'locked' and the other team must earn the way out.
  2. Design 4-5 puzzles in a chain. Each solved puzzle gives the clue to the next. Write the clue trail down.
  3. Build it: hide clues, set 'locks' (taped-shut boxes, guarded envelopes, combination locks if you have them).
  4. Playtest with your own team first, if you can't solve it, nobody can. Fix the unfair puzzles.
  5. Swap rooms: each team attempts another team's escape room while the builders watch silently and take notes.

Variations

Easier: Build 3 puzzles instead of 5, or give every team the same starter puzzle.

Harder: Add a red-herring clue, or require one puzzle that needs two people solving together.

Safety

No locking anyone in a real room. 'Locked' means a taped box or a guarded door, never a real lock.
