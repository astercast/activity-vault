Homemade Christmas Ornaments

Classic kid-made ornaments — keep some for your tree, gift the rest.

Ages
4–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set up a decorating station with one ornament blank per kid and the decorations in shared bowls. Loop a ribbon hanger through each ornament first.

Materials

  • clear plastic ornament balls or cardstock circles (one per kid)
  • glitter, sequins, and stickers
  • paint pens or markers
  • ribbon or yarn

Steps

  1. Each kid picks an ornament blank and decorates it any way they like — glitter, stickers, painted dots, initials.
  2. Let paint dry flat on a tray so it doesn't smudge.
  3. Tie on the ribbon hanger and do a 'gallery walk' of everyone's tree.
  4. Kids wrap one ornament to gift to someone and keep one for the classroom or family tree.

Variations

Easier: Pre-fill ornament balls with glitter so the littlest ones just shake and admire.

Harder: Try salt-dough cutouts (baked ahead) as the ornament base for a two-day project.

⚠️ Safety

Glitter travels — cover the table and keep it out of eyes and mouths.
