Paper Gingerbread Houses

Design and decorate paper gingerbread houses with zero baking — all the decorating joy, none of the crumbs.

Ages
5–12
Group
1–25 kids
Time
45–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pre-cut house shapes; set out decorating stations with shared supplies.

Materials

  • brown construction paper
  • white paper
  • scissors
  • glue sticks
  • markers
  • cotton balls
  • stickers
  • sequins

Steps

  1. Pre-cut simple house shapes from brown paper (square + triangle roof), or let older kids design their own.
  2. Glue the house onto white paper as a snowy background.
  3. Decorate with 'icing' — white paint or glue lines along the roof edges — then add gumdrop stickers, cotton-ball snow, and sequin candies.
  4. Draw a door, windows, and a chimney; add a marshmallow snowman or candy-cane fence beside the house.
  5. Display all houses together as a 'gingerbread village' on the wall.

Variations

Easier: Use pre-assembled house bases; just decorate.

Harder: Design a whole gingerbread street with connected houses and a story.

⚠️ Safety

Scissors safety for younger kids; pre-cut for ages 5–6.
