Freeze Dance

Dance while the music plays, freeze like a statue when it stops.

Ages
5–12
Group
6–40 kids
Time
10–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Push furniture to the walls. One person controls the music.

Materials

  • music player and speaker

Steps

  1. Play the music — everyone dances however they want.
  2. Pause the music without warning. Everyone freezes exactly as they are.
  3. Anyone still moving after the freeze is out (or does 5 jumping jacks and rejoins — your call).
  4. Play again. Last dancer standing wins, or just play round after round.
  5. Theme rounds: dance like a robot, like you are underwater, like a statue coming to life.

Variations

Easier: Eliminated players become freeze judges instead of sitting out.

Harder: Freeze in a specific pose the caller names ('freeze like a tree!').

⚠️ Safety

Clear the dance floor of chairs and bags. No roughhousing — dancing only.
