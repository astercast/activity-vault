Jump Rope Countdown

Count, trick, and show off — jump rope as a personal-best sport.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–40 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Lay out the ropes in an open area with room to swing. If using one long rope, pick two steady turners.

Materials

  • jump ropes (a few, or one long rope for group jumping)

Steps

  1. Warm up with 30 seconds of free jumping to find a rhythm.
  2. Challenge one: how many jumps in one minute? Count and record.
  3. Challenge two: trick ladder — 10 normal, 10 high-knee, 10 criss-cross arms, 10 backwards.
  4. Group round: two turners, one long rope, jumpers take turns running in and jumping.
  5. End with a freestyle show-off round — best trick wins the cheer.

Variations

Easier: Let beginners jump without the rope first to learn the timing, or swing the rope slowly.

Harder: Double-unders (rope passes twice per jump) for advanced jumpers.

⚠️ Safety

Give each jumper space — ropes and faces do not mix. Check ropes for knots before starting.
