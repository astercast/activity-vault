Leaf Boat Races

Leaf hulls, twig masts, and a finish line downstream.

Ages
4–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

You need slow-moving shallow water — a stream edge, a rain gutter with the hose trickling, or a long trough.

Materials

  • large leaves or pieces of bark
  • twigs and string for masts and sails
  • access to a stream, gutter, or long water trough

Steps

  1. Each kid picks a big leaf or piece of bark as a hull.
  2. Build a mast from a twig and a sail from a smaller leaf, tied on with string.
  3. Line the boats up at the start line and release them together.
  4. Cheer them downstream. First boat past the finish stick wins the heat.
  5. Run a losers' bracket — redesign and race again.

Variations

Easier: Race plain leaves with no building — pick the fastest floater.

Harder: Weight limit: boats must carry two pebbles and still finish.

⚠️ Safety

Stay back from the water's edge and keep feet dry — no wading in to fetch a stuck boat.
