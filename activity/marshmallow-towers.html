Marshmallow Tower Challenge

Twenty marshmallows, twenty spaghetti sticks, fifteen minutes — build the tallest freestanding tower you can.

Ages
6–12
Group
6–30 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Count out 20 marshmallows and 20 spaghetti sticks per team.

Materials

  • mini marshmallows
  • dry spaghetti
  • rulers or tape measures
  • timer

Steps

  1. Split into teams of 3–4. Each team gets 20 marshmallows and 20 pieces of dry spaghetti.
  2. Challenge: build the tallest freestanding tower in 15 minutes. It must stand alone for 10 seconds to count.
  3. No eating the building materials until judging is done. (This rule will be tested.)
  4. Measure every tower. Winning team explains their design: wide base? triangles? cross-bracing?
  5. Rebuild round: teams get 5 minutes to improve using what they learned from the winners.

Variations

Easier: Allow tape as well as marshmallows for joints.

Harder: Add a weight test: the tower must hold a paperback book.

⚠️ Safety

No eating until judging is done; check dietary needs if shared.
