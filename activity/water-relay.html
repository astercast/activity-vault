Sponge Water Relay

Sponge-soak, sprint, squeeze — teams race to transfer the most water bucket-to-bucket. Everyone gets soaked.

Ages
6–12
Group
8–30 kids
Time
25–25 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set paired buckets per team; mark start lines.

Materials

  • buckets (2 per team)
  • large sponges
  • measuring cups
  • towels

Steps

  1. Set up two buckets per team: one full of water at the start line, one empty at the far end.
  2. First runner soaks a big sponge in the full bucket, sprints to the far end, squeezes it into the empty bucket, sprints back.
  3. Tag the next runner. The sponge is the baton — dropped sponge means going back for it.
  4. After 10 minutes, measure: which team transferred the most water? Pour into measuring cups for a dramatic reveal.
  5. Everyone's soaked, everyone's happy. End with free sponge-toss time.

Variations

Easier: Shorten the distance between buckets.

Harder: Sponges must be carried overhead.

⚠️ Safety

Hot-day activity only; watch for slippery grass.
