👋 Icebreakers

Find-Someone-Who Bingo

Mingle to find classmates who match each bingo square — first to complete a row verifies it aloud.

Ages
6–12
Group
8–30 kids
Time
25–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Print 3–4 different bingo card versions.

Materials

  • printed bingo cards
  • pencils

Steps

  1. Hand out bingo cards with squares like 'has a pet lizard,' 'can whistle,' 'was born in another state,' 'loves broccoli.'
  2. Everyone mingles, asking classmates questions to find matches. One name per square — no repeats.
  3. First to complete a row shouts 'BINGO!' and reads their row aloud to verify.
  4. Keep playing for blackout (whole card) while early winners help others.
  5. Debrief: what was the most surprising thing you learned about someone?

Variations

Easier: Play for one row only.

Harder: Blackout the whole card, and every square needs a follow-up fact.

⚠️ Safety

Walking feet while mingling.
