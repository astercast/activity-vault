Pin the Hat on the Witch

The party classic with a Halloween twist. Blindfold, spin, stick the hat.

Before you begin

Ages
4–9
Group
4–30 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Hang the witch poster at kid height. Put a tape loop on the back of each paper hat.

Materials

  • a large witch face poster (drawn or printed)
  • paper witch hats (one per kid)
  • tape or sticky tack on each hat
  • a soft scarf for a blindfold

Steps

  1. One at a time, a kid stands a few steps back, gets gently blindfolded, and turns around once.
  2. Walk forward slowly (a buddy can give 'left / right / straight' hints) and press the hat onto the poster.
  3. Lift the blindfold. The room decides if the hat landed on the head or somewhere hilarious.
  4. Everyone gets a turn. Closest hat to the head wins the round.
  5. Leave the finished poster up as party decor.

Variations

Easier: Skip the blindfold, just stick the hat from a marked line.

Harder: Add decoy targets: also pin the broom, the cat, and the wart.

Safety

Clear the walking path of furniture and spot younger kids so nobody bumps into anything.
