Density Rainbow Jars

Stack liquids into a rainbow that refuses to mix.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pre-mix the colored water and colored rubbing alcohol in pitchers. Line up the liquids in density order (heaviest first) at a central station. Each kid gets a jar.

Materials

  • clear jars or tall cups
  • honey or corn syrup
  • dish soap
  • water with food coloring
  • vegetable oil
  • rubbing alcohol with food coloring
  • spoons for pouring

Steps

  1. Ask the group to guess what happens if you pour honey, water, and oil into one jar. Take bets.
  2. Pour the liquids in this order, slowly down the side of the jar: honey, dish soap, colored water, oil, colored alcohol.
  3. Watch the layers settle into a rainbow. The densest sinks, the lightest floats.
  4. Let kids drop in small objects (grape, bead, coin, cork) and guess which layer each will stop at.
  5. Try tilting the jar gently — the layers shift but settle back.

Variations

Easier: Use just three liquids (honey, water, oil) for a simple stack.

Harder: Have kids predict each object's resting layer before dropping, then explain any surprises.

⚠️ Safety

Rubbing alcohol is not for drinking — label it clearly and keep it at the teacher station.
