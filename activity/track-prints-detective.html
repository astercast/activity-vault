Track Prints Detective

Find animal prints in snow or mud, match them to a chart, solve the mystery.

Ages
5–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

After fresh snow or rain, scout a route with visible prints. Print one track chart per pair.

Materials

  • a printed animal-track ID chart
  • a camera or phone for photos
  • a notebook

Steps

  1. Walk the route quietly and look for prints — snow and mud both work.
  2. Photograph each set of prints and note where you found them.
  3. Match them against the ID chart: how many toes, claw marks or not, size?
  4. Log the 'case file': what animal, how many, which direction, and the evidence that convinced you.

Variations

Easier: Teacher pre-prints photo cards of three local tracks; kids match at the site.

Harder: Measure stride length between prints and estimate the animal's speed.

⚠️ Safety

Stay on paths; don't follow prints into streets or private yards.
