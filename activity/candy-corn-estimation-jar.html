Candy Corn Estimation Jar

Guess how many are in the jar — then count by tens to find out.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–40 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Fill the jar ahead of time and count the pieces as you fill (write the total down secretly). Set the jar where everyone can see it — no touching.

Materials

  • a clear jar filled with candy corn (or dried beans for an allergy-safe version)
  • small paper slips and pencils

Steps

  1. Everyone studies the jar and writes down a guess on a slip — one guess each, no changing.
  2. Collect the slips. Read a few guesses aloud — marvel at the range.
  3. Pour out the jar and count together in groups of ten. Compare to the guesses.
  4. Closest guess wins — the prize can be the honor of refilling the jar for the next class.
  5. Talk strategy: who guessed by layers? By rows? What would you do differently next time?

Variations

Easier: Use a smaller jar and count by fives.

Harder: Fill two jars with different sizes of candy and estimate which holds more pieces.

⚠️ Safety

Check allergies before any tasting happens — and keep the jar sealed until counting time if your class has food allergies.
