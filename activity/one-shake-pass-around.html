One Shake, Pass It Around

One tambourine for the whole circle. When it reaches you: one shake, a real one, then pass it on. The music is taking turns.

Ages 3–3 · 10–15 min · 1 material

Ages
3–3
Group
4–16 kids
Time
10–15 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Check the tambourine yourself first: shake it hard over your own hand and look, every jingle fixed, no cracks, nothing that a determined three-year-old could work loose. Sit the circle close enough that the tambourine passes hand to hand without anyone leaning across a lap: passing distance is the set-up, and a circle that is too wide makes droppers of everyone.

Materials

  • one big tambourine (sturdy, adult-checked, no loose jingles)

Steps

  1. Start with the tambourine in your own hands and model the whole turn in four beats: receive it in two hands, ONE shake, wrist up, a proper ring, say nothing, pass it two-handed to the next person. State the one rule: 'One shake when it comes to you. Then it travels.'
  2. First round is slow on purpose and that is the music of it: shake, pass, shake, pass, the circle finds the rhythm of the PASSING itself, and you name it when it settles: 'Listen, shake, wait, shake, wait. We are playing the silences too.'
  3. Name round: as the tambourine arrives, the receiver says their name first, then shakes once. Name, ring, pass. A name said into a listening circle and answered by an instrument is a performance a three-year-old can actually hold, and each one gets the circle's whole attention.
  4. Growing round: the LAST kid in the circle earns the double, two shakes to close the song, and the group counts them: 'One... two!' Then the tambourine starts a fresh round the other way around the circle, which at three is a genuinely new journey.
  5. Finish by putting the tambourine to sleep in the middle: the final receiver carries it in two hands to the centre, lays it down flat, and the whole circle puts one finger on their lips over it. An instrument resting is an instrument respected, and a quiet circle of three-year-olds, achieved by music, is worth sitting in for a moment.

Variations

Easier: The adult passes hand-over-hand with each receiver. The shake is still the kid's own, the pass is shared until hands learn it.

Harder: Choice round: the receiver may shake once OR tap the skin once. Choosing your sound is arranging, and the circle hears two instruments in one.

⚠️ Safety

The tambourine is passed, never thrown, and never snatched: a pass that gets grabbed is redone slowly, kindly, because the passing is half the instrument. Jingles are checked before every use. A loose jingle on the floor is picked up by an adult immediately; small metal discs and small hands are a combination this activity simply designs out.
