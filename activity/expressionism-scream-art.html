Big-Feeling Scream Art

Borrow the wavy style of a famous painting to draw your own big feeling.

Ages
8–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
30–50 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Show the famous painting and talk about what you notice: the wavy lines, the bold colors, the huge feeling on the face. The rule: we're borrowing the STYLE, not copying the painting.

Materials

  • paper
  • oil pastels or crayons
  • a printed image of Munch's 'The Scream' to look at together

Steps

  1. Pick a big feeling you've had: excited, nervous, overjoyed, frustrated.
  2. Draw a face showing that feeling — big and simple, filling the page.
  3. Fill the background with wavy, swirly lines in bold colors, like the painting's sky.
  4. Write one sentence under your art: what feeling is it, without telling the whole story.
  5. Gallery walk: look at everyone's piece and guess the feeling. Artists reveal at the end.

Variations

Easier: Draw just the face with the wavy background — skip the sentence.

Harder: Make a diptych: the same scene drawn twice, once with calm straight lines and once with wild wavy ones.

⚠️ Safety

Feelings are welcome; stories behind them stay private unless a kid chooses to share.
