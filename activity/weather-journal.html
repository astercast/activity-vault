Weather Journal

A week of sky-watching turns kids into junior meteorologists.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–40 kids
Time
10–20 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Print a simple daily log: date, sky (sunny/cloudy/rainy), temperature guess, wind, one observation. Works as a recurring 10-minute opener.

Materials

  • paper and pencils or a printed journal sheet
  • optional: outdoor thermometer

Steps

  1. Each day, kids step outside (or look out the window) and record the sky, clouds, wind, and temperature.
  2. Add one free observation: birds, puddles, what people are wearing.
  3. After a week, look back together: what patterns show up? Did cloudy days feel cooler?
  4. Let kids make a one-day forecast for tomorrow and check who was closest.
  5. Keep the journals going for a month for a real mini climate record.

Variations

Easier: Use picture checkboxes (sun, cloud, rain) instead of written entries for younger kids.

Harder: Add cloud-type identification (cumulus, stratus, cirrus) with a simple cloud chart.
