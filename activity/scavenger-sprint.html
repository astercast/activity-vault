Photo Scavenger Sprint

Teams sprint around capturing a photo list — funniest, tallest, bluest — then present their best shots for votes.

Ages
7–12
Group
6–30 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Print photo lists; charge one device per team.

Materials

  • phone or tablet with camera
  • printed photo list
  • timer

Steps

  1. Make a photo list: something that makes you laugh, the tallest thing you can find, a perfect shadow, something blue, your team's best jump.
  2. Teams of 3–4 get 20 minutes and one phone or tablet per team (or the leader's phone, passed around).
  3. Teams sprint around the area capturing every item. Creativity counts double.
  4. Everyone returns and each team presents their 3 best photos on the big screen or passed around.
  5. Vote: funniest photo, most artistic, best team jump. Winners pick the next activity.

Variations

Easier: Stay in one defined area (playground only).

Harder: Add cryptic riddle clues instead of a straight list.

⚠️ Safety

Set hard boundaries; teams stay together; no running near roads.
