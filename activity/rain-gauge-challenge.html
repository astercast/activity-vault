Rain Gauge Challenge

Build a rain gauge, predict the week, check the sky's score.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–50 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cut the top third off each clear bottle and flip it into the base as a funnel. Tape it so it stays. Weigh bases down with pebbles.

Materials

  • clear plastic bottles (one per kid or pair)
  • rulers
  • permanent markers
  • pebbles for stability

Steps

  1. Have kids mark a ruler scale on the side of their bottle with a permanent marker.
  2. Place all gauges outside in the open, away from overhangs.
  3. Predict: how much rain will fall this week? Write guesses on a chart.
  4. Check and record the level each day. Pour out after recording so each day starts fresh.
  5. At week's end, compare guesses to totals and crown the best weather predictor.

Variations

Easier: Build one class gauge together and check it as a group.

Harder: Graph the week's rainfall and compare it to the local forecast.

⚠️ Safety

Cut the bottles ahead of time — no kid cutting plastic. Place gauges where nobody will trip over them.
