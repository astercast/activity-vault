Pumpkin Letter Hunt

Letters hide all over a pumpkin — find the one the teacher calls.

Ages
3–6
Group
2–20 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Stick letter stickers all over the pumpkin — uppercase on one side, matching lowercase nearby. Set the pumpkin on a table everyone can reach.

Materials

  • a small pumpkin
  • letter stickers or masking tape with letters written on
  • a pointer stick (optional)

Steps

  1. Gather the kids around the pumpkin. Name a letter out loud.
  2. Kids take turns finding that letter on the pumpkin and pointing to it (or gently tapping it).
  3. When they find it, everyone says the letter's sound together.
  4. Level up: call out a letter and ask for a word that starts with it before the hunt continues.
  5. End by singing the alphabet while pointing to letters around the pumpkin in order.

Variations

Easier: Use just 5–6 letters spaced far apart on the pumpkin.

Harder: Hide sight words instead of letters and have kids read the word they find.

⚠️ Safety

Little hands can topple the pumpkin — keep it on a low, steady table.
