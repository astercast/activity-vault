Corn Husk Dolls

Soak, bundle, and tie dried corn husks into old-fashioned dolls.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
30–50 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Soak the corn husks in warm water for 10 minutes so they're bendable. Give each kid 6–8 husks and a length of twine.

Materials

  • dried corn husks (craft store or grocery)
  • a bowl of warm water
  • string or twine
  • scissors
  • markers

Steps

  1. Lay 4 husks flat and tie them together in the middle — this bundle is the body.
  2. Fold the top half down over the tie to make the head; tie again just below to form the neck.
  3. Take 2 more husks, roll them tight, and tie both ends — these are the arms. Slip them through the body bundle and tie at the waist.
  4. Arrange the bottom husks into a skirt shape and tie at the waist.
  5. Draw a face with markers, or leave it blank like traditional husk dolls. Name your doll.

Variations

Easier: Make a simple version: one bundle tied for the head, one tie for the waist — no separate arms.

Harder: Dress the dolls with extra husk layers, tiny hats, or woven belts.

⚠️ Safety

Soak water can be slippery — wipe up spills so nobody slips.
