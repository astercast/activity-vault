👋 Icebreakers

Winter Joke Slam

Everyone writes a clean winter joke — the applause-o-meter picks the champion.

Ages
7–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Set the rules: winter-themed, clean, and kind — no jokes at anyone's expense. Hand out slips.

Materials

  • slips of paper and pencils
  • a jar or hat

Steps

  1. Everyone writes one winter joke on a slip (a groaner is perfect) and drops it in the jar.
  2. Shake the jar. Draw slips one at a time and read each joke aloud with full dramatic flair.
  3. After each joke, the room rates it with applause — louder means funnier.
  4. Top 3 joke-writers read their own jokes for the final round.
  5. Crown the Winter Joke Champion. Post the winning joke on the board for the week.

Variations

Easier: The teacher reads pre-collected jokes instead of kids writing their own.

Harder: Kids illustrate the winning jokes into a class joke book.

⚠️ Safety

Jokes stay kind — nothing that teases a person or group.
