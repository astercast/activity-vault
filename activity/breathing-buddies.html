🧘 Quiet & Focus

Breathing Buddies

Stuffed animals ride bellies up and down as kids practice slow breathing — the invisible made visible.

Ages
4–8
Group
1–20 kids
Time
10–10 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Have a basket of spare stuffed animals.

Materials

  • stuffed animals (one per child)

Steps

  1. Each child picks a favorite stuffed animal — their 'breathing buddy.'
  2. Lie down and place the buddy on your belly.
  3. Breathe in slowly through the nose: watch the buddy rise. Breathe out slowly through the mouth: watch it fall.
  4. Count together: in for 4, out for 4. Five rounds.
  5. The buddy makes the invisible visible — kids can SEE their breath working.

Variations

Easier: Three rounds instead of five.

Harder: Practice 'smell the flowers, blow the candle' extended exhales.

⚠️ Safety

None.
