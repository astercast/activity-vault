Parachute Play

Lift, billow, and trap the air — popcorn balls, mushroom tents, and color swaps with the classic play parachute.

Ages
4–10
Group
6–30 kids
Time
25–25 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Spread the parachute; space everyone evenly.

Materials

  • play parachute
  • soft foam balls

Steps

  1. Everyone grabs the parachute edge, evenly spaced. Lift it high on 'UP!' and let it billow.
  2. Popcorn: toss soft foam balls on top and shake to make them bounce like popcorn.
  3. Mushroom: lift high, then everyone steps in and sits on the edge, trapping the air — you're inside a giant mushroom.
  4. Sharks: one child crawls underneath while the rest make waves; 'sharks' try to tag feet from below.
  5. Color call: leader shouts a color; everyone holding that color runs under the parachute to swap places.

Variations

Easier: Just lift-and-billow waves.

Harder: Choreograph a 3-move routine and perform it.

⚠️ Safety

No wrapping the parachute around necks; keep grips loose.
