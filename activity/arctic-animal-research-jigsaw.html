Arctic Animal Research Jigsaw

Small expert teams research one arctic animal each, then teach the class.

Ages
8–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
30–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Prepare one simple fact sheet per animal (diet, habitat, one superpower adaptation). Split the class into expert teams of 3–4.

Materials

  • arctic animal fact sheets (one animal per team: polar bear, arctic fox, snowy owl, narwhal, penguin, seal)
  • poster paper
  • markers

Steps

  1. Each team studies its animal's sheet and picks the three coolest facts.
  2. Teams make a poster: drawing, the three facts, and one question for the class.
  3. New mixed groups form with one expert from each animal — each expert teaches their animal in two minutes.
  4. Back together: the class votes on the most surprising adaptation learned.

Variations

Easier: Teacher reads the fact sheet aloud; teams draw the animal and dictate one fact.

Harder: Add a 'myth vs. fact' round: teams invent one plausible-sounding fake fact and the class guesses which is fake.
