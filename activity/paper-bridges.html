Paper Bridge Engineering

Engineer a paper bridge across a book canyon, load it with pennies until it fails, then redesign and retest.

Ages
7–12
Group
1–20 kids
Time
35–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set up book canyons (12-inch gaps) and penny stations.

Materials

  • paper
  • tape
  • stacks of books
  • pennies
  • rulers

Steps

  1. Give each builder 5 sheets of paper and 12 inches of tape. The challenge: span a 12-inch gap between two stacks of books.
  2. Test each bridge by stacking pennies in the center until it collapses. Record the weight held.
  3. Rebuild round: same materials, but now builders can fold, roll, or accordion the paper first.
  4. Test again. Almost every bridge holds dramatically more — folded paper is corrugated, like real bridge beams.
  5. Discuss: which shapes held most? Where did bridges fail? That's exactly how engineers talk.

Variations

Easier: Allow unlimited tape.

Harder: Limit to 3 sheets of paper and require a 100-penny hold.

⚠️ Safety

Paper and pennies are safe.
