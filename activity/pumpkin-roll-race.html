Pumpkin Roll Race

Roll a pumpkin across the field with a pool noodle — no hands allowed.

Ages
5–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
20–35 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set up straight lanes with cones, one pumpkin and one noodle per lane. Mark a clear finish line.

Materials

  • small pumpkins (one per racer)
  • pool noodles (one per racer)
  • cones to mark lanes and a finish line

Steps

  1. Racers line up behind their pumpkins, noodle in hand.
  2. On 'go', guide your pumpkin down the lane using ONLY the noodle — no hands, no feet.
  3. If your pumpkin leaves your lane, roll it back in before continuing.
  4. Run heats of 4–6 racers; winners race a final.
  5. Cool-down: everyone helps carry pumpkins back. Losers' pumpkins get a consolation cheer.

Variations

Easier: Shorten the lane and let younger kids use their hands to roll.

Harder: Add slalom cones to weave around, or race in pairs with one noodle each.

⚠️ Safety

Noodles guide pumpkins — never swing them at people. Pumpkins stay on the ground.
