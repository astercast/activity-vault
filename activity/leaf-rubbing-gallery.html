Leaf Rubbing Gallery

Collect fall leaves, rub their veins onto paper, and hang a natural gallery.

Ages
5–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Outdoors & Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Best in fall. Start with a short leaf-collection walk — each kid gathers 5–10 interesting leaves.

Materials

  • fallen leaves (collect outside)
  • plain white paper
  • crayons with wrappers peeled

Steps

  1. Take a leaf walk and collect fallen leaves of different shapes and sizes.
  2. Back at the table, place a leaf vein-side up under a sheet of paper.
  3. Rub the side of a crayon firmly over the paper — the leaf's shape and veins appear.
  4. Make a gallery page with several rubbings. Label each with the leaf's shape or a made-up name.
  5. Hang the pages together as a fall display.

Variations

Easier: Use big sturdy leaves (maple, oak) which give the clearest rubbings.

Harder: Turn it into a leaf ID challenge — match each rubbing to the tree it came from.

⚠️ Safety

Collect only fallen leaves from the ground — no pulling leaves off plants. Wash hands after.
