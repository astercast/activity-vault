🧘 Quiet & Focus

Listening Bell Practice

Ring a bell once and listen until the sound vanishes completely — three rounds, and the room transforms.

Ages
4–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
10–10 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Have the bell ready; arrange comfortable seating.

Materials

  • singing bowl, bell, chime, or a phone app with a bell sound

Steps

  1. Everyone sits comfortably, eyes closed or softly focused on the floor.
  2. Strike a singing bowl, bell, or chime once. The only job: listen until the sound completely disappears.
  3. When you can't hear it anymore, silently raise your hand — eyes still closed.
  4. Ask: how long did it last? What did you notice at the very end, when it was almost gone?
  5. Repeat 3 times. Each round, the group gets quieter and listens longer. That's the whole magic.

Variations

Easier: Do one round only.

Harder: Try it with eyes open, soft gaze — notice the difference.

⚠️ Safety

Keep volume gentle.
