Blanket Ball Bounce Together

Everyone holds the edge of one blanket, one soft ball rolls in the middle, and the whole group's job is the same: keep it on, keep it low.

Ages
3–5
Group
6–16 kids
Time
10–20 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Spread the blanket and walk around it testing edges: every kid needs a piece of edge they can hold with two hands standing still — too many kids on one side means a smaller group per round, not a stretch. The ball goes in the middle only when every hand is holding on; test the lift yourself: blanket up to knee height, flat and steady.

Materials

  • one sturdy blanket or large sheet
  • one big soft ball

Steps

  1. State the one rule before the ball exists: 'The ball stays on the blanket. We keep it low and slow — a ball that flies off means we all went too fast together.' Practise one lift and lower with no ball until the blanket rises as one piece.
  2. Ball in. First job is stillness: hold the blanket flat and watch the ball sit in the middle. A group of three-year-olds holding one thing still together has already done the hard part — say so.
  3. Rolling round: on your call, one side lifts a little and the ball rolls slowly toward the low side; then that side lifts and it rolls back. Name the kid the ball is visiting each time: 'It's going to see Maya…' — the rolling has a destination, and the destination is a person.
  4. Small bounce round: tiny lifts together, the ball hopping no higher than the kids' knees. Count the hops out loud as a group; when the ball leaves the blanket (it will), it goes back to the middle and the count starts fresh — leaving is part of the game, never the end of it.
  5. Finish with the carry: ball resting in the middle, the group carries the blanket together — slow walking, all hands on — to the box or basket and tips the ball in as one team. Blanket folded by its holders, two to an edge.

Variations

Easier: Fewer holders and a lighter ball, adult on the edge as a permanent holder — the lift is smaller and slower.

Harder: Two soft balls, and the group tries to keep both on through the rolling round — visits get called for both.

⚠️ Safety

Soft ball only, bounced low — knee height at most — and the blanket never goes higher than the shortest holder's waist. Nobody gets under the blanket, nobody lets go suddenly on purpose, and a dropped edge just gets picked back up; the game pauses, it does not scold.
