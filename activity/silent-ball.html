Silent Ball

Pass the ball in total silence. Talk, laugh, or drop it and you are out.

Ages 7–12 · 10–25 min · 1 material

Ages
7–12
Group
8–30 kids
Time
10–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Stand in a circle. One soft ball. The rule that matters: total silence.

Materials

  • one soft foam or beach ball

Steps

  1. Toss the ball to anyone in the circle, but nobody may talk, laugh out loud, or make noise.
  2. If you talk, drop the ball, or make a bad throw, you sit out for that round.
  3. The circle shrinks as players drop out. Last one standing wins.
  4. Play again. It is harder than it sounds to stay quiet while catching.
  5. Variation round: allow whispering but no laughing.

Variations

Easier: Allow one 'free' mistake per player before they sit out.

Harder: Two balls in play at once, or players must catch one-handed.

⚠️ Safety

Gentle tosses only, no fast throws at faces.
