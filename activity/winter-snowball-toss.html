Indoor Snowball Toss

Soft pom-pom 'snowballs' and bucket targets turn any room into a winter carnival — zero frostbite, zero cleanup.

Ages
5–12
Group
4–25 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set buckets at varied distances; mark throwing lines with tape.

Materials

  • white pom-poms or crumpled paper balls
  • buckets or boxes
  • tape
  • scorecards (optional)

Steps

  1. Set up 3–4 buckets at different distances and assign point values (near = 10, far = 50).
  2. Mark throwing lines with tape — one close for little kids, one farther for big kids.
  3. Each player gets 5 snowballs per turn. Toss underhand only.
  4. Play rounds: individual high score, then team totals, then a 'trick shot' finale round.
  5. End with a 60-second 'snowball fight' free-for-all where everyone throws soft pom-poms (no targets, just joy), then teams race to collect them all.

Variations

Easier: Move all buckets close; everyone throws from one line.

Harder: Add trick-shot targets (behind the back, non-dominant hand) for bonus points.

⚠️ Safety

Underhand throws only; soft pom-poms or paper balls — nothing hard.
