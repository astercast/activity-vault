Easter Egg Relay Race

Teams race to transport eggs on spoons through silly obstacles — drop it and you start that leg over.

Ages
5–12
Group
8–30 kids
Time
25–25 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Build the obstacle course; line up teams with spoons and eggs.

Materials

  • plastic eggs
  • spoons
  • cones or chairs for obstacles

Steps

  1. Set up a simple obstacle path: weave cones, go under a table, around a chair.
  2. Split into teams and line them up. First runner in each team balances a plastic egg on a spoon.
  3. Runners complete the course and hand the spoon-and-egg to the next teammate. Drop the egg? Go back to the last obstacle.
  4. First team with everyone through wins. Run it twice — second round, everyone must hold the spoon in their non-dominant hand.
  5. Finale: champions vs. teachers race while the group cheers.

Variations

Easier: Straight-line relay with no obstacles.

Harder: Add a second egg per runner and a zigzag cone weave.

⚠️ Safety

Clear the course of tripping hazards; no running with spoons near faces.
