Improv Scene Showdown

Suggestions in, scenes out, a bracket of improv games with the room as judge.

Before you begin

Ages
11–14
Group
6–30 kids
Time
25–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Collect suggestions on slips: locations, occupations, weird objects. Split into teams of 3-4.

Materials

  • slips of paper
  • a hat or bowl

Steps

  1. Teach the three games: freeze (two actors, someone yells freeze and swaps in), yes-and story (build one sentence each), and prop switch (one object, ten uses).
  2. Round one: each team draws a suggestion and plays one game, 2 minutes. The room is the audience.
  3. Audience votes by applause after each round. Loudest wins the round. Losing teams become the judges' panel.
  4. Semifinal and final: winners play a mashup. Start with one game, the judges call the switch mid-scene.
  5. Champions get the golden prop (any silly object). Everyone plays every round, judges perform too.

Variations

Easier: Play single rounds with no bracket, or let the teacher call all the switches.

Harder: Add audience-thrown curveballs mid-scene, or require every scene to end with a moral.
