I Spy Walk

Turn any walk into a game of sharp eyes and clever clues.

Ages
5–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

None — just a route. A park loop, a school block, a nature trail.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Walk in pairs. One partner picks something they can see and says, 'I spy something green and wiggly...'
  2. The other guesses. Three guesses, then a new clue.
  3. Swap roles every few minutes so everyone leads.
  4. Collect the best spies: who stumped their partner the longest?
  5. End the walk by sharing one cool thing you noticed that you walk past every day.

Variations

Easier: Give the first letter as a free clue for younger kids.

Harder: Ban color clues — describe by shape, texture, or what it does.

⚠️ Safety

Walk in pairs on paths — stay off roads and out of parking lots.
