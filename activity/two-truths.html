👋 Icebreakers

Two Truths and a Fib

Two truths, one believable fib — the group votes on which is which, and the best liars earn glory.

Ages
7–12
Group
4–25 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Hand out paper slips and pencils.

Materials

  • paper slips
  • pencils

Steps

  1. Everyone writes down three statements about themselves: two true, one false. Keep them believable — the best fibs are almost true.
  2. One by one, each person reads their three statements. The group votes on which one is the fib.
  3. Reveal the answer. Award a point to everyone who guessed right — and a bonus point to the reader if they fooled the majority.
  4. Set the tone first: no fibs about serious things, keep it fun and safe.
  5. End with a lightning round: the leader reads anonymous ones and the group guesses who wrote them.

Variations

Easier: Do it in small groups of 5.

Harder: The group can ask one follow-up question before voting.

⚠️ Safety

Keep fibs light — nothing about serious topics.
