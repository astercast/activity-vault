Toy Wagon Pull Together

A small wagon, a load of soft toys, and a blanket across the room: two pullers, one slow trip, every toy delivered together.

Ages 3–5 · 10–20 min · 3 materials

Ages
3–5
Group
2–12 kids
Time
10–20 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Set the station first: the blanket laid flat across the room or yard, the wagon at the loading end with the cargo in a pile beside it. Test the route yourself with the empty wagon. A route the wagon tips on gets shortened or moved now; the pullers' job is pulling, not rescuing a tipped wagon. Check the handle and wheels roll freely.

Materials

  • a small sturdy wagon with a pull handle (child-size)
  • soft toys or beanbags (the cargo, six to ten pieces)
  • a blanket (the delivery station)

Steps

  1. Load together: the whole group puts the cargo in the wagon, one piece each in turn, counting the pieces in out loud. State the one rule before anyone pulls: 'The wagon goes slow, and it only goes when BOTH pullers are holding on.'
  2. Two pullers on the handle, side by side, hands on, and the trip is a walk, at the pace of the slower puller, with the rest of the group as the road crew walking alongside, never in front of the wheels.
  3. Halfway stop, on your call: pullers swap sides, road crew checks the cargo. A toy riding near the edge gets tucked back in by its loader. Checking the load is part of delivering it, said out loud.
  4. Arrive at the blanket: the group unloads together, one piece each in turn, laying the toys out on the blanket in a row, and counts them back out. The count in and the count out must match, and finding a missing one in the wagon is the group's detective moment.
  5. Return trip: two new pullers take the empty wagon back the same slow way, road crew alongside. Then new loaders start the next delivery if there is time. Everyone who wants a turn pulling gets one before anyone gets a second.

Variations

Easier: A shorter trip, blanket ten steps away, and one puller plus the adult's hand on the handle beside theirs.

Harder: Two deliveries to two blankets: the loaders call which station each load is for, and the road crew has to remember it on arrival.

⚠️ Safety

Walking only, ever. A wagon pulled at a run tips, and tipping wagons land on feet. Hands on the handle only while pulling; nobody rides in the wagon, nobody sits on its edges, and fingers stay out of the wheels. The route keeps clear of slopes the wagon could roll down on its own.

🤝 Inclusion notes

Pulling is one job on a crew with several: the loader who counts the cargo in, the road-crew checker who tucks it safe, and the unloader who lays the row at the station all deliver the same toys. A kid can pull from a seated position alongside the handle's low end with the wagon moving at their pace. The wagon goes at the crew's pace, and the crew is everybody.
