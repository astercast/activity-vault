Fall Scavenger Bingo

A bingo card full of fall — hunt the outdoors for every square.

Ages
5–12
Group
2–40 kids
Time
25–45 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Print bingo cards with fall finds (red leaf, acorn, pinecone, yellow leaf, smooth rock, feather, piece of bark, something round, a seed, a stick shaped like a letter) — or have kids draw their own 3x3 grids and fill in fall items.

Materials

  • printed fall bingo cards (or blank cards kids draw themselves)
  • pencils or crayons

Steps

  1. Hand out a card and pencil to each kid (or pair).
  2. Walk the yard, field, or trail together. When you spot a square's item, mark it — look, don't always pick.
  3. First row, column, or diagonal filled = BINGO. Keep walking for a full-card blackout if energy allows.
  4. End in a circle: each kid shows their favorite find and says why they picked it.
  5. Leave everything where it belongs — the next class gets the same hunt.

Variations

Easier: Use a 3x3 card with pictures instead of words for younger kids.

Harder: Add a tally challenge: count how many of each item the whole group found and graph it.

⚠️ Safety

Set clear boundaries for the walk area first. Watch for poison ivy, thorns, and busy paths.
