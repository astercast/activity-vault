Teach-a-Move Dance Tutorial

Each group invents a dance move, teaches it to everyone, then strings them all into one epic group routine.

Ages
6–12
Group
4–25 kids
Time
25–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Split into groups; have music ready.

Materials

  • music player and speaker

Steps

  1. Each small group picks or invents a simple 8-count dance move — the floss, the worm (attempted), or something original.
  2. Groups get 10 minutes to perfect their move and figure out how to teach it: break it into 3 steps, demo slowly, then at speed.
  3. Teach-off: each group teaches their move to everyone else. The whole room learns 4–5 new moves.
  4. String all the moves together into one epic group routine, in order.
  5. Perform the routine and film it. This becomes the group's signature dance.

Variations

Easier: Teach one move to the whole room together.

Harder: Film the routine and edit it into a music video.

⚠️ Safety

Clear the floor; warm up first.
