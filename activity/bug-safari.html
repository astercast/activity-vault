Backyard Bug Safari

Magnifying glasses, bug jars, and sketchbooks — a real field-research expedition for tiny creatures.

Ages
5–10
Group
2–20 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Hand out jars and magnifiers; set boundaries.

Materials

  • magnifying glasses
  • clear jars with air holes
  • field guide or identification app
  • sketch paper
  • pencils

Steps

  1. Hand out magnifying glasses and bug jars (clear containers with air holes poked in the lids).
  2. Set the mission: find and observe 5 different creatures. Draw each one — count legs, note colors, sketch the shape.
  3. Rule: observe, don't harm. Everything gets released exactly where it was found.
  4. The group naturalist (you) helps identify finds with a simple field guide or app.
  5. Back inside, everyone shares their coolest discovery. Award titles: tiniest, fastest, weirdest, most legs.

Variations

Easier: Safari in one garden bed with the leader pointing out finds.

Harder: Identify every find to family level with a field guide.

⚠️ Safety

Look, don't touch unknown insects; roll logs toward you and replace them.
