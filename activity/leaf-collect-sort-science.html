Leaf Collect & Sort

Every kid collects ten leaves, then the group sorts them like real scientists.

Ages
5–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–40 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Set a collection boundary and a time limit (10 minutes). Hand out bags.

Materials

  • a bag or basket per kid
  • sorting trays, paper plates, or big sheets of paper

Steps

  1. Everyone collects exactly 10 different leaves — different is the goal.
  2. Dump them out and sort them into groups. The rule is yours to invent: by color, by size, by smooth vs. jagged edges.
  3. Count each group and lay the leaves out as a living bar graph.
  4. Each kid explains their sorting rule to a partner. Try someone else's rule on your leaves — does it still work?
  5. Pick the most surprising leaf and sketch it with three labeled features.

Variations

Easier: Collect 5 leaves and sort by one rule: color.

Harder: Press the most interesting leaves between book pages and start a class leaf herbarium with labels.

⚠️ Safety

Check the area for hazards before collecting. No tasting leaves.
