Cushion Train All Aboard

A row of cushions and chairs becomes a train: a ticket block to hand the driver, stops the kids name, and everyone rides.

Ages 3–5 · 10–20 min · 3 materials

Ages
3–5
Group
4–18 kids
Time
10–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Build the train before the group boards: seats in a row close enough that a seated kid can pass the ticket block to the seat behind without standing. The front seat is the driver's and it stays empty until the first driver climbs in. Walk the row and sit in every seat yourself; a seat that tips gets swapped now.

Materials

  • cushions, mats, or sturdy chairs in a row (one seat per rider)
  • one big soft block or a beanbag (the ticket)
  • floor space for a route around the room

Steps

  1. Board in order, back seat first, and state the one rule as they sit: 'The train moves when the driver says go, and the train is pretend, so our feet stay still and the moving is in our voices.' The first sound the train makes, the group invents together and keeps.
  2. Tickets: the last rider holds the big block. On 'all aboard', the block travels hand to hand up the row to the driver. A ticket collected is the train's permission to leave. Dropped tickets are picked up by the nearest hands; the train waits.
  3. Stops are named by the riders: the driver calls 'Next stop…' and a rider names somewhere real to them, grandma's house, the shop with the good bread, the moon, and the whole train says it back as they 'arrive', with a gentle lean-and-stop sway.
  4. Swap the driver every two stops: drivers climb out on the outside of the row, walk up, and the new driver takes the front seat while everyone shuffles one seat back. Shuffling seats without a fuss is a skill this game practises on purpose.
  5. Last stop is the station, your carpet or rug, and the group 'arrives' with the train sound one final time, then helps return the seats to the walls, one cushion carried by two riders.

Variations

Easier: Four seats, the adult as permanent driver, and stops you name, the ticket still travels and the riders still ride.

Harder: A second carriage: a second short row behind, with its own ticket, and the driver has to collect both before the train can go.

⚠️ Safety

Seats are sat on, never stood on, and climbing in and out happens on the outside of the row at a walk. The ticket is soft and travels hand to hand at lap height. Nothing is thrown up the train.
