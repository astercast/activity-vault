Pumpkin Life Cycle Wheel

Spin the paper-plate wheel: seed, sprout, vine, flower, pumpkin.

Before you begin

Ages
5–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cut a wedge window out of the top plate. Kids will draw the 5 stages around the bottom plate.

Materials

  • paper plates (2 per kid)
  • brass fasteners
  • crayons or markers
  • scissors

Steps

  1. Draw the life cycle around the bottom plate: seed, sprout, vine, flower, green pumpkin, orange pumpkin.
  2. Label each stage in your own words.
  3. Fasten the plates together in the center - spin to reveal each stage through the window.
  4. Quiz a partner: spin to a random stage, name what comes next.
  5. Hang the wheels up: the whole class life cycle, spinning.

Variations

Easier: Three stages instead of five: seed, vine, pumpkin.

Harder: Add the real timeline: how many days does each stage take?

Safety

Brass fasteners are pointy - teacher pokes the center holes.
