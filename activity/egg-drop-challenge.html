Egg Drop Challenge

Design a container that keeps a raw egg safe in a drop from up high.

Ages
8–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
30–60 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Divide the group into teams of 2–4. Put the building supplies on a central table and give each team one egg. Pick a drop spot — a balcony, stairwell landing, or playground structure with a safe landing zone below.

Materials

  • raw eggs (one per team, plus a spare or two)
  • tape, rubber bands, string
  • scrap paper, cardboard, newspaper
  • plastic bags
  • cotton balls or crumpled paper for padding

Steps

  1. Explain the goal: the egg must survive a drop from the marked height. No catching.
  2. Give teams 20 minutes to build a container around their egg using the supplies.
  3. Walk everyone to the drop spot together. One drop per team, one at a time.
  4. Open each container and check for cracks. Survivors move to a higher drop if you want a finals round.
  5. Debrief: what protected the winners — padding, a parachute, a cage? Connect it to how real things are packaged.

Variations

Easier: Drop from a lower height (one story or a table) so success is likely.

Harder: Limit teams to 5 items total, or ban parachutes so they solve it with structure alone.

⚠️ Safety

Keep the drop zone clear — nobody stands under the drop. Clean up any broken egg immediately; wash hands after.
