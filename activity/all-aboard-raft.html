All Aboard Raft

The whole team on the tarp. Then fold it in half.

Ages
8–12
Group
8–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Lay out one tarp or blanket per team of 6–8 on the floor or grass.

Materials

  • a tarp, old blanket, or large sheet of paper per team

Steps

  1. The whole team must stand on the raft for 10 seconds without anyone touching the ground.
  2. Easy? Fold the raft in half and try again.
  3. Keep folding — half, quarter — until it seems impossible.
  4. Let teams strategize: carrying each other and creative balancing are allowed.
  5. Debrief: what worked — and what did the team learn about asking for help?

Variations

Easier: Bigger tarp, fewer folds.

Harder: Silent round — no talking while shrinking the raft.

⚠️ Safety

Feet only on the raft — no jumping on, and keep hands to yourself while balancing.
