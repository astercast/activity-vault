Dragon Parade

A walking, waving paper dragon — every kid gets a section.

Ages
5–12
Group
8–30 kids
Time
30–60 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Build the dragon ahead of time: tape long fabric or crepe-paper strips to poles so a line of kids can carry them, and make a head from a decorated box.

Materials

  • long strip of fabric or crepe paper for the body
  • a decorated box or paper bag for the head
  • a drum, speaker, or rhythm sticks

Steps

  1. Line up: one kid carries the head, the rest each hold a section of the body.
  2. Practice the wave — the head dips, the body follows one beat later.
  3. Parade around the room or field to a drum beat, weaving in S-curves.
  4. Freeze-dance: when the music stops, the dragon freezes in its pose.
  5. Swap roles so a new kid leads the head each round.

Variations

Easier: Skip the poles — kids hold the fabric directly.

Harder: Choreograph a figure-eight weave through cones.

⚠️ Safety

Keep the line moving at a walk — no running while holding the dragon up.
