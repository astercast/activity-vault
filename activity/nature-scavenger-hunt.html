Nature Scavenger Hunt

A checklist, a bag, and twenty minutes of sharp-eyed hunting.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Print checklists tailored to your space: something smooth, something rough, a Y-shaped stick, 3 different leaves, something red, a feather, something that makes a sound.

Materials

  • printed scavenger checklists
  • paper bags for collecting
  • pencils

Steps

  1. Hand out checklists and bags. Set the boundaries and the time limit (20 minutes).
  2. Kids hunt in pairs — pairs find more and argue less.
  3. Regroup and show-and-tell the finds. Award points for the trickiest items.
  4. Bonus: find something NOT on the list and convince the group it should have been.
  5. Leave no trace — put back anything alive, take only photos of the rest.

Variations

Easier: Use picture checklists for kids who cannot read yet.

Harder: Make it a photo hunt — find it, photograph it, leave it where it lies.

⚠️ Safety

Collect only what is on the ground. Stay in the marked area; no eating anything found.
