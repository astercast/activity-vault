Egg Carton Spiders

One egg-carton cup + pipe-cleaner legs = a spider that actually dangles.

Ages
4–9
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

An adult cuts the cartons into single cups ahead of time — one cup per kid. Set out paint and let cups dry before decorating.

Materials

  • cardboard egg cartons
  • black paint or markers
  • pipe cleaners
  • googly eyes
  • glue
  • string

Steps

  1. Paint or color the cup black (or any spider color you like).
  2. Poke 8 pipe-cleaner legs through the sides — 4 on each side — and bend the ends into little feet.
  3. Glue on googly eyes. Give your spider a name.
  4. Tie a string to the top and hang the spiders around the room — a whole spider drop.
  5. Optional: write one true spider fact on a tag and hang it with your spider.

Variations

Easier: Skip the paint — decorate the cup with markers instead.

Harder: Build a spiderweb from yarn between two chairs and hang the whole class's spiders in it.

⚠️ Safety

Adults cut the cartons — the cardboard is stiff. Wash hands after painting.
