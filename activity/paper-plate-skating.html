Paper Plate Skating

Two paper plates, one slippery floor — instant indoor skating.

Ages
4–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Clear a big open space on a hard floor. Hand out two paper plates per kid.

Materials

  • paper plates (two per kid)

Steps

  1. Put one plate under each foot — instant skates.
  2. Practice gliding: push off gently and slide across the floor.
  3. Add challenges: glide to the line, spin in place, skate backwards.
  4. Relay: teams skate a loop and tag the next skater.
  5. Freeze skate: when the music stops, freeze mid-glide.

Variations

Easier: Sit-down scooting: sit on a plate and push with your hands.

Harder: Obstacle slalom between cones.

⚠️ Safety

Hard smooth floors only — no carpet, no wet spots. Clear the area of furniture and keep a wall or chair nearby for wobbly skaters.
