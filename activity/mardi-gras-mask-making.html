Mardi Gras Mask Making

Paper-plate masks, maximum sparkle.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–50 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pre-cut eye holes in the paper plates if the group is young. Set out decorating supplies in shared trays.

Materials

  • paper plates (one per kid)
  • elastic string or ribbon
  • paint, feathers, sequins, glitter glue
  • scissors

Steps

  1. Hold the plate to your face and mark where the elastic should attach.
  2. Decorate: paint a base color first, then add feathers, sequins, and glitter glue.
  3. While masks dry, string the elastic through punched holes.
  4. Hold a mini parade around the room showing off the finished masks.
  5. Vote on categories: brightest, silliest, most mysterious.

Variations

Easier: Use stickers only — no paint, no drying time.

Harder: Symmetry challenge: both halves of the mask must mirror.

⚠️ Safety

Scissors only for cutting the mask shape — cut away from your body and keep them closed when walking.
