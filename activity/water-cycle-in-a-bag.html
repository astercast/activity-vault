Water Cycle in a Bag

The whole water cycle, sealed in a bag on the window.

Ages
5–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Tape zip bags to a sunny window. Give each kid a marker and a small cup of water.

Materials

  • zip-top bags (one per kid)
  • water
  • blue washable markers
  • tape

Steps

  1. Draw a sun and clouds on the upper part of the bag with the marker.
  2. Pour in a quarter-cup of water and seal the bag tight.
  3. Tape the bag to the window and predict what will happen.
  4. Check twice a day: droplets form on the plastic (condensation) and slide down (precipitation).
  5. After a few days, draw the cycle on the board and label each step the class observed.

Variations

Easier: One class bag everyone watches together.

Harder: Two bags — one in sun, one in shade — and compare condensation speed.

⚠️ Safety

Bags hang on the window — tape them well so nobody slips on a fallen, wet bag.
