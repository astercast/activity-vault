Recycled Paper Making

Yesterday's scrap paper becomes tomorrow's paper, blended, scooped, and pressed.

Ages 7–12 · 30–50 min · 7 materials

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
30–50 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Soak the shredded paper in water for at least an hour ahead of time. Set up the blender station where only the teacher stands. This is a two-day project: make today, use tomorrow.

Materials

  • shredded scrap paper
  • a blender (teacher-operated)
  • water
  • a basin
  • a window screen in a frame or fine mesh
  • sponges
  • towels

Steps

  1. Tear the soaked paper into small bits and blend it with plenty of water into a smooth pulp. The teacher runs the blender.
  2. Pour the pulp into the basin and stir in more water until it looks like thin oatmeal.
  3. Slide the screen frame under the pulp and lift straight up. A thin layer stays on the mesh.
  4. Sponge the water out through the screen, then flip the sheet onto a towel.
  5. Let the sheets dry overnight. Tomorrow: write on the paper you made.
  6. Optional: press flower petals into the wet pulp for decorated paper.

Variations

Easier: Make one big class sheet together instead of individual ones.

Harder: Experiment: which scrap makes the strongest sheet, newspaper, printer paper, or construction paper?

⚠️ Safety

The teacher operates the blender, no exceptions. Wipe up water spills right away; wet floors are the real hazard here.
