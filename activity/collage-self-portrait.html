Collage Self-Portrait

Cut-up magazines become portraits of who you are inside.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–50 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Collect a stack of old magazines. Put out glue, scissors, and big paper.

Materials

  • magazines and newspapers to cut up
  • glue sticks
  • scissors
  • large paper or cardstock

Steps

  1. Talk about it first: a self-portrait does not have to look like you — it can show what you love.
  2. Kids cut out images, colors, and words that feel like them.
  3. Arrange the pieces on the cardstock before gluing — composition first.
  4. Glue everything down. Add drawn details or a title with markers.
  5. Do a gallery walk. Kids guess whose portrait is whose before names are revealed.

Variations

Easier: Limit it to 10 cutouts so nobody gets overwhelmed.

Harder: Theme it: 'me in 10 years' or 'my perfect weekend' as the portrait subject.

⚠️ Safety

Scissors stay at the table.
