Indoor Obstacle Course

Crawl, hop, balance, and toss through a furniture obstacle course — then time it, tweak it, and beat your record.

Ages
5–12
Group
2–20 kids
Time
30–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Build the course; walk the group through it once slowly.

Materials

  • chairs and tables
  • hula hoops
  • painter's tape
  • beanbags and a bucket
  • cones
  • timer

Steps

  1. Build the course with what you have: crawl under tables, hop through hula hoops, balance along a tape line, toss a beanbag into a bucket.
  2. Walk the group through it once slowly, naming each station.
  3. Time each runner individually — or run it as a team relay for bigger groups.
  4. After round one, let kids suggest one change to make it harder. Rebuild and run again.
  5. Record best times on a leaderboard. Revisit the same course next month and watch times drop.

Variations

Easier: Untimed practice runs only.

Harder: Add a beanbag-carry leg and a blindfolded guided section.

⚠️ Safety

Pad sharp table corners; secure anything climbable.
