Snowflake Paper Patterns

Fold, snip, unfold — the six-fold surprise that never gets old.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Cut the paper into circles (or use coffee filters — they're already round and easy to fold).

Materials

  • white printer paper or coffee filters (3 per kid)
  • scissors

Steps

  1. Fold each circle in half, then into thirds, so you get a six-fold wedge.
  2. Snip triangles and curves out of the folded edges — not too close to the fold line.
  3. Unfold and gasp. Hang the snowflakes on string across the windows.
  4. Optional: tape two kids' snowflakes together back-to-back and notice no two match.

Variations

Easier: Pre-fold the wedges so little ones just cut the easy outer edges.

Harder: Challenge: try one cut too many and see what happens — then repair it with tape.

⚠️ Safety

Scissors supervision; remind kids to cut away from fingers.
