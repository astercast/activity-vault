No-Carve Pumpkin Decorating

All the pumpkin fun, zero knives — paint, stickers, and googly eyes.

Ages
5–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–50 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

A fall favorite with zero sharp tools. Cover tables, set out decorating supplies, hand out one pumpkin per kid.

Materials

  • small pumpkins (one per kid)
  • acrylic paint or paint pens
  • stickers, googly eyes, pipe cleaners
  • glue
  • newspaper for tables

Steps

  1. Wipe pumpkins clean so paint sticks.
  2. Plan a design: a face, a monster, a pattern, a mini scene.
  3. Paint the base coat first and let it dry while planning details.
  4. Add faces with paint pens, stick on googly eyes, glue on pipe-cleaner arms.
  5. Line up the finished pumpkins as a fall display — vote on silliest, spookiest, most creative.

Variations

Easier: Stickers and googly eyes only — no paint, no drying time.

Harder: Theme it: decorate your pumpkin as a book character.

⚠️ Safety

No carving means no knives — that is the whole point. Paint pens over brushes for less mess.
