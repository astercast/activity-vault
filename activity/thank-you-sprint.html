Thank-You Sprint

Five minutes, three thank-you notes. Who gets yours?

Ages 6–14 · 5–10 min · 2 materials

Ages
6–14
Group
4–60 kids
Time
5–10 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Everyone gets three paper scraps and a pencil.

Materials

  • paper (three scraps per kid)
  • pencils

Steps

  1. Think of three people you are thankful for. School counts.
  2. Five minutes: write one thank-you note to each. Two sentences is plenty.
  3. Fold them and write the name on the outside.
  4. Deliver them today, or save them for the week's end.

Variations

Easier: One note instead of three.

Harder: The notes must name something specific the person did.

⚠️ Safety

Kind and true, if you cannot say it kindly, pick someone else.
