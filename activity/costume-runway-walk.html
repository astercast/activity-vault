Costume Runway Walk

Every costume gets its strut: a runway, a narrator, and one big pose at the end.

Ages
4–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
20–30 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Lay the runway down the middle of the room. Line chairs along both sides for the audience. Pick your first narrator — or be it yourself.

Materials

  • a long rug, tape line, or chalk path for the runway
  • a pretend microphone

Steps

  1. One kid at a time walks the runway in costume while the narrator announces them: 'And here comes… the bravest pirate in Room 4!'
  2. At the end of the runway, strike one big pose and hold it for three seconds.
  3. The audience gives a silent cheer — jazz hands only, so the next walker can hear their intro.
  4. Walk back along the side and take a seat; the next walker goes.
  5. Finale: everyone walks the runway together for the group photo.

Variations

Easier: Walk in pairs so shy kids have company.

Harder: Walkers invent a signature move and the audience votes on categories — silliest, spookiest, most creative.

⚠️ Safety

Costumes must let kids see and walk — nothing over the eyes on the runway, and long capes get held up or pinned. Clear the runway of bags and shoes first.
