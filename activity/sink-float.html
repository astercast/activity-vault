Sink or Float Lab

Predict, test, and chart whether everyday objects sink or float — then try to flip the results.

Ages
4–8
Group
1–20 kids
Time
25–25 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Fill the tub; lay out 8–10 test objects with a prediction chart.

Materials

  • large tub or bin
  • water
  • test objects (rock, leaf, coin, sponge, apple, crayon, spoon, cap)
  • chart paper
  • towels

Steps

  1. Fill a big tub or bin with water and lay out 8–10 test objects: a rock, a leaf, a coin, a sponge, an apple, a crayon, a spoon, a plastic cap.
  2. Before each object goes in, every child votes: sink or float? Hands up, count it, record the prediction on a chart.
  3. Drop the object in. Cheer or groan at the result, then mark the chart.
  4. After all objects, sort them: what did the sinkers have in common? The floaters?
  5. Test round two: can you make a sinker float (put the rock in a bowl)? Can you make a floater sink (push the air out of the sponge)?

Variations

Easier: Test 4 obvious objects (rock, leaf, coin, sponge).

Harder: Challenge kids to make a sinker float and a floater sink, then explain how.

⚠️ Safety

Mop spills immediately — wet floors are the main hazard.
