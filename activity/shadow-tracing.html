Shadow Tracing

Trace your shadow in the morning, come back at noon, and watch it move.

Ages
6–11
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–60 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Find a sunny paved spot where kids can stand safely. Bring chalk.

Materials

  • sidewalk chalk
  • a sunny patch of pavement

Steps

  1. Each kid picks a pose and stands still while a partner traces their shadow outline in chalk.
  2. Write the time next to the outline.
  3. Come back an hour or two later and stand in the same spot. Trace the new shadow in a different color.
  4. Compare: the shadow moved and changed length. Ask why — guide them to the sun moving across the sky.
  5. Optional: trace once more before pickup for a three-color shadow record.

Variations

Easier: Trace a fixed object (a cone, a ball) instead of a person so nobody has to hold still.

Harder: Have kids predict where the shadow will be next time before re-tracing.

⚠️ Safety

Stay off parking lots and driveways — use a playground or courtyard with no vehicle traffic.
