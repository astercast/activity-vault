Desert Island Picks

Three things for a desert island — defend your picks.

Ages
8–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Circle up. The prompt: you are stranded on a desert island and can bring three things.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Each kid names their three desert island items and says why.
  2. The group can ask one follow-up question per person.
  3. Vote on the most practical list and the most creative list.
  4. Twist round: now you can only bring ONE thing — what changes?
  5. Close with the group's all-star survival kit: the best picks combined.

Variations

Easier: One item instead of three.

Harder: Add a constraint: nothing electronic, or everything must fit in a backpack.

⚠️ Safety

Keep picks friendly — no choosing people as items, and respect every answer.
