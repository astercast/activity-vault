🎉 Holidays

Thankful Garland

A paper chain where every link is something someone is thankful for.

Ages
5–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
15–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Cut paper into strips ahead of time (about 1 x 8 inches). Each kid needs 4–6 strips.

Materials

  • paper strips in fall colors
  • glue sticks or tape
  • markers

Steps

  1. On each strip, write one thing you are thankful for — people, foods, moments, anything.
  2. Loop the first strip into a ring and glue or tape it closed.
  3. Thread the next strip through the ring, loop it, and seal — a paper chain grows.
  4. Keep adding until every strip is linked. Combine small chains into one long garland.
  5. Hang the garland across the room as holiday decor.

Variations

Easier: Let kids draw instead of write on the strips.

Harder: Color-code the strips: people = red, things = orange, moments = yellow.
