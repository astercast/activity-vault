Winter Freeze Dance

Freeze dance with a twist: when the music stops, the caller shouts a winter animal.

Ages
4–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
10–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

You need nothing but music and space to move. Assign a caller (rotate the job).

Materials

  • music to play and pause

Steps

  1. Everyone dances while the music plays.
  2. Music stops — everyone freezes, and the caller shouts a winter animal: 'penguin!', 'polar bear!', 'snowy owl!'
  3. Everyone holds the pose of that animal until the music restarts.
  4. Rotate callers every few rounds; the silliest-held pose wins applause.

Variations

Easier: Caller gives extra time to find the pose; no one sits out for a wrong guess.

Harder: Add 'migrating goose': kids link up in a V and glide to a new spot in the room.

⚠️ Safety

Keep dance space clear of furniture corners; freeze means stop, not crash.
