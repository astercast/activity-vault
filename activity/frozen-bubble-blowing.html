Frozen Bubble Blowing

Blow a bubble, watch it freeze mid-air.

Before you begin

Ages
4–10
Group
2–25 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
No prep needed

Setup

Needs air below freezing. Head outside with bubble solution and wands.

Materials

  • bubble solution and wands
  • a below-freezing day

Steps

  1. Blow a bubble and catch it gently on the wand. Do not let it pop.
  2. Watch the surface: frost crystals spread across the bubble like frost on a window.
  3. Count how many seconds it takes to fully freeze.
  4. Try different sizes. Do big bubbles freeze faster or slower?
  5. Photograph the best crystal patterns before they shatter.

Variations

Easier: Just blow and watch, no counting, no experiments.

Harder: Test variables: blow in shade vs. sun, big vs. small wands.

Safety

Only works below freezing. Bundle up, and keep bubble solution off mittens you will put near your face.
