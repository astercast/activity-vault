Nature Weaving Looms

A stick loom and a nature walk — weaving with what the ground gives you.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
30–60 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Build a simple loom: tie four sticks into a square and string warp threads across it.

Materials

  • sticks for the loom frame
  • string or yarn
  • found nature items: long grass, leaves, feathers, bark

Steps

  1. Build the loom frame with sticks and string — warp threads about a finger apart.
  2. Head out on a collection walk: gather long grass, fallen leaves, feathers, bark strips.
  3. Weave finds over and under the warp threads, packing rows tight.
  4. Tie off the ends and trim the frame.
  5. Hang the finished weavings on a branch gallery.

Variations

Easier: Weave on a pre-made cardboard loom instead of sticks.

Harder: Pattern challenge: alternate textures in repeating bands.

⚠️ Safety

Collect only what has already fallen — no picking live plants, and watch for thorns.
