Continents Map Puzzle

Cut out the seven continents and fit them onto a blank world map.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Print a blank-ish world map (outlines only) and a sheet of labeled continent shapes per kid.

Materials

  • printed world map outlines (one per kid)
  • printed continent cutouts
  • scissors
  • glue sticks
  • crayons

Steps

  1. Cut out the seven continents. Mix them up.
  2. Fit each continent onto the map outline like a puzzle — no peeking at a finished map yet.
  3. Glue them down, then label each one and color it a different color.
  4. Partner quiz: cover the labels and name each continent by pointing.
  5. Bonus: mark where you live, and draw a line to somewhere you'd love to visit.

Variations

Easier: Use pre-cut continents — just match, glue, and label.

Harder: Add the oceans, then research one animal from each continent and draw it on the map.

⚠️ Safety

Scissors cut paper only.
