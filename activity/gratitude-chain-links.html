Gratitude Chain

One link per thank-you. Watch the class chain grow.

Before you begin

Ages
5–14
Group
4–60 kids
Time
5–10 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
No prep needed

Setup

Cut paper into strips ahead of time, or have kids tear them.

Materials

  • paper strips
  • markers or crayons
  • tape or glue

Steps

  1. Write one thank-you on a strip: a person, a thing, a moment.
  2. Loop your strip into a ring and tape it shut.
  3. Link your ring through a classmate's ring. The chain grows.
  4. Hang the finished chain where everyone can see it all week.

Variations

Easier: Draw the thank-you instead of writing it.

Harder: Every link must thank a different person. No repeats until everyone is named.

Safety

Tape tears, do not bite it, and watch fingers with the dispenser.
