Frost Observation

Magnify the ice crystals on windows and leaves and sketch their patterns.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

On a frosty morning, find windows, car hoods (with the owner's okay), or leaves with visible frost. Hand out magnifiers and sketch paper.

Materials

  • magnifying glasses (one per pair)
  • paper and pencils
  • a thermometer (optional)

Steps

  1. Look at the frost through the magnifier — find the six-pointed crystals.
  2. Sketch three different crystals you actually see, not what you imagine.
  3. Compare sketches: does everyone's frost look the same? Different?
  4. Discuss: what did the night have to be like for this frost to form?

Variations

Easier: One shared magnifier, teacher narrates; kids draw one big crystal together.

Harder: Check the overnight low online and connect it to how feathery the frost is.

⚠️ Safety

Don't lick frosty metal, and don't touch anyone's car without permission.
