Paper Bridge Challenge

Five sheets of paper, one gap, as many coins as it can hold.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–50 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Set two stacks of books about 8 inches apart as the 'river banks.' Hand out paper and a short strip of tape each.

Materials

  • plain paper (5 sheets per kid)
  • tape
  • coins or small blocks as weights

Steps

  1. The challenge: span the gap with paper and hold as many coins as possible.
  2. Give 15 minutes of building — folding, rolling, and corrugating are all legal.
  3. Test one bridge at a time: lay it across the banks and stack coins in the middle.
  4. Record each bridge's coin count on a class chart.
  5. Winner's debrief: what shape held the most — and why?

Variations

Easier: Allow unlimited tape and skip the coin test — longest span wins.

Harder: Two sheets only, and the bridge must hold a full cup of water.

⚠️ Safety

No running with scissors if you cut paper — and keep the testing area clear so bridges do not get stepped on.
