Story Dice

Roll the dice, get your ingredients, write the story.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Make simple story dice: label each face of a die with a character, setting, object, or problem — or print a story-dice sheet with 6 options per category.

Materials

  • dice (or printed story-dice sheets)
  • paper and pencils

Steps

  1. Each kid rolls three times: once for a character, once for a setting, once for a problem.
  2. They have 10 minutes to write or draw a story using all three elements.
  3. Volunteers read their stories aloud.
  4. Vote on awards: funniest, most surprising, best twist.
  5. Collect the stories into a group storybook.

Variations

Easier: Roll only two dice (character + setting) for younger writers.

Harder: Add a fourth die: the story must end with a plot twist.
