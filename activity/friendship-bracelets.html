Friendship Bracelets

Knot embroidery floss into bracelets to trade — the playground classic.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
20–60 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cut floss into arm-length strands ahead of time and bundle by color. Demo the two basic knots slowly on a big scale first.

Materials

  • embroidery floss in many colors
  • tape or safety pins to anchor
  • scissors

Steps

  1. Each kid picks 3–4 strands, knots them together at the top, and tapes the knot to the table.
  2. Teach the forward knot: make a 4-shape with the working strand, pull it through, tighten — twice per strand.
  3. Work down the row of strands, one knot at a time. The pattern emerges on its own.
  4. When the bracelet fits around the wrist, knot the end and trim.
  5. Trade bracelets with a friend — that is the whole point.

Variations

Easier: Do simple braided bracelets (three strands, plain braid) for kids who find knots frustrating.

Harder: Learn the chevron (V-shape) pattern or add beads to the design.
