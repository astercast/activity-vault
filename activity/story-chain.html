One-Word Story Chain

Build a story one sentence per person around the circle — plot twists mandatory, logic optional.

Ages
7–12
Group
5–25 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Sit in a circle; leader prepares an opening sentence.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Sit in a circle. The leader starts a story with one sentence: 'The old lighthouse had been dark for fifty years.'
  2. Going around the circle, each person adds exactly ONE sentence. No more, no passing.
  3. The story will go weird. That's the point. Plot twists are mandatory; logic is optional.
  4. After one full round, go around again — now each person can add one sentence OR end a subplot.
  5. Read the whole thing back at the end. Vote on the best plot twist and the funniest line.

Variations

Easier: Allow passing once per round.

Harder: Each sentence must start with the last word of the previous one.

⚠️ Safety

None.
