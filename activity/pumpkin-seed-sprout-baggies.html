Pumpkin Seed Sprouting Baggies

Seeds wake up on the classroom window — watch germination happen day by day.

Ages
5–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Dampen the paper towels (wring out the drips). Give each kid a bag, a towel, and 3–4 seeds.

Materials

  • pumpkin seeds (saved from a carved pumpkin or a seed packet)
  • paper towels
  • zip-top sandwich bags
  • water
  • tape

Steps

  1. Fold the damp paper towel and slide it into the bag with 3–4 seeds pressed against the towel so you can see them.
  2. Seal the bag and tape it to a sunny window — the bag is its own little greenhouse.
  3. Each day, kids observe and draw what they see: nothing, a crack, a root, a sprout.
  4. After a week, compare: which seeds sprouted first? Did seeds near the top of the bag beat the ones at the bottom?
  5. Transplant the best sprouts into cups of soil if you want a longer project.

Variations

Easier: Sprout just one seed per bag so it's easy to watch.

Harder: Run a side experiment: one bag in the dark, one in the light — compare the sprouts.
