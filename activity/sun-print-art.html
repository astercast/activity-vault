Sun Print Art

The sun does the painting — you just pick the shapes.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
30–60 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

You need a sunny spot and dark construction paper. Gather interesting flat objects for silhouettes.

Materials

  • dark-colored construction paper
  • flat objects: leaves, keys, toys, stencils
  • tape

Steps

  1. Lay dark construction paper flat in full sun.
  2. Arrange flat objects on the paper to make a design — leaves, keys, toy shapes.
  3. Tape corners down so nothing blows away, then leave it in the sun for 2–4 hours.
  4. Lift the objects to reveal the unfaded silhouettes underneath.
  5. Display the prints — they keep fading, so photograph favorites the same day.

Variations

Easier: One big class print on a single sheet.

Harder: Layer two rounds: move objects halfway through for double-exposure effects.

⚠️ Safety

Do this on a day with real sun. Remind kids not to stare at the sun while arranging their prints.
