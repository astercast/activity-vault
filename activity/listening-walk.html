Listening Walk

A walk where the only job is to listen — and write down everything you hear.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

A quiet route works best — a garden, a park path, a calm street. Explain the one rule: this walk is about ears, not eyes.

Materials

  • paper and pencils

Steps

  1. Walk slowly in pairs. For the first 5 minutes, nobody talks — just listen.
  2. Each person writes down every sound they hear: birds, wind, footsteps, distant cars, a dog barking.
  3. Compare lists. Who heard the most? Who heard the quietest sound?
  4. Walk back and listen for sounds you missed the first time.
  5. Share the most surprising sound and where it came from.

Variations

Easier: Give young kids a checklist of sounds to listen for (bird, car, wind, voices).

Harder: Sit still for 5 minutes with eyes closed, then map where each sound came from.

⚠️ Safety

Walk in pairs on a safe path. Stay aware of surroundings even while listening closely.
