Yarn Dream Catchers

Weave yarn webs on wire hoops with beaded hangers — and learn the real cultural story behind the craft.

Ages
8–12
Group
1–20 kids
Time
45–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pre-bend hoops; set out yarn, beads, and feathers.

Materials

  • chenille stems or craft wire
  • yarn
  • pony beads
  • feathers
  • scissors

Steps

  1. Bend a chenille stem or thin wire into a circle about 4 inches across and twist the ends together.
  2. Tie yarn to the circle and wrap it all the way around until the frame is fully covered.
  3. Tie a new strand and weave a web: loop around the frame, then loop each new stitch through the previous loop, spiraling inward.
  4. Leave a small hole in the center. Tie on 3 strands hanging down and thread beads and feathers onto them.
  5. Hang near a window. Share the Ojibwe origin story of dream catchers respectfully — this is a real cultural tradition, not just a craft.

Variations

Easier: Wrap the hoop and tie on pre-made hanging strands.

Harder: Weave a tight traditional web with an even center hole.

⚠️ Safety

Wire ends can be sharp — fold them flat or use chenille stems.
