Haunted Hallway Build

Teams transform one hallway into a silly-spooky walkthrough - designed by kids.

Ages
6–14
Group
8–40 kids
Time
40–60 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Get permission for the hallway first. Assign each team one 'scare station' along the route, with a clear entrance and exit.

Materials

  • black paper or tablecloths
  • tape
  • flashlights or string lights
  • cardboard boxes
  • craft supplies

Steps

  1. Teams blueprint their station: silly scare, secret passage, marked exit - nothing actually scary.
  2. Build it: black-paper walls, cardboard tunnels, flashlight spotlights.
  3. Safety walk: every builder walks the whole route checking for trip hazards and dead ends.
  4. Open the hallway: other classes tour in small groups with a guide.
  5. Deconstruct together - everything sorted for reuse or recycling.

Variations

Easier: One station per team in the classroom instead of a full hallway.

Harder: Add a storyteller guide who narrates the tour with a script.

⚠️ Safety

Nothing blocks fire exits or doors - ever. Tape, never nails. An adult checks every station before tours open.

🤝 Inclusion notes

Every station has a lights-on version; tour guides ask each group's scare comfort level first.
