Sound Effects Story

Groups become living sound effects — thunder, rain, wind — performing a story read aloud by the leader.

Ages
5–10
Group
3–20 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Pick a story; assign sound groups.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Pick a simple story with lots of action — a walk through a jungle, a stormy night, a rocket launch.
  2. Assign sound effects to small groups: one group is thunder (stomping), one is rain (finger snapping), one is wind (whooshing), one is animals.
  3. The leader reads the story slowly. When their cue comes up, each group makes its sound.
  4. Run it once for practice, then perform it straight through — record it if you can.
  5. Let kids write their own 5-line sound story for the next session.

Variations

Easier: Use just 2 sound groups.

Harder: Kids write and perform their own 5-line sound story.

⚠️ Safety

Indoor voices for sound effects — no screaming.
