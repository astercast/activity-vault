Chalk Maze

Draw a giant maze on the pavement, then race through it.

Ages
6–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
25–50 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Find a big paved area. Teams design maze sections that connect into one mega-maze.

Materials

  • sidewalk chalk (lots of it)

Steps

  1. Split into teams. Each team draws a maze section on the pavement — paths, dead ends, and one entrance and exit.
  2. Connect the sections into one giant maze across the whole area.
  3. Teams swap: navigate another team's section without crossing lines.
  4. Time the runs. Fastest navigator wins — then everyone free-draws in the leftover space.
  5. Photo the maze from above before the next rain takes it.

Variations

Easier: Draw one simple spiral maze together as a whole group.

Harder: Add challenge stations inside the maze: 10 hops, a riddle to solve, a balance beam line.

⚠️ Safety

Use a paved area away from traffic and parking. Chalk washes off — skip painted walls.
