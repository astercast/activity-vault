Ujima Unity Mural

One huge mural, every kid holding one section. Ujima means collective work, so the art works collectively.

Before you begin

Ages
5–12
Group
6–40 kids
Time
30–50 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Tape the big paper to a wall or floor. Lightly grid it into one section per kid, nobody owns the middle alone.

Materials

  • one very large paper or taped-together sheets
  • markers, crayons, paint sticks
  • painter's tape

Steps

  1. Explain ujima: collective work and responsibility. The mural only works if every section works.
  2. Each kid claims one grid square and sketches their piece first in pencil: something their family or block does together.
  3. Color it in boldly. The rule: your colors must touch your neighbor's colors at the border, no white gaps.
  4. Walk the whole mural together. Each artist explains their square in one sentence.
  5. Title it as a class and hang it where everyone passes. The hallway is the gallery.

Variations

Easier: Pair up so two kids share each square, or do a smaller 4-square group mural.

Harder: Add a border of handprints, or make it a before/after: mural of the block as it is vs as you want it.
