Simon Says

Only move when Simon says — the listening game that never gets old.

Ages
5–10
Group
6–40 kids
Time
10–20 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

One leader faces the group. Everyone else spreads out so they can move.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. The leader gives commands: 'Simon says touch your toes' — everyone does it.
  2. Without 'Simon says,' the command is a trap: 'Jump!' — anyone who jumps is out.
  3. Speed up as the group shrinks. Slow, tricky commands catch more players.
  4. Last player standing becomes the next Simon.
  5. Silly Simon round: all commands are dance moves.

Variations

Easier: Give one warning before a player is out.

Harder: Chain commands: 'Simon says touch your head AND stomp your foot.'
