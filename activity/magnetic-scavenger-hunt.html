Magnetic Scavenger Hunt

Hunt the room with a magnet and discover what metal really means.

Ages
5–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Lay out a table of mixed objects, half magnetic and half not. Hand out one magnet per kid or pair.

Materials

  • magnets (one per kid or pair)
  • trays or bowls for sorting
  • a table of assorted objects: keys, paper clips, coins, rubber bands, foil, plastic toys

Steps

  1. Let kids freely test objects with their magnets — sort finds into magnetic and not-magnetic trays.
  2. Ask the group to spot the pattern: what do the magnetic ones have in common?
  3. Round two: hide ten magnetic objects around the room and hunt with magnets.
  4. Debrief: magnets attract iron, nickel, and cobalt — not all metal.
  5. Bonus challenge: can a magnet pull a paper clip through paper? Through a hand?

Variations

Easier: Test only the objects on the table — skip the hiding round.

Harder: Predict first, test second: write guesses before touching the magnet.

⚠️ Safety

Keep magnets away from phones and tablets, and warn kids not to swallow small magnets — ever.
