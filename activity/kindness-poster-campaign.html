Kindness Poster Campaign

Pairs design posters with one clear kindness message — then the hallways become the gallery.

Ages
7–12
Group
4–40 kids
Time
30–50 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pair kids up. Brainstorm what kindness actually looks like at school (including someone, using kind words, helping without being asked).

Materials

  • large paper or poster board (one per pair)
  • markers, crayons, or paint
  • tape for hanging

Steps

  1. Each pair picks ONE clear message — short enough to read from across the hall.
  2. Design the poster: big readable letters, one strong image, the message front and center.
  3. Present the poster to the class in 30 seconds: what does it say, and who is it for?
  4. Hang the posters in hallways with permission — the campaign is the point.
  5. A week later, walk the hall and notice: which posters do people stop to read?

Variations

Easier: Make small desk posters instead of hallway-sized ones.

Harder: Turn the best posters into a printed kindness calendar for the school office.
