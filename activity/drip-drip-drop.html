Drip Drip Drop

Duck-duck-goose with a wet sponge — the 'goose' gets a squeeze overhead.

Ages
4–10
Group
6–30 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Fill the bucket, soak the sponge, and wring it once so it drips but doesn't pour. Kids sit in a circle on the grass.

Materials

  • one big soft sponge
  • a bucket of water

Steps

  1. One kid is it and walks the circle with the dripping sponge, tapping heads: 'drip, drip, drip…'
  2. On 'DROP!' they squeeze the sponge over that kid's head and run.
  3. The dropped-on kid chases them around the circle for the empty spot.
  4. Caught? The chaser stays it. Made it around? The chaser sits and the runner picks the next it.
  5. Re-soak the sponge every few rounds — a dry sponge is just a head tap.

Variations

Easier: The teacher is always it; kids only run the chase.

Harder: Two sponges, two runners — twice the sprints, half the waiting.

⚠️ Safety

Squeeze gently, never hard. Barefoot-friendly grass only — no running on wet concrete.
