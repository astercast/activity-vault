Snowflake Mirror

Pairs mirror each other's snowflake arm poses — then try to catch the change.

Ages
4–9
Group
4–40 kids
Time
10–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

No setup at all. Pair the kids up, facing each other, arms' length apart.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. One partner is the 'snowflake', the other is the 'mirror'.
  2. The snowflake slowly moves arms and body into a new pose; the mirror copies in real time.
  3. After a minute, swap roles.
  4. Round two: the mirror has to spot three deliberate changes the snowflake sneaks in.
  5. Close: each pair shows their best snowflake pose to the group.

Variations

Easier: Whole group mirrors the teacher instead of pairing up.

Harder: Three-person teams: one caller shouts pose words ('branch!', 'crystal!').
