Paper Chain Countdown

One link per day. A decoration that doubles as the countdown.

Before you begin

Ages
4–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
No prep needed

Setup

Cut construction paper into 1-inch by 8-inch strips (about 20 per kid). Set out one stapler or tape roll per table.

Materials

  • colored construction paper cut into strips
  • stapler or tape (one per table)

Steps

  1. Give each kid a stack of strips and explain the countdown: one link per day until the holiday.
  2. Have them write one thing they are looking forward to on each strip before linking it.
  3. Thread each new strip through the last loop and staple or tape the ends together.
  4. Loop strips together into one long chain per table.
  5. Hang the chains across the room. Tear one link off each day as the holiday gets closer.

Variations

Easier: Use fewer links and write single words instead of sentences.

Harder: Pattern challenge: links must follow a color pattern nobody repeats.

Safety

Staplers stay at the table and get passed hand-to-hand, no stapling games.
