Balloon Rocket Race

A balloon, a straw, and a string — the fastest physics demo in the room.

Ages
7–12
Group
4–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Thread string through a straw and stretch it tight across the room, taping both ends. Build one lane per team.

Materials

  • balloons (one per team, plus spares)
  • long string or yarn
  • drinking straws
  • tape

Steps

  1. Tape a straw to the top of an uninflated balloon — the straw rides the string.
  2. Inflate the balloon (do not tie it), pinch the end, and clip it to the lane's start.
  3. Countdown and release. The escaping air drives the rocket down the line.
  4. First rocket to the far end wins the heat. Run a bracket.
  5. Engineering round: redesign with two balloons, angled straws, or lighter tape.

Variations

Easier: One lane, one rocket at a time — just watch it go.

Harder: Cargo challenge: the rocket must carry a paper-clip passenger the whole lane.

⚠️ Safety

Balloons can pop — keep them away from faces while inflating, and pick up popped pieces right away.
