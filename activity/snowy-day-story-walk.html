Snowy Day Story Walk

A story in stations: walk the pages, act the weather, end warm inside.

Ages
4–8
Group
4–24 kids
Time
20–30 min
Where
Outdoors & Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pick a winter story and mark 5-6 stopping points. Number cards mark each station along a short loop.

Materials

  • a winter picture book
  • page-number cards
  • a warm drink or snack for the end (optional)

Steps

  1. Gather at station 1: read the first pages aloud, right there in the cold.
  2. Walk to the next station the way the character walks — trudging, slipping, crunching.
  3. At each stop, read the next pages, then everyone acts out the weather in that scene.
  4. The last station is back inside: finish the story warm, with the optional warm drink.
  5. Retell round: can the group retell the whole story from the station numbers alone?

Variations

Easier: Do three stations instead of six, or read the whole book at one outdoor spot.

Harder: Kids illustrate one station each afterward, or write the 'next chapter' the book doesn't have.

⚠️ Safety

Cold-weather basics: this runs 20 minutes max outside, then inside. Watch for red cheeks and cold fingers.
