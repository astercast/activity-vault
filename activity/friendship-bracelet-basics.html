Friendship Bracelet Basics

The camp classic: knotted embroidery floss bracelets, learned in one sitting.

Ages 7–12 · 25–45 min · 4 materials

Ages
7–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–45 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cut floss into arm-length strands (3 per bracelet). Knot the tops together and pin them to pillows, one workstation per kid.

Materials

  • embroidery floss in many colors
  • safety pins or tape
  • scissors
  • pillows or clipboards to pin to

Steps

  1. Pick 3 colors and knot them together at the top.
  2. Learn the only knot that matters: the forward knot. Make a '4' with the working thread, pull the tail through, slide it up. Twice per knot.
  3. Work left to right, one color at a time, until the bracelet fits around a wrist.
  4. Tie the end in a knot, braid the tails, and gift it. Bracelets are for giving.
  5. Trade-up round: everyone makes one for the person on their left.

Variations

Easier: Two colors, chunky yarn instead of floss, and the teacher ties the starting knot.

Harder: Pattern challenge: stripes, chevrons, then the diamond, photograph each stage for the camp board.

⚠️ Safety

Safety pins pin to pillows or denim, never to skin. Scissors cut away from fingers.
