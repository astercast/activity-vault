👋 Icebreakers

Human Bingo

Find a different classmate for every square.

Ages
7–12
Group
6–40 kids
Time
20–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Make bingo grids with prompts like 'has a pet lizard,' 'can whistle,' 'was born in another state.' Print or hand-draw one per kid.

Materials

  • human bingo sheets (printed, or hand-drawn grids)
  • pencils

Steps

  1. Hand out sheets and explain: find a different person for each square.
  2. Mingle and interview: ask, listen, get a signature per matching square.
  3. First to complete a row calls BINGO — verify by asking the signers.
  4. Keep going until five kids have bingo, then stop.
  5. Share-out: the most surprising signature on your sheet.

Variations

Easier: Three in a row wins instead of five.

Harder: Blackout round: fill the whole card.

⚠️ Safety

Roam, don't run — and only ask someone to sign if they agree to the square.
