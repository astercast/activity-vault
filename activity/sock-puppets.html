Sock Puppet Workshop

Turn stray socks into a cast of characters, then put on a full puppet show behind a table stage.

Ages
4–10
Group
1–20 kids
Time
40–40 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Set out one sock per child plus shared bins of eyes, yarn, and felt.

Materials

  • clean socks (one per child)
  • googly eyes
  • yarn
  • felt scraps
  • fabric glue
  • pom-poms
  • markers

Steps

  1. Each child picks a sock and decides their character: hero, monster, animal, grandma.
  2. Glue on googly eyes, yarn hair, and a mouth made from a folded felt oval or a drawn-on smile.
  3. Add personality: ears from felt triangles, a pom-pom nose, a cape from a fabric scrap.
  4. Let glue dry 15 minutes while kids name their puppets and invent one catchphrase each.
  5. Puppet show time: set up a table 'stage,' hide behind it, and perform 2-minute scenes in pairs. Applaud everything.

Variations

Easier: Draw faces with markers instead of gluing pieces.

Harder: Write and perform a 3-scene puppet play with a beginning, middle, and end.

⚠️ Safety

Fabric glue only for little kids — hot glue is adult-only.
