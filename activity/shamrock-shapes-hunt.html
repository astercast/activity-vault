Shamrock Shapes Hunt

Green shamrocks are hiding. Each one carries a shape. Find them, sort them, count the haul.

Before you begin

Ages
3–7
Group
2–25 kids
Time
15–25 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cut the shamrocks and draw one clear shape on each, circles, triangles, squares, hearts. Hide them at knee height or lower, in sight once you are close: this is a hunt for 3-to-7-year-olds, not a search warrant.

Materials

  • paper shamrocks (teacher-cut, about 20)
  • a marker
  • a basket or bag per team

Steps

  1. Show one shamrock and its shape. The job: find a shamrock, name its shape out loud, carry it back. Naming it is what counts it.
  2. Hunt in pairs or small teams, one basket per team. Walk, don't run. The shapes are hiding, not fleeing.
  3. Back at the rug, sort the haul into shape piles together.
  4. Count each pile and line the piles up: which shape hid best? The smallest pile wins the hiding prize.
  5. Re-hide round, if energy allows. This time the kids hide them for another team, same knee-height rule.

Variations

Easier: Hunt for green only. Find any shamrock, shapes come later.

Harder: Add shape clues instead of free hunting ('find a triangle hiding near something soft'), or graph the counts on the board.

Safety

Set the hunt boundary out loud before anyone moves, and keep it where one adult can see the whole field.
