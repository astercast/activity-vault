Paper Plate Masks

Cut, paint, and decorate paper plates into wearable animal and character masks — then parade them in silly voices.

Ages
4–10
Group
1–20 kids
Time
35–35 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Pre-cut plate shapes for younger kids; set out decorating supplies.

Materials

  • paper plates
  • scissors
  • paint or markers
  • glue
  • yarn
  • feathers
  • pom-poms
  • elastic cord

Steps

  1. Hold a paper plate up to your face and mark where your eyes are, then cut out two eye holes.
  2. Cut the plate into the mask's basic shape: round it for animals, point the top for cats, add ears.
  3. Paint or color the base, then glue on extras: yarn hair, feather ears, a pom-pom nose.
  4. Poke a small hole on each side and thread elastic through for wearing.
  5. Mask parade: everyone introduces their character in a silly voice before the masks come off.

Variations

Easier: Decorate pre-cut masks instead of cutting.

Harder: Design a mask for an original character with a backstory to perform.

⚠️ Safety

Cut eye holes generously; check each mask for visibility before wearing.
