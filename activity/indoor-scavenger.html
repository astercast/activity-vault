🌧️ Rainy Day

Indoor Color Scavenger Hunt

Hunt the room for red things, soft things, noisy things — point, don't touch, and beat the 10-minute clock.

Ages
4–10
Group
2–20 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Print scavenger lists.

Materials

  • printed scavenger lists
  • pencils
  • timer

Steps

  1. Give each child or team a list: find something red, something soft, something that makes noise, something with letters, something smaller than your thumb.
  2. Set a 10-minute timer. Everything must stay where it is — point and check off, don't collect.
  3. For older kids, make it riddles instead of descriptions: 'I have hands but no arms' (a clock).
  4. Everyone returns and shares their weirdest find.
  5. Lightning round: the leader calls out new items one at a time — first to point at one wins the round.

Variations

Easier: Use picture lists for non-readers.

Harder: Riddle clues where each answer is the next item.

⚠️ Safety

Walk, don't run; everything stays where it is.
