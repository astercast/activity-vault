Rock Painting

Smooth stones become ladybugs, monsters, and messages — then hide them around the neighborhood.

Ages
5–12
Group
2–30 kids
Time
25–60 min
Where
Outdoors & Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Wash and dry the rocks ahead of time. Cover tables. Put out paint pens — they are far less messy than brushes and cups.

Materials

  • smooth flat rocks (collected or bought in bulk)
  • acrylic paint pens or washable paint
  • cups of water and paper towels
  • newspaper to protect tables

Steps

  1. Each kid picks 2–3 rocks and plans a design: an animal, a pattern, a kind word.
  2. Paint the design. Paint pens make details easy; let layers dry before adding on top.
  3. While paint dries, kids write where they plan to hide their rock.
  4. Optional: hide finished rocks around the school or park for strangers to find.
  5. Keep one rock each for a group display shelf.

Variations

Easier: Stick to simple dot patterns or single-color designs for younger painters.

Harder: Paint a set of story stones (a character, a place, an object) and use them to tell stories.

⚠️ Safety

Acrylic paint stains clothes — smocks or old shirts recommended.
