Teddy Says My Name

Every kid holds a soft toy, and the toy does the introducing: its name, your name, and one thing you love. Shy voices welcome — teddies are brave.

Ages
3–6
Group
4–20 kids
Time
10–20 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Gather the toys in a basket in the middle before anyone sits: a mix of bears, dolls and whatever soft things the program owns, enough for a real choice — choosing your toy is the first minute of this activity and it is not wasted time. Sit the circle first, toys second, so the choosing does not happen over laps.

Materials

  • one soft toy or doll per kid (the program's, or their own from home)

Steps

  1. Each kid picks a toy from the basket and holds it in their lap. Model the whole thing first with your own toy, held up where the circle can see it: 'This is Bruno. Bruno says: my friend's name is Sam, and Sam loves puddles.' Short, plain, a bit silly — your turn sets the size of theirs.
  2. State the one rule: 'The toy does the talking.' The kid holds the toy up; the toy 'says' the kid's name and one thing they love — in the kid's own voice, a whisper to you that you relay in the toy's voice, or a point at the thing if it is in the room. All three are the toy talking.
  3. Go around the circle with no skipping-pressure: a kid who passes gets a 'teddy will wait' and the circle comes back to them once at the end, by which time they have heard ten examples and the toy has usually found its voice.
  4. Favourites echo: after each introduction, the whole circle says the love back to the toy — 'Puddles! Sam loves puddles!' — so every kid hears their thing said gladly by the whole room, once.
  5. Finish with the toys introduced to each other: two neighbouring teddies 'shake hands' (paws touched together by their holders) and the holders say each other's names across the handshake. Toys go back to the basket by name — 'Back you go, Bruno' — which the group will insist on doing properly.

Variations

Easier: Names only, no favourite thing — the toy says the name, the circle echoes the name, done.

Harder: Add the toy's own favourite: each toy also 'says' what IT loves (honey, naps, being squeezed), invented entirely by its holder.

⚠️ Safety

Toys are held, never thrown — a thrown teddy sits with the adult until its turn, kindly. Program toys go back in the basket at the end; a kid's own toy from home stays in their own hands throughout if that is what they need, and introducing from inside a hug counts in full.
