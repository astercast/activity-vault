Card Tower Challenge

One deck of cards, no tape, tallest tower wins.

Ages
7–12
Group
2–25 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Clear table space. Each team gets one deck. No tape, no glue — cards only.

Materials

  • decks of playing cards (one per team)

Steps

  1. Challenge: build the tallest free-standing card tower in 15 minutes.
  2. Show the basic technique once: lean two cards together into an A-frame, then bridge across the top.
  3. Let teams build. Collapses are part of the game — rebuild fast.
  4. Measure standing towers at the end of time. Tallest wins.
  5. Debrief: what worked — wider bases, lighter touches, patience.

Variations

Easier: Allow a flat table edge or book as a windbreak and base.

Harder: The tower must survive a gentle table tap to qualify.
