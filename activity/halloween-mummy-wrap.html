Mummy Wrap Relay

Teams race to wrap a volunteer head-to-toe in toilet paper — the neatest mummy wins, then everyone parades.

Ages
6–12
Group
8–30 kids
Time
20–20 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Clear floor space; hand each team two toilet paper rolls.

Materials

  • toilet paper rolls (2 per team)
  • timer

Steps

  1. Split into teams of 4–6. Each team picks one volunteer to be the mummy.
  2. On 'go,' teams have 3 minutes to wrap their mummy using only the toilet paper — arms, legs, torso, everything but the face.
  3. When time's up, mummies line up for judging: best coverage, most creative, funniest walk.
  4. Mummies do a runway walk while the group cheers, then everyone helps unwrap (great cleanup built in).
  5. Bonus round: mummy relay — wrapped mummies race across the room without tearing the paper.

Variations

Easier: Wrap just arms and torso, skip legs.

Harder: Mummies must complete an obstacle course without tearing the wrap.

⚠️ Safety

Never cover faces. Stop if anyone feels dizzy or uncomfortable.
