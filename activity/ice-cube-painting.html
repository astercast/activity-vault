Ice Cube Painting

Frozen paint cubes that melt into watercolor-style art — perfect for hot days.

Ages
5–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
20–40 min
Where
Indoors & Outdoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

The day before: mix a little washable paint into water in each ice tray slot, add a popsicle stick handle, and freeze. A summer-cooldown classic.

Materials

  • ice cube trays
  • washable paint or food coloring
  • popsicle sticks
  • thick paper
  • smocks or old shirts

Steps

  1. Hand out the frozen paint cubes — they melt fast, so work quickly.
  2. Drag the melting cubes across thick paper. Colors blend where they meet.
  3. Try layering: let one color dry slightly, then paint over it.
  4. While paintings dry, talk about it: the melting IS the paintbrush.
  5. Hang the finished art — the soft blended edges look like watercolors.

Variations

Easier: Use food coloring instead of paint for zero-stain cleanup.

Harder: Paint on black paper — the colors pop dramatically.

⚠️ Safety

Washable paint only. Paints stain — smocks on, and protect tables.
