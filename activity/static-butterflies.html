Static Butterflies

Static electricity makes tissue butterflies dance.

Ages
5–10
Group
2–30 kids
Time
15–30 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
Some prep needed

Setup

Cut butterfly shapes from tissue paper ahead of time — or let older kids cut their own. Lay them on a table.

Materials

  • balloons (one per kid)
  • tissue paper cut into butterfly shapes
  • a wool cloth or sweater for charging

Steps

  1. Rub a balloon on a wool cloth for 10 seconds to charge it with static.
  2. Hold the charged balloon just above a tissue butterfly — it lifts and dances without touching.
  3. Challenge: keep the butterfly floating for 10 seconds without it landing.
  4. Test what kills the magic: does a wet butterfly still fly? A heavy one?
  5. End with a butterfly ballet: everyone floats theirs at once to music.

Variations

Easier: One big group demo with a single balloon and butterfly.

Harder: Race: guide your butterfly across the table using only the balloon.

⚠️ Safety

Keep balloons away from faces; rub the balloon on cloth, not on hair, to keep it simple.
