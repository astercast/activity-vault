Helping Hands Pledge

Name one way you will help the class this week — then do it.

Ages
5–14
Group
4–60 kids
Time
5–8 min
Where
Indoors
Prep
✅ No prep needed

Setup

Sit in a circle. Teacher starts with their own pledge.

Materials

  • No materials needed.

Steps

  1. Each person names one specific, doable way they will help the class this week.
  2. Keep it small and real: 'I will stack the chairs,' 'I will help Sam with spelling.'
  3. Go around fast — one sentence each, no discussion.
  4. Teacher writes the pledges on the board. Revisit at week's end: who kept theirs?

Variations

Easier: Pledge as table groups instead of individuals.

Harder: Pledges must help a specific person, named out loud.

⚠️ Safety

Pledges are promises to the group — keep them kind and keepable.
